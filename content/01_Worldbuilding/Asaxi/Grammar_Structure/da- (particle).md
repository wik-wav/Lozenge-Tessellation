# da- ([[39_Numerals & Mathematics]])

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Number Prefix
- **Function:** **Hundreds**
- **Logic:** Multiplies the attached digit by 100.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /da/

### Example sentence

**dasă** (100). **datam** (200).
