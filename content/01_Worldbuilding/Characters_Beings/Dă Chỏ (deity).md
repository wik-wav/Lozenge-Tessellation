# Dă Chỏ - Deity of food and desire

Dă Chỏ is a deity heeded by the inhabitants of the [[The Lozenge Tessellation|Lozenge T.]] He is often depicted as a giant mammoth. 

The songs and chants created for the worship of Dă Chỏ usually serve the purpose of: 
- channelling desire
- clenching desire
- reckoning with desire
- relieving anxiety (of lust, hunger, uncertain futures)

A literal translation of Dă Chỏ is Elephant-Maw
