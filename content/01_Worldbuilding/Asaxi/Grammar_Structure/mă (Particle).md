# mă ([[02_Particles in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">mă</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Frequency Suffix
- **Meaning:** "Every", "Per", "Each" (in a temporal sense).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /maɪ/

### Translations

- English: every, per, each
- Polish: co (np. co godzinę), każdy (w sensie powtarzalności)

### Usage & Forms

Attaches to Time Units or Numbers.

- **ximă** (Every day / Daily).
- **åmă** (Every time).
- **tammă** (Every other day / Alternating days).
- **gămă** (Whenever / At any time).

### Grammatical Distinction: mă vs. să

It is important to distinguish frequency from total quantity.

- **mă** (Frequency): Used for **repetition**.
    - *Ximă:* Every day (Day after day).
- **[[să (Number)|să]]** (Totality): Used for **the whole subset**.
    - *Să xi:* All days (The entirety of the days).

### Example sentence

Wo ximă shěsonů.
I read every day.

### Alternative forms

**-mă** (Suffix form).

### Etymology

Derived from Japanese **毎** (*mai* - every).

x
