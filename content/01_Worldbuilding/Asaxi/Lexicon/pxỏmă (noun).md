# pxỏmă ([[01_Asaxi Nouns (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">pxỏmă</span>

### Phonetic Profile

**IPA:** /pɦou̯maɪ/

### Lexical Classification

**Category:** Noun
**Noun Class:** Warm (Animate/Energy-exerting)

### Meanings & Translations

**English:** the sum of one's toil, the total effort exerted, cumulative exertion, life's work (in terms of energy spent)
**Polish:** suma wykonanego wysiłku, ogół trudu, dorobek energetyczny, "wszystkie oddechy"

### Definition

A collective noun representing the sum total of physical or spiritual exertion a person has expended. Based on the concept that breathing (`pxỏpxỏ`) is the fundamental unit of effort and life-force exertion, `pxỏmă` ("every breath") metaphorically quantifies the cost of living or achieving. It is classified as **Warm** because it represents active energy and the vitality required to struggle or build.

### Etymology

A compound derived from the noun **[[pxỏpxỏ (verb)|pxỏpxỏ (verb)]]** (breathing; reduced to **pxỏ** in compounding) + the quantifier **[[mă (Particle)|mă (Particle)]]** (every, all).
