# bădå ([[15_NPCP and The Agglutinative Block|Relational Particle Compound]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">bădå</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Relational Particle Compound
- **Function:** **Causal Purpose**
- **Meaning:** "Because of and for the sake of", "Driven by and aiming for", "Due to and for".

### Usage Notes

Used when the motivation for an action is both the **Origin/Cause** (bă) and the **Goal/Beneficiary** (då). It implies a complete cycle of motivation.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /baɪdau̯/

### Example sentence

**Wa bădå dăfrë paxăhètáka.**
_We will be forced to wage war for and because of Great Freedom._ (The cause compels the action).

### Etymology
**[[bă (Relational Particle)|bă]]** (Instrumental/Cause) + **[[då (Relational Particle)|då]]** (Benefactive/Purpose).
