### Grammatical function
- **Type:** Mood Particle (Sentence Final)
- **Function:** **Positive Potentiality**
- **Meaning:** "Can", "Able to", "Possible".

### Pronunciation
IPA: /ken/

### Example sentence
**To John haśù ken.** _John can run._

### Etymology
Borrowed from Singlish **"Can"**.

### Antonyms
- **[[ken.ná (particle)]]** (Cannot).
