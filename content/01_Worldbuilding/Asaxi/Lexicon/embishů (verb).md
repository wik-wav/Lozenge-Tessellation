# embishů ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_ů (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">embishů</span>

### Transitivity / Valency

**Transitive**
Takes an object (the opponent).

### Lexical Aspect

**Achievement:** The moment of defeat.

### Grammatical Note

Derived from **[[embi (noun)|embi]]** (Plane/Surface) + **[[06A_-sh- (Morphological Infix)|-sh-]]** (Privative Mode) + **-ů**.
* *Lit:* "To plane-strip" / "To remove the surface."

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Motion]] / Conflict

### Pronunciation

IPA: /em.biʃuw/

### Translations

- English: to defeat, to beat, to overthrow, to pull the rug out from under
- Polish: pokonać, zwyciężyć

### Usage Note (Visual Metaphor)

Describes defeating someone by removing their footing or foundation (like opening a trapdoor).

### Example sentence

**To wo no embishů.**
_I defeat you._ (I remove your plane).
