# ỏ- ([[07_Describing Location in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">ỏ-</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Relational Locative Prefix
- **Function 1:** **Topological State** (Central)
    - *Meaning:* In the middle of, center, between.
- **Function 2:** **Compound Comparative Prefix** (Equality)
    - *Meaning:* "Equal to", "As [Noun] as".
    - *Usage:* Attaches to the Reference Object in a simile compound.
    - *Example:* **ỏjhecèŕănabi** ("Jet-speed" / As fast as a jet).

### Syntax

Attaches to the front of the noun.
* **Locative:** `ỏ-[Noun]` (Middle of Noun).
* **Comparative:** `ỏ-[Noun]-[AdjectiveRoot]` (Noun-like Quality).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ou̯/

### Example sentence

**To John ỏshosa xiŕa.**
_John is in the middle of the road._

**To wo ỏjýnoŕănabinýj.**
_I am sonic-speed-fast (as fast as sound)._

### Etymology

Sound symbolism for "Middle/Center."
