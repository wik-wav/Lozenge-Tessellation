# ănå ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">ănå</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">ănå</span>
### Transitivity / Valency
**Transitive** (Waiting for X) or **Intransitive** (Waiting).

### Lexical Aspect
- **State** — an ongoing condition, not an event.
- **Aspect markers**: **na-** "do repeatedly" · **tå-** "do once" · **ni-** "begin to" · **chå-** "do fully".

### Grammatical Note
**[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** This functions as a primitive root verb.

### Semantic Field
[[Smntc_Field Time]] / [[Smntc_Field Emotion]] (Patience)

### Pronunciation
IPA: /aɪnau̯/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">ă · nå</span>
- **Pattern:** `H · L`
- **Class:** `lexical`

### Translations
- English: to wait, to bide time, to anticipate, to expect
- Polish: czekać, oczekiwać

### Example sentence

#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** to wo no ănå.
- **English:** I wait for you.
- **Polish:** x

#### Example 2

- **Asaxi:** vozè, to wo zèxănå.
- **English:** I was waiting a moment ago.
- **Polish:** x

### Alternative Forms

### Etymology
**[[ă (particle)|ă]]** (Subjective/Internal Topic) + **[[nå (particle)]]** (Now/Time).
*Lit:* "Subjective-Now" or "The Main Topic is Time."
