# vină ([[03_Asaxi Adjectives (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">vină</span>

### Grammatical function
- **Type:** Qualitative Adjective (`-nă`)
- **Source Agreement:** **Warm** (Root `vi` is Warm).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /vinaɪ/

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Emotion]] / Value

### Translations

- English: okay, alright, fine, decent, good enough
- Polish: w porządku, okej, dobry

### Usage Note

Less formal or "virtuous" than **[[gavină (adjective)]]** (Good). It implies acceptability or a satisfactory state.

### Example sentence

**To wo vină ů.**
_I am doing okay._

**To onă o-shá vină xiŕa.**
_This person is alright._

### Etymology

**[[vi (Particle)|vi]]** (OK) + **-nă**.

### Synonyms

- **gavină** (Good).
