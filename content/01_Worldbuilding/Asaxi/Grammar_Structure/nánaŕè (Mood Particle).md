### Grammatical function
- **Type:** Mood Particle (Sentence Final)
- **Function:** **Negative Obligative Mood**
- **Meaning:** "Should not", "Not supposed to", "Improper to".

### Pronunciation
IPA: /nɑnaɾə/

### Example sentence
**To no haśù nánaŕè.**
_You shouldn't run._
