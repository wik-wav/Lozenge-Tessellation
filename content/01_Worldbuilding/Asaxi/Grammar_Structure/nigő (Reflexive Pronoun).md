# nigő ([[13_Pronouns in Asaxi]])

- - -

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Reflexive Pronoun
- **Function:** **Self-Reference** (3rd Person Singular Non-Binary)
- **Meaning:** "Themself", "Themselves" (Singular).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɲiɡoɪ/

### Example sentence

**To gő nigő ijo.** _They see themself._

### Etymology

**[[ni (Relational Particle)|ni]]** (Towards) + **[[gő (Pronoun)|gő]]** (They-sg).
