# memm ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">memm</span>

### Transitivity / Valency

**Ambitransitive**
To close / To be closed.

### Lexical Aspect

**Achievement:** Instantaneous shutting.

### Grammatical Note

**[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** Root Verb.

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Motion]] / Action

### Pronunciation

IPA: /me.m̩/
*Note:* Two syllables. The final `mm` functions as a syllabic nasal nucleus.

### Translations

- English: to close, to shut, to seal
- Polish: zamykać

### Example sentence

**Kem zèmemm.**
_The door closed._

### Etymology

Sound symbolism: The bilabial `m` closes the mouth, mimicking the shutting action.
