# dăxă ([[33_Desiderative Subjunctive Moods]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">dăxă</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">dăxă</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Desiderative Particle (Sentence Final)
- **Function:** **Optative Marker** (Positive Wish)
- **Meaning:** "Hopefully", "I wish", "Desirably".

### Pronunciation

IPA: /daɪɦaɪ/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">dă · xă</span>
- **Pattern:** `L · L`
- **Class:** `atonal`

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** to wo gajýnnshá niŕa dăxă.
- **English:** I wish I were a were-hyena.
- **Polish:** x

### Etymology

**[[dăshá (noun)|dă]]** (Elephant) + **[[xa- (relational locative) (particle)|xa/xă]]** (Above/High). _Lit:_ "High Elephant." (Cultural association of the Elephant with height, greatness, and desire).

x

### Antonyms

- **pùxă** (I hope not).
