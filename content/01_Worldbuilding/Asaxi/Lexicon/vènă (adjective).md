### Etymology
The **aura/simile** colour of [[vètxe (noun)|větxe]] (leaf): leaf + **-nă**. A non-`ga` colour word — where the `ga-` form names the *objective* hue, the `-nă` simile names its **mood / aura** (cf. [[ga (particle)|ga]] vs the simile rule in [[16_Adjectives_Constitution vs Simile]]).
