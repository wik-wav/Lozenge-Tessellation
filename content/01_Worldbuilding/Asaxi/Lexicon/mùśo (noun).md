# mùśo ([[01_Asaxi Nouns (List)]])

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">mùśo</span>
### Noun class (warm / cold)
**Cold**
- Reasoning: Damp fungal growth — and, like mushrooms, fungi are reckoned **cold**, set apart from the warm life of plants.

### Pronunciation
IPA: /mɯɕo/

### Semantic Field
[[Smntc_Field Environment]]

### Translations
- English: mould, lichen, mildew
- Polish: pleśń

### Example sentence
**To mùśo vèno nanů.**
_Mould is on the wood._

### Alternative Forms
Null

### Etymology
A primitive root for dank fungal growth; the cold central vowel **ù** evokes damp and rot. Conceptually opposed to the warm growth-roots [[vivi (noun) - as in life|vivi]] (life/grass) and [[vètxe (noun)|větxe]] (leaf).
