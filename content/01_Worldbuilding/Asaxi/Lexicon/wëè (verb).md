### Transitivity / Valency
_monotransitive verb_ — takes a requirer and the thing required (e.g. The work(1) requires effort(2).)

### Lexical Aspect
- **State** — an ongoing condition, not an event.
- **Aspect markers**: **na-** "do repeatedly" · **tå-** "do once" · **ni-** "begin to" · **chå-** "do fully".

### Grammatical Note
- **[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** A primitive (root) verb; it may take **-ů** to derive a durative reading, and `na-`/`tå-`/`ni-`/`chå-` for other aspects.
