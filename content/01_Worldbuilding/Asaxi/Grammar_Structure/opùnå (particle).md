# opùnå ([[18_Conjunctions & Clause Linking]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">opùnå</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">opùnå</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Temporal Particle / Adverb
- **Function:** **Habitual / Usual Aspect**
- **Meaning:** "Usually", "Normally", "Typically", "As a rule".

### Logic & Etymology

**[[pù- (relational locative) (particle)|pù]]** (Below) + **[[onå (particle)|onå]]** (Always/Eternal).

- _Lit:_ "Below Always."
- _Logic:_ Defines the frequency as being close to 100% (Always), but subtly reduced or subordinate (Below). This establishes the standard or average frequency of an action.

### Syntax

Placed at the **end** of the sentence (Event Scope) or before an adjective (Attribute Scope).

- _Structure:_ `[Action] opùnå.`

### Pronunciation

IPA: /opɯnau̯/

### Example sentence

**To wo shěsonů opùnå.** _I usually read._

### Antonyms

- **[[gănå (particle)]]** (Sometime / Exceptionally).
- **[[nåsi (particle)|nåsi]]** (Never).
