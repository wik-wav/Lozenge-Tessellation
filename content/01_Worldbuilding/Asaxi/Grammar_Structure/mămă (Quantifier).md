# mămă ([[02_Particles in Asaxi]])

- - -

### Pronunciation

**IPA:** /maɪmaɪ/

### Lexical Classification

**Category:** Particle
**Sub-category:** Quantifier (Totalizing)

### Meanings & Translations

**English:** all, every, every single one, the complete set
**Polish:** wszystko, każdy, każdy jeden, cały zbiór

### Definition

An intensive quantifier used to denote every individual item within a specific subset or frame of reference without exception. It functions as the semantic antonym to **fůmă** (none) and provides more emphasis on the individual components of a group than the base particle **mă**.

### Etymology

A reduplicative form of the quantifier **mă** (every), used to signal totality and exhaustive inclusion.

### Examples

**Mămă apo gapo xiŕa.**
 _Every apple is red._
