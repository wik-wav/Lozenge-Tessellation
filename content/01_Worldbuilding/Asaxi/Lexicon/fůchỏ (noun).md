# fůchỏ ([[01_Asaxi Nouns (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">fůchỏ</span>

### Noun class (warm / cold)

**Warm**
- Reasoning: Biological state of the body (digestive/emotional).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /fuu̯t̠ʃou̯/

### Semantic field

[[Smntc_Field Food & Drink]] / [[Smntc_Field Emotion]]

### Translations

- English: satiation, fullness, satisfaction, un-hunger
- Polish: sytość, nasycenie

### Usage Note

Unlike **[[fůjå (noun)|fůjå]]** (Wantlessness - a spiritual virtue), **fůchỏ** is a physical or emotional state of having been fed. It is the result of consuming.

### Example sentence

**To wo fůchỏ ůchů.**
_I feel satiated._

### Etymology

**[[fů (root word)|fů]]** (Lack/Un-) + **[[chỏ (noun)|chỏ]]** (Hunger/Maw).
*Lit:* "Un-hunger."

### Antonyms

- **[[chỏ (noun)|chỏ]]** (Hunger).
