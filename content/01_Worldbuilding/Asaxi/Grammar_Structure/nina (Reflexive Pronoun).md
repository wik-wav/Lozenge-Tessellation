# nina ([[13_Pronouns in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">nina</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">nina</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Reflexive Pronoun
- **Function:** **Self-Reference** (2nd Person Plural)
- **Meaning:** "Yourselves".

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɲina/

### Example sentence

**To na nina ijo.** _You (all) see yourselves._

### Etymology

**[[ni (Relational Particle)|ni]]** (Towards) + **[[na (Pronoun)|na]]** (You-all).
