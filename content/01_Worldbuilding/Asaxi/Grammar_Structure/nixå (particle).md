# nixå ([[15_NPCP and The Agglutinative Block|Relational Particle Compound]])

### Grammatical function

- **Particle type:** [[15_NPCP and The Agglutinative Block|Compound Relational Particle]]
- **Function:** **Durative** / **Perfect Progressive** Marker
- **Meaning:** "For the duration of", "Into the time of".

### Logic & Etymology

A fusion of **[[ni (particle)|ni]]** (Into/Allative) + **-x-** (Bridge) + **[[å (noun)|å]]** (Time).

- _Logic:_ "Entering into the time of [X]." It marks an action that spans a specific duration, connecting the past to the present.

### Syntax

Occupies a **Temporal Argument** slot, typically appearing before the verb.

- _Efficiency Rule:_ Since `nixå` contains the root "Time" (`å`), you do not need to repeat a generic unit noun (like "hours") if using a number.
- _Structure:_ `nixå [Number/Duration]`

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɲiɦau̯/

#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** wo nixå 3 shěsonů.
- **English:** I have been reading for 3 (hours/units).
