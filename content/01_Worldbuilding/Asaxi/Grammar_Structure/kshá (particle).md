# kshá ([[25_Interrogative Pronouns (Wh-Words)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">kshá</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">kshá</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Interrogative Pronoun (Animate)
- **Meaning:** Who?

### Pronunciation

IPA: /kʃɑ/

### Etymology

Derived from the question prefix **k-** + **[[shá (noun)|shá]]** (Person). _Lit:_ "Question-Person?"

### Example sentence

**Kshá shěsonů?** _Who reads?_ (Note: The particle `kè` is dropped for efficiency).
