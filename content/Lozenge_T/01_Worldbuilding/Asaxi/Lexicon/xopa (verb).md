# xopa ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">xopa</span>

### Transitivity / Valency

**Ambitransitive**
* **Intransitive:** To advance, to move forward.
* **Transitive:** To push forward, to promote, to advance X.

### Lexical Aspect

**Activity:** Forward motion.

### Grammatical Note

**[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** Compound root verb.

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Motion]] / Progress

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɦopa/

### Translations

- English: to advance, to proceed, to move forward, to propel
- Polish: naprzód, postępować, popychać

### Example sentence

**Nipa, to wa xopa.**
_Into the future, we advance._

### Etymology

**xo** (Move) + **[[pa- (Relational Locative Prefix)|pa]]** (Front).

### Antonyms

- **xohù** (To retreat).
