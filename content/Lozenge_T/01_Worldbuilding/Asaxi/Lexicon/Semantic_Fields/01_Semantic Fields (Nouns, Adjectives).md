## A1-A2 (beginner)

- **[[Smntc_Field Communication]]**
- [[Smntc_Field Colours]]
- [[Smntc_Field Animals]] (Fauna):
    - pets, 
    - farm animals, 
    - wild animals (often grouped by biome), 
    - insects/arachnids, 
    - marine life.
- [[Smntc_Field Geography (Topography)]]: 
	- landforms (mountain, valley, island), 
	- bodies of water (river, lake, ocean),
	- cardinal directions.
- [[Smntc_Field Environment]]
	- elements of the environment (sun, tree, cloud, rock)
- [[Smntc_Field Food & Drink]]
	- fruit,
	- vegetables,
	- meats/proteins,
	- dairy,
	- grains,
	- cutlery/crockery.
- [[Smntc_Field Weather & Climate]]
	- precipitation types (rain, snow, hail), 
	- temperature descriptors, 
	- sky conditions (cloudy, clear).
- [[Smntc_Field The Body]]
	- _external:_ Head, limbs, sensory organs.
	- _internal:_ Organs (heart, lungs), 
	- skeletal system.
- [[Smntc_Field Emotion]]:
    - _States:_ Happy, sad, angry, anxious.
    - _Traits:_ Generous, stingy, brave, cowardly.
- [[Smntc_Field Time]]:
	- day night cycle, clock, hours, minutes
- Smntc_Field Materials: [Important materials](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Material) — wood, metal, plastic, fabric, glass, ceramic.
- [[Smntc_Field Tools]]
	- Construction & Manual Labor: Hammer, Saw, Drill, Pickaxe, Wrench, Pliers, Level
	- Gardening & Agriculture: Shovel, Rake, Hoe, Axe, Scythe, Spade
	- General Utility & Household: Scissors, Tape Measure, Flashlight, Ladder, Knife, Pen

## A2-B1 (elementary)

- [[Smntc_Field Shapes & Dimensions]]: 
	- 2D shapes (circle, square), 
	- 3D forms (sphere, cube), 
	- lines (curved, straight).
- Smntc_Field Temporal Units
	- days of the week, 
	- months of the year, 
	- seasons, 
	- units of time (second, minute, decade).
- [[Smntc_Field Numerals]]
	- cardinal (one, two), 
	- ordinal (first, second), 
	- fractions,
	- multiples.
	- Iteratives (frequency)
	- Collective Numerals
- [[Smntc_Field Circumstance]]
- Smntc_Field Clothing (Garments)
	- footwear, 
	- headgear, 
	- outerwear, (clothing designed to be worn as protective layers outside other garments)
	- underwear, 
	- accessories (jewelry, belts)
- [[Smntc_Field Housing & Shelter]]
	- _Rooms:_ Kitchen, bathroom, attic.
	- _Furniture:_ Seating, storage, surfaces, bedding.
	- _Appliances:_ White goods, electronics.
- [[Smntc_Field The City]] (Urban Infrastructure):
	- public buildings (post office, library),
	- transport hubs (station, airport),
	- road infrastructure (sidewalk, intersection).
- Transport: 
	- land (car, bus, train), 
	- water (boat, ferry), 
	- air (plane, helicopter).
