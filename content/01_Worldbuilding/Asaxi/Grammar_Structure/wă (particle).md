# wă ([[02_Particles in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">wă</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Mood Particle (Sentence Final)
- **Function:** **Volitional Mood**
- **Meaning:** "Want", "Desire".

### Pronunciation

IPA: /waɪ/

### Usage Note

Distinct from the prefix **jå-**.

- **jå-** (Prefix): Fuses with the verb ("I wannaread"). Action-focused.
- **wă** (Particle): Modifies the clause ("I have a desire to read"). Feeling-focused.

### Example sentence

**To wo shěsonů wă.** _I want to read._

### Etymology

Derived from **[[wa (Pronoun)|wa]]** (We) / **[[wo (Pronoun)|wo]]** (I). _Logic:_ Represents the "Will of the Self."
