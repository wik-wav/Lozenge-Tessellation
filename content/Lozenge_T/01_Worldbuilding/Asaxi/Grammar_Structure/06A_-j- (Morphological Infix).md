# -j- ([[06_Verbs in Asaxi]])

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Morphological Infix (Bridge)
- **Function:** **Visual Mode**
- **Meaning:** To look like, to appear as.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /Ʝ/

### Example sentence

**To fwoo gajýnnjů.** _The cloud looks like a were-hyena._

### Etymology

Derived from **[[ijo (verb)|ijo]]** (To see).
