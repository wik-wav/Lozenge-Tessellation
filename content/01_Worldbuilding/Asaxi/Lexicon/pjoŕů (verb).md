# pjoŕů ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_ů (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">pjoŕů</span>

### Transitivity / Valency

**Intransitive**

### Lexical Aspect

**State / Activity:** Emanating light.

### Grammatical Note

Derived from **[[pjo (noun)|pjo]]** (Light) + **[[06A_-ŕ- (Morphological Infix)|-ŕ-]]** (Generative Mode) + **-ů**.
* *Logic:* "To generate/produce light."

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Environment]]

### Pronunciation

IPA: /pʲoɾuu̯/

### Translations

- English: to shine, to beam, to emanate light, to radiate
- Polish: świecić, jaśnieć, promieniować

### Usage Note

Distinct from **[[iŕoŕů (verb)|iŕoŕů]]** (To glow/shine a colour).
* **iŕoŕů:** Focuses on the *hue/quality* of the light (e.g., "It glows red").
* **pjoŕů:** Focuses on the *intensity/physics* of the light (e.g., "It shines brightly").

### Example sentence

**To Xi pjoŕů.**
_The sun shines._

### Synonyms

- **iŕoŕů** (To glow with color).
