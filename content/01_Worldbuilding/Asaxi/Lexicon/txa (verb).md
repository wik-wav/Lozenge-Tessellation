### Transitivity / Valency

### Lexical Aspect
- **Durative (Activity)** — unfolds over time.
- **Aspect markers**: **na-** "do repeatedly" · **tå-** "do once" · **ni-** "begin to" · **chå-** "do fully".

### Grammatical Note
- **[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** Root verb. **Use only for a genuine change of state / morphing — NOT as a catch-all 'alter / affect / stir' verb.** Pick the precise verb instead (e.g. [[xo (verb)|xo]] move, [[kiŕonů (verb)|kiŕonů]] cut, [[tănů (verb)|tănů]] add).
