### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Mood Particle (Sentence Final)
- **Function:** **Negative Necessitative**
- **Meaning:** "Don't need to", "Don't have to", "Not necessary".

### Pronunciation

IPA: /nou̯weɪ/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">nỏ · wë</span>
- **Pattern:** `L · L`
- **Class:** `atonal`

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** to no xoxo nỏwë.
- **English:** You don't have to leave.
