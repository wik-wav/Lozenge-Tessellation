### Grammatical function
Bound **suffix**. Adds the meaning *"built construct / made surface"* to a base. Unlike **-kam** (an *enterable* structure, ← [[kamm (noun)|kamm]]), **-kŕo** forms structures one does **not** go inside — floors, fences, and the like.
