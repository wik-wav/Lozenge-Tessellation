## **Definition & Logic**

In Asaxi, particles are uninflected function words that serve as the grammatical "glue" . They do not carry physical meaning (like nouns) or action (like verbs) but instead define the **Meta-Data** of the sentence: Is it true? Is it a command? Is it finished? Is it polite?

Particles are strictly categorized by **Binding Strength**, which dictates their position relative to the word they modify .

---

## **I. High Binding Particles (Morphological)**

These particles are "glued" tightly to the verb or predicate . They act as Prefixes, Suffixes, or Infixes. They alter the fundamental reality or structure of the action itself.

### **1. Polarity & Validity (Truth)**

These determine if the statement is factually true, false, or emphatic.

- **Positioning Rule (Flexibility & Stacking):**
    - **Present Tense (Flexible):** In the unmarked present tense, polarity particles can appear as either a **Suffix** or a **Prefix**. Both forms are valid.
        - _Suffix:_ `shěsonů` + `ná` → `shěsonůná` ("Reads-not") .
        - _Prefix:_ `ná` + `shěsonů` → `náshěsonů` ("Not-reads").
    - **Marked Tenses (Past/Future):** When a Tense Prefix is present, the polarity particle typically migrates to the **front** (as an infix between the Tense and the Root) to maintain the prefix block structure .
        - _Structure:_ `[Tense]` + `[Polarity]` + `[Root]` .
        - _Ex:_ `zè` + `ná` + `shěsonů` → `zènáshěsonů` ("Did-not-read") .
- **The Particles:**
    - **[[xă (particle)|xă]] (Positive / Emphasis):** "Yes", "Indeed", "Truly" . Used to assert reality against doubt.
        - _Free Shifting:_ Can appear as `shěsonůxă` (Suffix) or `xăshěsonů` (Prefix) .   
    - **[[ná (particle)|ná]] (Negative):** "Not" .
        - _Variant:_ **[[nỏ (particle)|nỏ]]** is used exclusively before particles starting with /w/ (e.g., `nỏwă`) .
    - **[[xiŕa (particle)|xiŕa]] (Stative):** "Is" / "Exists" (Fact) .
    - **[[nèŕa (particle)|nèŕa]] (Negative Stative):** "Is not" (Fusion of `ná` + `xiŕa`) .
    - **[[pxů (particle)|pxů]]:** "No" / "Refusal" (Standalone Interjection) .

### **2. Mood & Agency (Voice)**

These define _who_ controls the action (Agency) and the _force_ behind it (Command/Request). They attach to the verb stem.

- **The Imperative Spectrum ([[hè (particle)|hè]]):**
    - **Suffix ([[-hè (particle)|-hè]]):** Standard Command ("Do it") .
    - **Prefix ([[hè- (particle)|hè-]]):** Emphatic Command ("DO it!") .
    - **Fused Imperative ([[-wë (particle)|-wë]]):** When `-hè` attaches to a verb ending in `-ů`, they fuse (e.g., `shěsonwë`) .    
- **Complex Agency (Prefixes):**
    - **[[xăhè- (particle)|xăhè-]] (Coercive):** "Force to", "Make do", "Must" .
    - **[[băhè- (particle)|băhè-]] (Permissive):** "Allow to", "Let", "Enable" .
    - **[[náxăbăhè- (particle)|náxăbăhè-]] (Prohibitive Voice):** "Ban", "Forbid", "Prohibit" .    
    - **[[náxăhè (particle)|náxăhè]] (Strict Prohibition):** "MUST NOT" (Sentence Final) .    
- **The Solicitative Spectrum ([[kă (particle)|kă]]):**
    - **Suffix ([[-kă (particle)|-kă]]):** Request ("Please") .
    - **Prefix ([[kă- (particle)|kă-]]):** Polite Voice ("Kindly do") .
    - **[[xăkă (particle)|xăkă]] (Instruction):** "You are requested to" (Formal/Recipe) .
    - **[[náxăkă (particle)|náxăkă]] (Polite Prohibition):** "Please do not" .

### **3. Aspect (Texture of Time)**

These define the "shape" of the action: is it starting, finishing, continuing, or switching?

- **Position:** Typically attach to the **front** of the verb root (inside the Tense prefix) .
- **The Particles:**
    - **[[chå (particle)|chå]] (Completive):** "Completely", "Fully", "Finish" (e.g., `zèchåxoxo` "Departed for good") .
    - **[[ni (particle)|ni]] (Inceptive):** "Start", "Begin" .
    - **[[na (particle)|na]] (Iterative):** "Re-", "Again" .
    - **[[nihè (particle)|nihè]] (Inceptive Command):** "Start!", "Begin!" .
    - **[[nă (particle)|nă]] (Cessation):** "Stop!" (Interrupts current action) .
    - **[[sů (particle)|sů]] (Continuation):** "Keep going!", "Continue!" .
    - **[[pỏni (particle)|pỏni]] (Prospective):** "Soon-to-be", "Would-be" (Adjectival) .
- **Switching Actions:**
    - **[[nåhè- (particle)|nåhè-]] (Imperative Switch):** "Now do!", "Switch to!" (e.g., `nåhèhaśù` "Run instead!") .
    - **[[panå- (particle)|panå-]] (Declarative Switch):** "Will now", "Going to (instead)" .

## **II. Low Binding Particles (Pragmatic/Syntactic)**

These operate on the **Utterance** level. They attach to the very end of a clause or sentence (Post-Predicate Tail) or act as independent connectors . They do not fuse inside the verb.

### **1. Nominal Coordinators (Linking Words)**

Connects nouns within a single argument slot .

- **[[ja (particle)|ja]]:** "And" (List) .
- **[[se (particle)|se]]:** "Or" (Noun choice) .
- **[[jhaná (particle)|jhaná]]:** "But not" (Exclusion) .

### **2. Clause Connectors (Linking Sentences)**

Appears at the end of the first clause to link it to the next .

- **Logical:**
    - **[[dzè (particle)|dzè]]:** "But", "However" .
    - **[[si (particle)|si]]:** "Or" (Alternative action) .
    - **[[ŕa (particle)|ŕa]]:** "And" (Coordination) .
    - **[[sèni (particle)|sèni]]:** "So", "Therefore" .
    - **[[sèwo (particle)|sèwo]]:** "Because" .

- **Temporal/Conditional:**
    - **[[vå (particle)|vå]]:** "When" .
    - **[[zå (particle)|zå]]:** "Then", "Next" .
    - **[[nivå (particle)|nivå]]:** "While", "During" .
    - **[[måniåkam (particle)|måniåkam]]:** "By the time that" .
    - **[[chě (particle)|chě]]:** "If" .
    - **[[chěxa (particle)|chěxa]]:** "Even if", "Although" .
    - **[[chěná (particle)|chěná]]:** "Unless" .

### **3. Temporal & Frequency (Adverbial Particles)**

These define the time state relative to "Now" (`nå`) or the frequency of the event .

- **State Markers (Sentence Final):**
    - **[[nå (particle)|nå]]:** "Now" / "Currently" (or "Already" with Past Tense) .
    - **[[panå (particle)|panå]]:** "Not yet" .
    - **[[hùnå (particle)|hùnå]]:** "Already" .
    - **[[vanå (particle)|vanå]]:** "Still" .

- **Frequency (Floating/Pre-Verbal):**
    - **[[onå (particle)|onå]]:** "Always", "Forever" .
    - **[[nanå (particle)|nanå]]:** "Often" .
    - **[[nåsi (particle)|nåsi]]:** "Never" .
    - **[[opùnå (particle)|opùnå]]:** "Usually" .
    - **[[gămă (particle)|gămă]]:** "Whenever" .
    - **[[ximă (particle)|ximă]]:** "Daily" .
    - **[[izånixå (particle)|izånixå]]:** "From time to time" .
    - **[[náxănåsi (particle)|náxănåsi]]:** "Absolutely never" (Emphatic) .
    - **[[găxăni (particle)|găxăni]]:** "For eternity" .

### **4. Grammatical Mood (Speaker Attitude)**

Placed at the end of the sentence to show the speaker's internal state regarding the fact.

- **Volition & Necessity:**
    - **[[wă (particle)|wă]]:** "Want to" .
    - **[[wë (particle)|wë]]:** "Need to" .
    - **[[nỏwă (particle)|nỏwă]]:** "Don't want to" .
    - **[[nỏwë (particle)|nỏwë]]:** "Don't need to" .
- **Obligation (Propriety):**
    - **[[naŕè (particle)|naŕè]]:** "Should", "Ought to" .
    - **[[nánaŕè (particle)|nánaŕè]]:** "Should not" .
- **Potentiality:**
    - **[[ken (particle)|ken]]:** "Can", "Able to"
    - **[[ken.ná (particle)|ken.ná]]:** "Cannot" .
- **Subjunctive (Hypothetical/Wish):**
    - **[[xăxă (particle)|xăxă]]:** "Would", "As if" (Neutral/Scheming) .
    - **[[dăxă (particle)|dăxă]]:** "Hopefully", "I wish" (Positive) .
    - **[[pùxă (particle)|pùxă]]:** "I hope not", "Lest" (Negative) .
    - **[[xădăchỏxă (particle)|xădăchỏxă]]:** "Please God" (Desperate Prayer) .

### **5. Interrogative (Question)**
- **[[kè (particle)|kè]]:** The verbal question mark .
- **[[kkè (particle)|kkè]]:** Emphatic/Agitated question ("?!") .

### **6. Quotative & Topic**

- **[[tte (particle)|tte]]:** "That", "It is said", "Speaking of". Binds the preceding phrase as a quote, topic, or hearsay .

### **7. Discourse Markers (Social Tone)**

The final element of the sentence. Negotiates the social relationship .

- **[[ë (particle)|ë]] / [[në (particle)|në]]:** "Right?", "Agreed?" (Soliciting) .
- **[[e (particle)|e]] / [[me (particle)|me]]:** "Is that so?", "Really?" (Skepticism) .
- **[[ő (discourse) (particle)|ő]] / [[wő (particle)|wő]]:** "I tell you", "It is known" (Assertion) .
- **[[aŕa (particle)|aŕa]]:** "Alas", "It is what it is" (Resignation) .
- **[[iŕè (particle)|iŕè]]:** "I object!", "No way" (Contention) .
- **[[jỏ (particle)|jo]]:** "Yo", "Hey" (Casual/Street) .
- **Pre-Clausal (Sentence Starters):**
    - **[[wå (particle)|wå]]:** "Woah!" (Awe) .
    - **[[ox (particle)|ox]]:** "Oh..." (Emotion/Sigh) .
