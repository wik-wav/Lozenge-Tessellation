# náxăhè ([[02_Particles in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">náxăhè</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">náxăhè</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Particle type:** [[02_Particles in Asaxi|High Binding Particle Compound]] (Suffix)
- **Function:** **Strict Prohibitive Mood** (Absolute Ban)
- **Meaning:** "Must not", "It is forbidden to", "NO \[Verbing\]".

### Usage Notes

Used for laws, safety warnings, strict rules, or angry commands. It implies that the action is not just unwanted, but impermissible or illegal.

- **Structure:** `[Verb]-náxăhè`
- **Contrast:**
    - `náhè`: "Don't." (Standard negative command).
    - `náxăkă`: "Please do not." (Polite prohibition).
    - `náxăhè`: "MUST NOT." (Strict ban).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /nɑɦaɪxə/

### Etymology

A fusion of **[[ná (particle)|ná]]** (Not) + **[[xă (particle)|xă]]** (Indeed/Force) + **[[hè (particle)|hè]]** (Command). _Lit:_ "Emphatically-Not-Command."
