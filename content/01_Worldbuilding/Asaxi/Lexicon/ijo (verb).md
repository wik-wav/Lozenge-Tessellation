# ijo ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_ů (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">ijo</span>

### Transitivity / Valency 

monotransitive verb

### Argument Structure

- **Arg 1 (Subject):** Experiencer (The entity perceiving/watching).
- **Arg 2 (Object):** Stimulus (The entity being perceived/watched).

### Grammatical Note

- **[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** This belongs to the closed class of primitive verbs. It is a root verb and does not utilize the Universal Verbalizer _-ů_ suffix.

### Lexical Aspect

- **Activity / State:** Depending on context.
    
    - **As Activity:** Volitional, continuous observation (watching).
    - **As State:** Passive, continuous sensory input (seeing).

### Semantic Field

- **[[Smntc_Field Perception]]** (Mental/Sensory)


### Pronunciation

IPA: /iꞲo/

### Translations

- **English:** see, watch
- **Polish:** widzieć, oglądać

### Alternative forms

- in speech or songs: jo
