# pa- ([[07_Describing Location in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">pa-</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">pa-</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Relational Locative Prefix
- **Function:** Topological State (Anterior)
- **Meaning:** In front of, before.

### Syntax

Attaches to the front of the noun.

- **Compound:** `pa` + `o` → **pawo** (Front-here).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /pa/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">pa</span>
- **Pattern:** `L`
- **Class:** `atonal`

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** to john pakamm xiŕa.
- **English:** John is in front of the building.
- **Polish:** x

### Derived terms

- **panů** - To be in front of.
- **paŕa** - To be in front (Stative).
- **pa-** (Future Tense: "Time in front").
