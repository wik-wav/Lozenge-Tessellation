### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Degree / Comparative Prefix
- **Function 1:** **Comparative Degree** ("More").
    - *Usage:* `na-[Adjective]`. (e.g., `nadănă` "Bigger").
- **Function 2:** **Compound Comparative Prefix** ("More than [Noun]").
    - *Usage:* `na-[Noun]-[AdjectiveRoot]`.
    - *Example:* **najhecèŕănabi** ("Faster than a jet").
- **Meaning:** On, on top of, touching the surface of.

### Syntax

Attaches to the front of the noun.

- **Compound:** `na` + `o` → **nawo** (On-here).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /na/

### Example sentence

**John navivi aśù.**
_John walks on the grass._

**To wo najhecèŕănabinýj.**
_I am faster than a jet._

### Derived terms

- **nanů** - To be on / To cover.
- **naŕa** - To be on (Stative).
- **na-** (Iterative Aspect: "To stack action").
