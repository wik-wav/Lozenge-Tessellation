# gămă ([[02_Particles in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">gămă</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">gămă</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Particle type:** Frequency Particle / Adverb
- **Function:** **Indefinite Universal Frequency**
- **Meaning:** "Whenever", "At any time", "Every time that...".

### Logic & Etymology

**[[gă (particle)|gă]]** (Somewhere/Indefinite) + **[[mă (particle)|-mă]]** (Every).
* *Lit:* "Every-somewhere" or "Every-indefinite-instance."
* *Logic:* Since `gă` represents indefinite existence (concepts that are "somewhere" but not fixed), combining it with "Every" creates the concept of "Any/Every time."

### Syntax

Placed at the **end** of the clause (Head-Final) or used as a response.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɡaɪmaɪ/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">gă · mă</span>
- **Pattern:** `L · L`
- **Class:** `atonal`

### Example sentence

#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** to wo gămă shěsonů.
- **English:** I read whenever (at any time).
- **Polish:** x

#### Example 2

- **Asaxi:** gămă toponů, wo shěsonů.
- **English:** Whenever it rains, I read.
- **Polish:** x
