### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Temporal Connector
- **Function:** **Sequential**
- **Meaning:** "Then", "Next", "After that".

### Syntax

Placed at the **end** of the first event.

- **Structure:** `[Event A] zå, [Event B].`

### Etymology

Derived from **[[izo (particle)|izo]]** (From). _Logic:_ "From (that event) forward."

### Pronunciation
