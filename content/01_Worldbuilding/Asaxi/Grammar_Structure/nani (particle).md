# nani ([[29_Degree & Comparison]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">nani</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">nani</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Comparative Coordinator
- **Function:** **Superiority** ("More X than...")
- **Meaning:** Indicates that the first noun possesses a higher degree of the quality than the second.

### Syntax

Must be paired with the complement **[[izo (particle)|izo]]** (From/Than).

- **Structure:** `nani [Noun A] izo [Noun B]...`

### Pronunciation

IPA: /naɲi/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">nani</span>
- **Pattern:** `L`
- **Class:** `atonal`

### Etymology

**[[na- (relational locative) (particle)|na]]** (Top) + **[[ni (particle)|ni]]** (Towards). _Lit:_ "Towards-Top."

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** nani john izo tom apa dănă chỏnů.
- **English:** John munches on apples more heavily than Tom.
