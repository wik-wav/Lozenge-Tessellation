# tonåjů ([[40_Subjective Epistemic Markers (The To-Predicates)|Subjective Epistemic Marker]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">tonåjů</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">tonåjů</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Epistemic Marker / Sentence Final Compound
- **Function:** Subjective Prediction / Foresight
- **Meaning:** "I predict that...", "I foresee that...", "I visualize that..."
    
    - _Usage:_ Marks the preceding clause as a future event or outcome that the speaker actively visualizes or predicts. Unlike the Future Tense (`pa-`), which simply states the future, `tonåjů` emphasizes the speaker's act of foresight.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /t̪onau̯juu̯/

### Semantic field

[[Smntc_Field Perception]]

### Translations

- **English:** I predict, I foresee, I envision
- **Polish:** przewiduję, że; widzę, że

### Example sentence

**Xő pahaśù tonåjů.** _I predict he will run._ (Lit: He will-run, [I]-time-see-[that]).

**Topo patoponů tonåjů.** _I predict it will raining (right now, somewhere else)._ / _I foresee rain._

### Alternative Forms

- **nåjů** (Dropped Subject Form).

### Etymology

**[[15_NPCP and The Agglutinative Block|to]]** (Subject Marker) + **[[18_Conjunctions & Clause Linking|nå]]** (Time/Now - functioning as "Timeline") + **[[ůjů (verb)|-jů]]** (Visual Suffix). _Logic:_ "The statement is time-visualized." The speaker "sees" the event on the timeline.

### Synonyms

- **toxăhè** (I am certain - stronger conviction).
- **paxiŕa** (It will be - objective future fact).

### Antonyms

- **tohùfwo** (I doubt / I cannot see clearly).
