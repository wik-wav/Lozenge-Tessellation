### Transitivity / Valency
**Transitive** (Spatial State)
Takes the location/container as the object.

### Lexical Aspect
- **Durative (Activity)** — unfolds over time.
- **Aspect markers**: **na-** "do repeatedly" · **tå-** "do once" · **ni-** "begin to" · **chå-** "do fully".

### Semantic Field
[[Smntc_Field The Body]]

### Pronunciation
IPA: /ou̯nuu̯/

### Translations
- English: to be in the middle of, to center, to bisect
- Polish: być w środku, pośrodku

### Example sentence
**To shěso tobo ỏnů.**
_The book is in the middle of the table._

**Toonýj shěso no-gă ỏnů.** 
_The book is around there._
