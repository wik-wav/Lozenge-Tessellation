# jůŕë ([[01_Asaxi Nouns (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">jůŕë</span>

### Noun class (warm / cold)

**Cold**
- Reasoning: Refers to the void/cosmos (Environment).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /juu̯ɾeɪ/

### Semantic field

[[Smntc_Field Geography (Topography)]] / Cosmology

### Translations
- English: outer space, the cosmos, the final frontier
- Polish: kosmos, przestrzeń kosmiczna

### Usage Note

While **[[xăsiŕo (noun)|xăsiŕo]]** refers to the "Great Unknown/Void" (abstract/philosophical), **jůŕë** refers to Space as a **navigable destination** or a place humans have reached.

### Example sentence

**To jhecè jůŕë ni pănů.**
_The jet exits into space._

### Alternative forms

x

### Etymology

Derived from the name **Yuri** (Gagarin), the first human to enter space.
