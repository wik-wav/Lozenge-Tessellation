### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Mood Particle (Sentence Final)
- **Function:** **Negative Volitional**
- **Meaning:** "Don't want to", "Dislike to".

### Pronunciation

IPA: /nou̯waɪ/

### Example sentence

**To wo xoxo nỏwă.** _I don't want to leave._
