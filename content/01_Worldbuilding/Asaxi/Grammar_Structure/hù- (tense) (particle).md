### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">hù</span>
- **Pattern:** `L`
- **Class:** `atonal`

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Tense Modifier Prefix
- **Function:** **Relative Past** ("Behind")
- **Meaning:** Places the action temporally "behind" the primary tense.

### Syntax

- **Pluperfect:** `hùzè-[Verb]` (Past of the Past / "Had done").
- **Past Future:** `hùpa-[Verb]` (Future relative to Past / "Was going to").

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** shěsokam hùzèchěcho'.
- **English:** The library had closed.
