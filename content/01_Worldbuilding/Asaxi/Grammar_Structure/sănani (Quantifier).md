# sănani ([[01_Asaxi Nouns (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">sănani</span>

### Noun class (warm / cold)

**Cold**
- Reasoning: Abstract quantity.

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Number Noun (Quantifier)
- **Meaning:** "Some", "Several", "A few (positive)", "More than one".

### Usage Notes

Functions as a **Floating Quantifier**.
* **Nuance:** implies plurality (at least two), but not a large amount. Synonymous with **fůtăgo**.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /saɪnaɲi/

### Example sentence

**To sănani sháma xogă.**
_Several (some) people arrive._

### Etymology

**[[să (Number)|să]]** (One) + **[[nani (Comparative Particle)|nani]]** (More/Upward).
*Lit:* "More than one."

### Synonyms

- **[[fůtăgo (Quantifier)]]** (Some / Unknown positive amount).
