### Semantic field

### Translations

- English: cushion, pillow, soft pad

### Example sentence

**To wo ỏbỏ nanů.**
_I am on the cushion._

### Etymology

Related to **[[ỏbwo (noun)|ỏbwo]]** (Belly/Softness) but adapted for inanimate objects (dropping the medial glide /w/).
*Lit:* "Middle-softness" or "Belly-object."
