# izo ([[15_NPCP and The Agglutinative Block|Relational Particle]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">izo</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">izo</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Particle type:** [[15_NPCP and The Agglutinative Block|Relational Particle (NPCP)]]
- **Function:** **Ablative** Case Marker
- **Meaning:** Indicates origin, source, or separation ("From", "Out of", "Since").

### Pronunciation

IPA: /izo/

### Example sentences

#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** izo kamm måmåni kjèpo aśù.
- **English:** Standard origin (source): I walk from the building as far as the tree.
- **Polish:** x

#### Example 2

- **Asaxi:** onýj o-shěso izowo-shěsokam xiŕa.
- **English:** Complex locative fusion (origin and location): The book is from the library here. The speaker has physically pointed first at the specific book, then at the library building.
- **Polish:** x

### Alternative Forms

- **izowo-** (When fused with a locative prefix starting with a vowel, utilizing the `-w-` bridge).

### Antonyms

- **[[ni (particle)|ni]]** (To / Allative)

### Derived terms

- **izoŕa** (Predicative Fusion): The result of fusing `izo` + `xiŕa`. Means "To be from" or "To originate from".
    
- **xăizoŕa** (Emphatic Predicative): The stacking of `xă` (Indeed) + `izo` + `xiŕa`. Means "To be indeed from" or "Absolutely originates from".
