# vå ([[18_Conjunctions & Clause Linking]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">vå</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">vå</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Temporal Connector
- **Function:** **Simultaneous Point**
- **Meaning:** "When".

### Syntax

Placed at the **end** of the time-setting clause.

- **Structure:** `[Event A] vå, [Event B].`

### Etymology

Derived from **[[va- (Relational Locative Prefix)|va]]** (Inside). _Logic:_ "Inside the time (of)..."

### Pronunciation

IPA: /vau̯/

### Example sentence

**John pănů vå, ko shěsonů.** _When John goes outside, she reads._
