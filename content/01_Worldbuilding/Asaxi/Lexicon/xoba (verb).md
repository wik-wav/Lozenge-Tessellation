# xoba ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">xoba</span>

### Transitivity / Valency

**Ambitransitive**
* **Intransitive:** To step aside, to strafe, to dodge.
* **Transitive:** To shunt, to move X aside, to sideline.

### Lexical Aspect

**Activity:** Lateral motion.

### Grammatical Note

**[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** Compound root verb.

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Motion]]

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɦoba/

### Translations

- English: to step aside, to move sideways, to dodge, to shunt
- Polish: przesunąć się, zrobić unik, odsunąć

### Example sentence

**To wo xoba.**
_I step aside (dodge)._

### Etymology

**xo** (Move) + **[[ba- (Relational Locative Prefix)|ba]]** (Beside).
