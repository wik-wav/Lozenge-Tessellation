# Synthesized Narrative Framework: The Arc of Escalating Insight

This document integrates the systems-based narrative framework with the specific world-building mechanics of the [[The Lozenge Tessellation|Lozenge T.]].

## 1. Core Systems & Environmental Volatility

The narrative engine relies on the friction between the Projection's avoidance and the environment's reactivity.

* **The Threshold of Volatility:** The NREM-like baseline is maintained solely by apathy and routine. The exact threshold that shifts the NREM zone into a highly volatile REM state is the Projection experiencing intense emotion, frustration, or a sudden realization.
* **The "Focus" Trade-off:** "Focus" is the ultimate expression of the Projection's avoidant balancing loop. By focusing intensely to overcome a specific obstacle, the Projection becomes completely unaware of the surrounding environment.
* **Delayed Feedback (Time Skips):** Time perception is broken. While the Projection uses Focus and ignores the environment, the environment ages or mutates rapidly in response to the suppressed emotional friction.
* **Physical Roadblocks:** The time skipped during Focus results in severe incongruities. When focus breaks, the Projection is blindsided by aggressive environmental mutations (e.g., sudden moss overgrowth, grotesque structural anomalies like "tumors") that physically impede their routine.

## 2. Contextual Bleed (False Memory/Deja Vu)

Events from previous Arrangements carry over, even if the Projection is unaware of the cycle. This "deja vu" is manifested purely through physical markers before the Projection gains conscious insight:
* **Muscle Memory:** The Projection reaches for objects, weapons, or handholds that existed in previous iterations but are missing in the current NREM zone.
* **Conditioned Flinching:** The Projection instinctively panics or flinches at specific sensory details (a smell, a sound) that consistently precede the violent shift into the REM state.
* **Environmental Echoes:** The Projection discovers physical marks (e.g., gouges in stone) that perfectly match the dimensions of an unremembered injury or lost tool.

## 3. Character Baseline: Form vs. Function

* **Feral Discrepancy:** Dominant life forms are animalistic. To avoid caricatures, the Projection's physical feral form must directly contrast with their underlying psychological Nature (e.g., a fragile prey animal form housing a highly aggressive, analytical function). This constant friction serves as a baseline stressor and a built-in incongruity.

## 4. The Micro-Structure (Chapter/Arrangement Loop)

Each chapter must resolve its immediate physical tension before awakening, leaving internal tension unresolved.

1.  **Baseline:** Projection engages in a localized, rigid routine within a stable NREM zone.
2.  **Incongruity:** A flaw appears. The Projection uses "Focus" to avoid or bypass it.
3.  **Delayed Escalation:** Focus breaks. The environment has mutated severely (time skip/physical roadblock). The suppressed emotion crosses the threshold.
4.  **REM Shift:** The environment violently fractures into the REM state.
5.  **Localized Resolution:** Not necessarily a triumphant resolution. Something must resolve, have local consequence, answer to the questions posed earlier, propose new ones, etc. The final resolution should unambiguously result in courageous (uncharacteristic) behaviour. One question should remain: is this change for the better, or is it erasure? 
6.  **Awakening Reset:** The excitation peaks. The environment resets, but the insight and compounding self-resentment carry into the next cycle.

## 5. The Macro-Arc (Zone Collision)

* **Act I (The Bubble):** Restricted to Zone 1. Total unawareness. Focus is on routine and the first failures of avoidance.
* **Act II (The Bleed):** Overlapping zones. Gained insight allows the Projection to witness gaps. Focus fails. Self-resentment compounds as the routine shatters.
* **Act III (The Convergence):** Self-resentment transforms into courage. The Projection intentionally triggers catastrophic excitation. This deliberate lucidity forces all zones into a simultaneous, fractal collision in Zone 1 to resolve the core psychological conflict.
