# xowỏ ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">xowỏ</span>

### Transitivity / Valency

**Ambitransitive**
* **Intransitive:** To converge, to center oneself.
* **Transitive:** To center X, to bring X to the middle.

### Lexical Aspect

**Activity:** Centering motion.

### Grammatical Note

**[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** Compound root verb.

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Motion]]

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɦowou̯/
*Phonotactics:* Bridge `-w-` inserted between `xo` and `ỏ`.

### Translations

- English: to center, to converge, to align to middle
- Polish: wyśrodkować, zbiegać się

### Etymology

**xo** (Move) + **-w-** + **[[ỏ- (Relational Locative Prefix)|ỏ]]** (Middle).
