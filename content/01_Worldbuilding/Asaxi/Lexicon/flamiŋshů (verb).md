### Transitivity / Valency
**Intransitive** (Self-concealment) or **Transitive** (Hiding an object).

### Lexical Aspect
- **Durative (Activity)** — unfolds over time.
- **Aspect markers**: **na-** "do repeatedly" · **tå-** "do once" · **ni-** "begin to" · **chå-** "do fully".

### Semantic Field
[[Smntc_Field Behaviour]] / Stealth

### Pronunciation
IPA: /fla.mi.ŋ.ʃuw/
*Morphology:* `flami` + `nŋ` (Reduced to `ŋ`) + `sh` + `ů`.

### Translations
- English: to hide, to conceal, to camouflage, to blend in, to stop showing off
- Polish: chować się, ukrywać, kamuflować

### Example sentence
**To wo flamiŋshů.**
_I am hiding._ (I am being un-conspicuous).

**To wo shěso flamiŋshů.**
_I hide the book._

### Alternative Forms
Null

### Etymology
**flaminŋ** (Flamingo/Display) + **-sh-** (Remove) + **-ů**.
*Cultural Logic:* Since the flamingo represents "Posing/Standing out," the removal of that quality represents "Hiding."
