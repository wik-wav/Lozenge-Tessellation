# -ch- ([[06_Verbs in Asaxi]])

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Morphological Infix (Bridge)
- **Function:** **Subjective Mode**
- **Meaning:** To feel like (internal belief/emotion), "I believe I am..."

### Pronunciation

IPA: /tʃ/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">ch</span>
- **Pattern:** `none`
- **Class:** `atonal`

### Example sentence

to wo apochů. I feel like an apple.

### Etymology

Derived from Polish _czuć_ (to feel/sense).
