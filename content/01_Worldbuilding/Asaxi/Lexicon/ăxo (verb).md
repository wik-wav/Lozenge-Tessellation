# ăxo ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">ăxo</span>

### Transitivity / Valency

**Transitive**
Takes an object (the person or path being followed).

### Lexical Aspect

**Activity:** Continuous movement in pursuit.

### Grammatical Note

**[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** Compound root verb.

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Motion]] / [[Smntc_Field Behaviour]]

### Pronunciation

IPA: /aɪɦo/

### Translations

- English: to follow, to pursue, to trail, to track, to go after
- Polish: podążać (za), śledzić, iść za

### Example sentences

**Literal:**
**To wo no ăxo.**
_I follow you._

### Etymology

**[[ă (Relational Particle)|ă]]** (Subjective Marker) + **[[xoxo (verb)|xo(xo)]]** (Move/Depart).
*Lit:* "To move (with/as) the subject." (To shadow the subject's movement).
