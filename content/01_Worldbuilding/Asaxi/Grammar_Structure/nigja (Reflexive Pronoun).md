# nigja ([[13_Pronouns in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">nigja</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">nigja</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Reflexive Pronoun
- **Function:** **Self-Reference** (3rd Person Plural Non-Binary)
- **Meaning:** "Themselves" (non-binary group).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɲigʲa/

### Example sentence

**To gja nigja ijo.** _They (nb) see themselves._

### Etymology

**[[ni (Relational Particle)|ni]]** (Towards) + **[[gja (Pronoun)|gja]]** (They-nb).
