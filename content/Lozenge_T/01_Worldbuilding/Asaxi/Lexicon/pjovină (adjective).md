### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Qualitative Adjective (`-nă`)
- **Source Agreement:** **Warm** (Root `pjovi` is Warm).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /pʲovinaɪ/

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Motion]] / [[Smntc_Field Behaviour]]

### Translations

**As an adjective:**
- English: direct, straight, focused, unwavering
- Polish: bezpośredni, prosty, skupiony

**As an adverb:**
- English: without changing course, directly, straightly, unwaveringly
- Polish: prosto, bez zbaczania, bezpośrednio

### Example sentences

**Adjective:**
**To onă shosa pjovină xiŕa.**
_The road is straight (laser-like)._

**Adverb (Manner):**
**To wo pjovină aśù.**
_I walk without changing course._ (I walk laser-ly).
