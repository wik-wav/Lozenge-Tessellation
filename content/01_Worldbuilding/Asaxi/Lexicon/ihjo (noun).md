### Noun class (warm / cold)

**Cold**
- Reasoning: Structural, mineral-like, survives death (inanimate quality).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /iço/

### Semantic field
[[Smntc_Field The Body]] / Materials

### Translations
- English: bone, os
- Polish: kość

### Plural Form
**ihja** (Bones).

### Example sentence
**To wo ihja gopů.**
_My bones feel hard._

### Etymology
Likely related to **[[hjo (noun)|hjo]]** (Star) due to the white/bright color of exposed bone, or **[[ijo (verb)|ijo]]** (See) implying "That which remains visible" after death.
*Lit:* "White-object" or "Star-substance."

### Derived terms
- **ihjokam** (Skeleton).
- **ihjonă** (Bony / Thin / Skeletal).
