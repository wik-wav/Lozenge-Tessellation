### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Interrogative Adverb (Spatial)
- **Meaning:** Where?

### Pronunciation

IPA: /kɕi/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">ksi</span>
- **Pattern:** `L`
- **Class:** `atonal`

### Etymology

Derived from the question prefix **k-** + **[[siŕo (noun)|si]]** (Root of _siŕo_ - The Great Unknown). _Phonotactic Note:_ Uses `si` instead of `gă` (Somewhere) to avoid the forbidden cluster `kg`.

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** john ksi aśù?
- **English:** Where is John walking?
