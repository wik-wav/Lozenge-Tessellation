# ńopa ([[46_Temporal Nouns]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">ńopa</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">ńopa</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Temporal Noun (Locative)
- **Meaning:** In the immediate future, on the brink, momentarily.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɲopa/
*Note:* The `ni` + `o` fusion creates the palatal nasal /ɲ/.

### Semantic field

[[Smntc_Field Time]]

### Example sentence

**Ńopa, to wa gavină pabůchů.**
_We will feel well in the immediate future._

### Etymology

**[[ni (Relational Particle)|ni]]** (Towards) + **[[opa (Noun)|opa]]** (Immediate Future).
