# aŕa ([[12_Discourse Markers in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">aŕa</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">aŕa</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Post-Clausal Discourse Marker
- **Function:** **Resignation / Acceptance**
- **Meaning:** "It is what it is", "Alas", "Can't be helped", "So it goes".

### Syntax

Placed at the **very end** of the sentence (Post-Predicate Tail).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /aɾa/

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** john shánă ů aŕa.
- **English:** John is being human (fallible), alas.
- **Polish:** x

### Etymology

Sound symbolism for a sigh or release of tension. Possibly related to **[[ă (particle)|ă]]** (Internal feeling) + **[[xiŕa (particle)|-ŕa]]** (Fact). "I feel it is a fact (and accept it)."
