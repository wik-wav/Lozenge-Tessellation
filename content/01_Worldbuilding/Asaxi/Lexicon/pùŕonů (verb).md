# pùŕonů ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_ů (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">pùŕonů</span>

### Transitivity / Valency

**Transitive** (Splitting an object) or **Ditransitive** (Sharing object with someone).

### Lexical Aspect

**Accomplishment:** Separation into parts.

### Grammatical Note

Derived from **[[pùŕo (Mathematical Operator)|pùŕo]]** (Division) + **-n-** + **-ů**.
* *Logic:* "To perform division."

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Behaviour]] / Social

### Pronunciation

IPA: /pɯɾonuu̯/

### Translations

- English: to share, to split, to distribute, to divide
- Polish: dzielić się, rozdzielać

### Usage Note

Used for sharing food or tasks. "To divide" is seen as a social act of distributing resources (lowering the value to the base level).

### Example sentence

**To wa apo pùŕonů.**
_We share (split) the apple._

### Antonyms

- **[[naŕo (Mathematical Operator)]]** (To mix / combine / multiply).
