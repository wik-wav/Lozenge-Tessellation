# se ([[02_Particles in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">se</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">se</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Particle type:** Connective Particle (Nominal Coordinator)
- **Function:** **Disjunctive Noun Linker**
- **Meaning:** "Or" (linking words/phrases, not clauses).

### Usage Notes

Used to present alternatives within a single argument slot (Subject, Object, Adjective).
* **Structure:** `[Noun A] se [Noun B]`.
* **Contrast:**
    * **si:** Connects Clauses ("Do A **or** do B").
    * **se:** Connects Nouns ("Apple **or** Pear").

### Pronunciation

IPA: /se/

### Example sentences

**Nouns:**
**To John se Tom pashěsonů.**
_John or Tom will read._

**Adjectives:**
**To wo gavină se gaponă apo jå.**
_I want a green or red apple._

### Etymology

Derived from **[[si (OR Logical Clause Connector)|si]]** (Clausal Or), with the vowel shifted to **e** to match the register of other nominal particles (like `sè`).
