### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Interrogative Adverb (Temporal)
- **Meaning:** When?

### Pronunciation

IPA: /kvau̯/

### Etymology

Derived from the question prefix **k-** + **[[vå (WHEN Temporal Connector)|vå]]** (When - Clause Connector).

### Example sentence

**Kvå xoxo?** _When do (you) depart?_
