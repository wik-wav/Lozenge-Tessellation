### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Tense Modifier Prefix
- **Function:** **Remote / Mythic Tense**
- **Meaning:** "Long ago" (with Past) or "Someday/Far future" (with Future).

### Syntax

Stacks on top of Tense prefixes.

- **Mythic Past:** `kozè-[Verb]`
- **Distant Future:** `kopa-[Verb]`

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** kozèxoxo.
- **English:** He departed long ago.
- **Polish:** x
