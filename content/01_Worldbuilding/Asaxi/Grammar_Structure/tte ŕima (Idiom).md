# tte ŕima ([[45_Idioms & Fixed Expressions]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">tte ŕima</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">tte ŕima</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Epistemic expression (Quotative + cognition verb).
- **Meaning:** Flags the preceding clause as an **opinion / uncertain belief** — "I think that..."

### Usage Note

Appended after a clause to mark it as belief rather than asserted fact.

### Structure

`[Clause] tte ŕima`

### Pronunciation

IPA: /tːə ɾima/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">t · te | ŕi · ma</span>
- **Pattern:** `L · L | H · L`
- **Class:** `phrase`

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** to john shánă tte ŕima.
- **English:** I think that John is wise.
- **Polish:** x

### Etymology

**[[tte (particle)|tte]]** (quotative) + **[[ŕima (verb)|ŕima]]** (to think, to meditate).
