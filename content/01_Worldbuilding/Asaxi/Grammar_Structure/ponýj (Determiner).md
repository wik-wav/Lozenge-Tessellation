### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Determiner (Specific Indefinite)
- **Agreement:** **Cold** Nouns
- **Meaning:** "A certain", "A specific".

### Example sentence

**To wo ponýj shěso jå.**
_I want a certain book (not just any book)._

### Etymology

**po** (Certainty) + **-nýj** (Cold Suffix).
