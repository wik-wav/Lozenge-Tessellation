# nỏwë ([[02_Particles in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">nỏwë</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">nỏwë</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Mood Particle (Sentence Final)
- **Function:** **Negative Necessitative**
- **Meaning:** "Don't need to", "Don't have to", "Not necessary".

### Pronunciation

IPA: /nou̯weɪ/

### Example sentence

**To no xoxo nỏwë.** _You don't have to leave._
