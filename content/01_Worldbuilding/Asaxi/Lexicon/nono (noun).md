### Noun class (warm / cold)
**Cold**
*Inanimate object. This is the quintessential "cold" noun, representing matter without spirit or specific sentimental value.*

### Pronunciation
IPA: /nono/

### Semantic Field
[[Smntc_Field Existence|Existence]], [[Smntc_Field Materials|Materials]]

### Translations
- English: thing, object, item, article, entity (inanimate)
- Polish: rzecz, przedmiot, obiekt

### Definition & Cultural Note
A generic physical object. **Nono** is used to refer to an item that has no specific name known to the speaker, or when the speaker wishes to emphasize the object's inanimate, ordinary nature.

### Example sentence
Sè shěsokam bwonă nono mëjonů.
I'm looking at a beautiful thing from a library.

### Alternative Forms
- no (root form, often used in compounds)

### Etymology
Reduplication of the root [[no (pronoun) (particle)|no]] (it/that/proximal object marker).
*Literally: "thing-thing" or "just a thing".*
