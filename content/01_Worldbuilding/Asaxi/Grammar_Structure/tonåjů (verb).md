### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Epistemic Marker / Sentence Final Compound
- **Function:** Subjective Prediction / Foresight
- **Meaning:** "I predict that...", "I foresee that...", "I visualize that..."
    
    - _Usage:_ Marks the preceding clause as a future event or outcome that the speaker actively visualizes or predicts. Unlike the Future Tense (`pa-`), which simply states the future, `tonåjů` emphasizes the speaker's act of foresight.
