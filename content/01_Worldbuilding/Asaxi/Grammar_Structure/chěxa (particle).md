# chěxa ([[18_Conjunctions & Clause Linking]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">chěxa</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">chěxa</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Conditional Compound
- **Function:** **Concessive**
- **Meaning:** "Even if", "Although", "Despite".

### Syntax

Placed at the **end** of the concessive clause.

- **Structure:** `[Condition] chěxa, [Result].`

### Etymology

**chě** (If) + **[[xa- (relational locative) (particle)|xa]]** (Above). _Logic:_ "If \[X\], but the result is **above** (overrides) the condition."

### Pronunciation

IPA: /tʃɚɦa/

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** topo toponů chěxa, pahaśù.
- **English:** Even if it rains, (I) will run.
