# okonoko ([[02_Particles in Asaxi]])

- - -

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Adverbial Particle / Aspectual Modifier
- **Function:** **Spatial Distributive**
- **Meaning:** "Here and there", "Scattered", "All over the place."
- **Nuance:** Implies chaotic or irregular distribution across space.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /okonoko/

### Etymology

A reduplicative chain of proximal locatives: **[[o (proximal locative) (particle)|o]]** (Here) + **[[ko (distal locative) (particle)|ko]]** (Yonder) + **[[no (medial locative) (particle)|no]]** (There) + **ko** (Yonder).
*Lit:* "Here-yonder-there-yonder."

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** topo toponů okonoko.
- **English:** It is raining here and there (scattered showers).
