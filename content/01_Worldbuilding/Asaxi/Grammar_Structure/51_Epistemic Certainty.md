## Grammatical Concept: The Hierarchy of Truth

In Asaxi, the grammar strictly separates **Objective Fact** from **Subjective Opinion**. The choice of words dictates whether the speaker claims to know the absolute truth or is merely offering a perspective.

### 1. Objective Certainty (The "Is" System)

The use of specific grammatical forms implies 100% certainty. You cannot use these if you are guessing.

**A. The Stative Particle (`xiŕa`)**

- **Meaning:** "It is a fact."
- **Logic:** Defines reality.
- **Constraint:** Using `xiŕa` means you have proof or absolute conviction.

**B. Constitutional Nouns (`ga-` Compounds)**

- **Meaning:** "Physically/Materially X."
- **Logic:** `Gafůbåbå` (Muscleless) is a diagnosis.
- **Constraint:** You cannot use a `ga-` noun to describe a vibe or impression. You must use an adjective (`-nă`) or qualify it.

**C. The Active Copula (`ů`)**

- **Meaning:** "Is being/acting."
- **Certainty:** High certainty regarding the _current action_, but does not assert permanent truth.

---

### 2. Subjective Qualification (The `tte` Construction)

To express degrees of certainty, Asaxi uses the **Quotative/Topic Particle [[tte (particle)|tte]]** followed by a **Cognitive Verb**.

- **Structure:** `[Statement] tte [Cognitive Verb]`
- **Logic:** "As for [Statement], I [Cognition]."

#### The Certainty Scale

|Level|Expression|Components|Meaning|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Absolute**|**(Direct Statement)**|_None_|"It is." (Fact).|
|**Sure**|**tte xăcè**|`xă` (Truly) + `cè` (Know)|"I am sure that..." / "I know for a fact."|
|**Known**|**tte cè**|`cè` (Know)|"I know that..."|
|**Think**|**tte ŕima**|`ŕima` (Think)|"I think that..." / "I reckon."|
|**Unsure**|**tte pùŕima**|`pù` (Under) + `ŕima`|"I'm not at all sure" / "I suspect/weakly think."|
|**Doubt**|**tte náŕima**|`ná` (Not) + `ŕima`|"I don't think that..."|
### 3. Temporal Judgments (Past & Future Certainty)

Speakers can comment on the _validity_ of a past or future event by using `tte` with a **Tensed Stative**.

**A. Subjective Past ("That's how I think it was")**

> **[Statement] tte ŕima zèxiŕa.** `QT` `think` `PAST-EXIST` _"That's how I think it was."_ (Somewhat uncertain).
> 
> - _Logic:_ "As for that, thought existed." (The thought occurred, but the fact is memory).

**B. Subjective Fact ("That's how it was")**

> **[Statement] tte sỏxiŕa.** `QT` `MEMORIC-EXIST` _"That's how it was (in my memory)."_
> 
> - _Logic:_ Uses the **Subjective Past [[sỏ (particle)|sỏ-]]**. It asserts the fact, but frames it as a personal memory rather than a history book entry.

**C. Future Fact ("That's how it will be")**

> **[Statement] tte paxiŕa.** `QT` `FUT-EXIST` _"That's how it will be."_ (Prophecy / Certainty).
