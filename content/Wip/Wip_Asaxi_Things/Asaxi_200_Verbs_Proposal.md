# Proposal — 200 New Verbs

**Mode:** reviewed. **Strategy:** gap-fill the most common missing actions. **Split:** ~70% **fresh root verbs** (basic actions — short, often mimetic; instantaneous *Achievements* unless locomotion), ~30% **noun+infix** derivations. **Loans:** one chosen loan (`ochi` ← Jp.). **Type** column: **R** = fresh root; otherwise the **mode-infix** used (`n` use · `x` force-upon · `w` act-like · `k` turn-into · `ŕ` produce · `sh` strip · `j/s/ŋ/p/zh/ch` sensory). Noun-based verb = `[noun]+[infix]+ů`.

> Review tips: strike/rename freely; flag aspect (root vs `-ů`) or infix-choice changes. Examples are short, first-pass (SOV; `na-` = iterative, `chå-` = completive). On approval I write full entries (templates, lists, fields, links).

### Tally
**200 verbs** — ~**146 fresh roots** (≈73%) + ~**54 noun+infix** (≈27%). Infix spread: `n` use (×20), `ŕ` produce (×9), `x` force-upon (×4), `sh` strip (×3), `k` turn-into (×2), and the sensory `ŋ/zh/s/p/w/ch` (×7). Native but for one chosen loan (`ochi`).

### Flags for you
- **Aspect:** I marked the basic actions as **root** verbs (instantaneous *Achievements*); a few are arguably durative (e.g. `nùnù` sleep, `vůwù` swim, `ănù` rest) — per canon those could instead be `-ů` activities. Tell me which should flip.
- **Phonotactics to confirm:** `hacù` (sneeze), `cka`/`cpỏka`-based forms, and the `kŕ-/fŕ-/pŕ-` clusters are all legal by the r-rule; `xŕ-` is **not**, so I'll re-cut `kŕaa`→`kŕaa` (wake) and `fŕopù`→`fŕopù` (faint) on the integrity pass.
- **`na-`/`chå-`** modify these roots for iterative/completive aspect as usual.
- **Pronunciation note:** `cèkma` (trust) = **/t̻͡s̪əʔma/** — the `k` surfaces as a glottal stop; I'll record that IPA on the entry.
