# waonă ([[03_Asaxi Adjectives (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">waonă</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Qualitative Adjective (`-nă`)
- **Source Agreement:** **Warm** (Root `wao` is Warm).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /wa.o.naɪ/

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Emotion]] / Comfort

### Translations

- English: homely, familiar, cozy, worldly (in a belonging sense)
- Polish: swojski, domowy, znajomy

### Usage Note

"World-like." Implies the feeling of being in one's own element or domain.

### Example sentence

**To o-kamm waonă xiŕa.**
_This building is homely (feels like my world)._

### Etymology

**[[wao (noun)|wao]]** (World) + **-nă**.
