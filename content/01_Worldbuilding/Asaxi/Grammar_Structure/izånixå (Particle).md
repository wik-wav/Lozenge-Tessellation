### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Frequency Adverb (Floating)
- **Function:** **Intermittent Frequency**
- **Meaning:** "From time to time", "Every now and again", "Occasionally".

### Usage Notes

Functions as a **Floating Adverb**, typically placed in the **Pre-Verbal** slot.
* **Structure:** `[Subject] + izånixå + [Object] + [Verb]`.

**To Tom izånixå shěsonů.**
_Tom reads every now and again._

*Lit:* "From-Time-Into-Time."
