# Act III Resolution: The Zone 1 Return Blueprint

This final section serves as the ultimate proof of Kjè's character arc. The environment and the physical stimuli are identical to Chapter 1, but Kjè’s internal response mechanism has been fundamentally rewired. 

## 1. The Waking World Resolution
* **The Second Bleed:** As the fractal collision of Zone 7 settles into the Zone 1 baseline, a final, brief flash of the waking world occurs.
* **The Act of Courage:** The Dreamer is shown executing an act of genuine self-acceptance or courage, directly contrasting the pathetic choice to throw away the plushie. This waking-world resolution stabilizes the dreamscape's foundation.

## 2. The Recontextualized Catalyst
* **The Identical Setup:** The narrative resumes precisely where Chapter 1 ended: *"The recorder still had the record function engaged."* Lem, identifiable by his specific color scheme and accessories, sits beside Kjè.
* **The Glitch:** The battery drains, and the audio mangles into the exact same stuttering loop: *"...gthing’s fine y felt off day off now-no-o-o-o-o..."*
* **The Insight Applied:** In Chapter 1, Kjè's internal logic interpreted this as an apocalyptic loss of control. Now, armed with the insight from Zone 7, he correctly identifies it as a simple mechanical failure. The fear of external punishment is gone.

## 3. The Consolidation (Shared Silence)
* **The Death of the Proxy:** The device clicks off. The loop ends. 
* **The Absence of Panic:** Kjè does not sob. He does not view the death of the device as the severing of his identity. 
* **True Connection:** Kjè recognizes that the music and the worth reside within him. He simply looks up at the sky and smiles. He shares this quiet, unforced moment with Lem. The environment remains perfectly calm, not because of avoidant "Focus," but because his internal psychological friction has ceased.
