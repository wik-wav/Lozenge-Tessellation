# wăpùni ([[01_Asaxi Nouns (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">wăpùni</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">wăpùni</span>
### Noun class (warm / cold)

**Cold**

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Number Noun (Quantifier)
- **Meaning:** "Few", "Not many", "Scarce".

### Usage Notes

Functions as a **Floating Quantifier**.
* **Nuance:** Implies a small, countable number (specifically "Single Digits" or "Less than ten").

### Pronunciation

IPA: /waɪpɯɲi/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">wă · pù · ni</span>
- **Pattern:** `L · L · L`
- **Class:** `atonal`

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** to wăpùni shěsa tobo nanů.
- **English:** Few books are on the table.
- **Polish:** x

### Etymology

**[[wă (Number)|wă]]** (Nine) + **[[pùni (particle)|pùni]]** (Less/Downward).
*Lit:* "Less than nine."

### Synonyms

- **[[hjigo (particle)]]** (Few - Abstract).

### Antonyms

- **wănani** (Many).
