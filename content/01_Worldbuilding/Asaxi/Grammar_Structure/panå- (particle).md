# panå- ([[14_Future & Complex Tenses]])

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Tense & Aspect Prefix
- **Function:** **Declarative Switch** / **Immediate Future Switch**
- **Meaning:** "Will now (do)", "Switching to", "Going to (instead)".

### Usage Notes

Indicates that the subject is immediately changing their course of action or plan.
* **Structure:** `panå-[Verb]`.
* **Distinction:** Distinct from the sentence-final particle **[[panå (particle)|...panå]]** ("Not yet").

### Pronunciation

IPA: /panau̯/

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** to wo panåshěsonů.
- **English:** I will read now (instead of what I was doing).
- **Polish:** x

### Etymology

**[[pa- (particle)|pa-]]** (Future) + **[[nå (particle)]]** (Now).
*Lit:* "Future-Now."

### Synonyms

- **[[opa- (particle)|opa-]]** (Immediate Future - "About to", but less emphasis on *switching*).
