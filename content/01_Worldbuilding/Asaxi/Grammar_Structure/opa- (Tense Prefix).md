# opa- ([[14_Future & Complex Tenses]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">opa-</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">opa-</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Tense Prefix.
- **Function:** **Immediate / near future** — "about to," an action on the verge of happening. Nearer than the plain future [[pa (Tense Prefix)|pa]]; contrast the not-yet [[panå- (Tense Prefix)|panå-]].
- **Meaning:** about to (do).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /o̞pa/

### Etymology

**o-** (proximal, "here/now") + **[[pa (Tense Prefix)|pa]]** (future): a future drawn near to the present.

### Example sentence

**To wo opashěsonů.**
_I am about to read._
