# sů ([[02_Particles in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">sů</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">sů</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Particle type:** [[02_Particles in Asaxi|High Binding Particle]] (Prefix/Suffix)
- **Function:** **Continuation Command** / **Persistive Mood**
- **Meaning:** "Continue!", "Keep [verbing]", "Proceed", "Don't stop".

### Usage Notes

* **Suffix:** `[Verb]-sů` (Keep doing X).
* **Prefix:** `sů-[Verb]` (Persist in X).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /suu̯/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">sů</span>
- **Pattern:** `L`
- **Class:** `atonal`

### Example sentence

#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** tom, shěsonůsů.
- **English:** Standard: Tom, continue reading.
- **Polish:** x

#### Example 2

- **Asaxi:** john, ni jåhjo sůijo.
- **English:** Idiomatic: John, don't give up! (Keep your eyes on the prize.)
- **Polish:** x

### Etymology

Derived from Japanese **進む** (*susumu* - to advance).

### Synonyms

- **xopa** (To advance/proceed - Verb).

### Antonyms

- **[[nă (particle)|nă]]** (Stop!).
- **[[tomo' (verb)|tomo' hè]]** (Halt).
