### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Number Noun (Indefinite Quantifier)
- **Meaning:** "Many", "A lot of", "Numerous", "A multitude".

### Usage Notes

Functions as a **Floating Quantifier**.
* **Structure:** `dăgo [Noun]` or `[Noun] dăgo`.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /daɪgo/
