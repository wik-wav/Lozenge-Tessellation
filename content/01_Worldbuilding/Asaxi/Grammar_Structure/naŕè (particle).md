### Grammatical function
- **Type:** Mood Particle (Sentence Final)
- **Function:** **Obligative Mood**
- **Meaning:** "Should", "Ought to", "Supposed to".

### Pronunciation

IPA: /naɾə/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">na · ŕè</span>
- **Pattern:** `L · L`
- **Class:** `atonal`

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** to no aśù naŕè.
- **English:** You should walk.
- **Polish:** x

### Etymology

**na** (On/Burden) + **ŕè** (Particle suffix).
