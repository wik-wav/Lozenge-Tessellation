# sèni ([[18_Conjunctions & Clause Linking]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">sèni</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Causal Connector (Fused)
- **Function:** **Mid-Sentence Link**
- **Meaning:** "So", "Therefore", "Consequently".

### Syntax

Functions as a bridge between two clauses.

- **Structure:** `[Clause A] sèni [Clause B].`

### Etymology

A fusion of **[[sèni (SO Logical Clause Connector)|sè(wo)]]** (Because) + **[[ninå (THEREFORE Logical Clause Connector)|ni(nå)]]** (Therefore). Literally: from cause (sè) to result (ni)

### Pronunciation

IPA: /səɲi/

### Example sentence

**Toponů sèni, wo izo kamm panápănů wő.** _It's raining, so I won't exit from the building, I tell you._
