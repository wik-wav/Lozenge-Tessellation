- **Type:** Qualitative Adjective (`-nýj`)

### Semantic Field

### Translations

- English: slow, sluggish, gradual, unhurried
- Polish: wolny, powolny

### Usage Note

Implies a pace similar to plant growth—quiet, barely perceptible, or lacking urgency.

_The lizard is slow._
