### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Interrogative Pronoun (Animate)
- **Meaning:** Who?

### Pronunciation

IPA: /kʃɑ/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">kshá</span>
- **Pattern:** `H`
- **Class:** `dominant`

### Etymology

Derived from the question prefix **k-** + **[[shá (noun)|shá]]** (Person). _Lit:_ "Question-Person?"

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** kshá shěsonů?
- **English:** Who reads? (Note: The particle `kè` is dropped for efficiency).
