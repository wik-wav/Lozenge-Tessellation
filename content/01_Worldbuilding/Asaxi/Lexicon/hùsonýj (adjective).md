### Etymology
The **aura/simile** colour of [[hùso (noun)|hùso]] (sand): hùso + **-nýj**. It is the **cold other-class equivalent** of [[xină (adjective)|xină]] — the same yellow at the opposite emotional temperature: the dry, washed-out, lifeless pale of sand rather than the radiant gold of the sun. Built from a genuinely **cold** object so the suffix obeys **Source Agreement**. See [[16_Adjectives_Constitution vs Simile]].
