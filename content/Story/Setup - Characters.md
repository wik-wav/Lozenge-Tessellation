# Setup - Characters of the Kjè Story

This note serves as documentation of the characters of the  Kjè story. The characters are categorized by their recurrence as well as impact on the plotline.

## Primary characters
#### Protagonist: The Projection

The Projection named Kjè  ([[kjè (root word)|kjè (Asaxi root word) - "of or related to trees"]] is a Giraffe of short stature (overall size and strength). Because of his physical state, he developed into a tracer-nightmare, but as with all natures, he is unaware of this. He is not keen on partaking in any activities that rely on strength, resilience or overall confidence in physical ability. For what he lacks in that department, he makes up for in conscientiousness and rigidity. He is like a homing missile, already fired and on track toward his target. As structured as he tries to be, things inevitably go awry and when they do, he becomes unpredictable and quite explosive. While he is aware of his pursuit of stability, deep down he yearns for connection, which requires ceasing control and taking note of emotion, balancing safety with risk.
Due to his nature for much of the story, he is not well liked. It remains unclear if he is still a nightmare after the story concludes.
Plausible deniability: Actions taken by The Projection usually stem from fear, not malice. In contexts where he lost control, his recklessness often results in events which trigger a deepening self-hatred festering within, even though there is no ill-will involved in the majority of the incidents in which he takes the role of a catalyst.

#### Deuteragonist: [[Lem the Shapeshifter|Lem the Shapeshifter]]

Lem takes the role of a helpful tracer-guide shapeshifter, but not overtly so. He is a recurring character, but each time appears in a different shape, recognized only by his clothing's colour scheme and specific accessories. He is very brave and as such, charismatic. Mostly all bark, no bite. The fact he is a tracer only surfaces once recognized by the Projection as Lem.

#### Contagonist: The environment of [[The Lozenge Tessellation|The Lozenge Tessellation]]'s dreamscape

Affected by state of affairs — sways either which way the action takes it. Often causes further chaos.

## Plot Devices

#### Extras

Appear in each chapter. Interact with the environment passively, until directly confronted. Once noticed, they attempt to maintain their routine. Given enough attention, they may snap out of it and cause turmoil or resolution in a brief moment of lucidity.

#### Nighthare 

See [[Natures of Projections in the Lozenge Tessellation|Natures of Projections in the Lozenge Tessellation]] for detail. One of the entities killed by The Projection in the latter chapters (mistakenly).

## Background characters
 
#### [[Dă Chỏ (deity)|Dă Chỏ (deity)]]

A giant mammoth of blue skin. Depictions of Dă Chỏ appear in the story as part of environmental worldbuilding.
