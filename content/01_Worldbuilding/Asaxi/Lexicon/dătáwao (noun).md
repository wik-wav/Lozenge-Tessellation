### Usage Note (Philosophy)
This term does not refer to "Nature" in the sense of greenery or peace. It refers to the world as a system of **Great Conflict** (`dă-tá`).
* *Logic:* The world is held together by opposing forces (Gravity, Thermodynamics, Entropy). The "Clash" is what creates the cohesion of reality. It is the world "as it is," stripped of social constructs.
