### Translations

- English: dream (sleeping), vision (nightly)
- Polish: sen (marzenie senne)

### Plural Form

**ijoŕa** (Dreams).

### Usage Note (Metaphor)

In Asaxi, dreams are viewed as **fluid**. You do not "see" or "have" a dream; you **drink** (`ŕoŕonů`) it.
* *Logic:* Sleep is a pool (waterhole) you drink from, and the visions are the water you consume.

### Example sentence

**Izo hwo mao, wo tam ijoŕa zèŕoŕonů.**
_Yesterday (night), I had (drank) two dreams._
*(Lit: From yesterday night, I two dreams drank).*]), mao (noun) - as in night-time.md]

*Lit:* "See-water" or "Visible-water."
