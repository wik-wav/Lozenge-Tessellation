# săsă ([[39_Numerals & Mathematics]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">săsă</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">săsă</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Quantifier / Prefix
- **Function:** **Completeness**
- **Meaning:** "All", "Entire", "Whole", "The full extent".

### Usage Notes

Can be used as a standalone word (`săsă`) or reduced to a prefix (`să-`) when attaching to units like days.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /saɪsaɪ/

### Example sentence

**Săhwo topo zètoponů.**
_It rained all yesterday._

**To wo săsă apo zèchỏnů.**
_I ate the entire apple._

### Etymology

**[[să (Number)|să]]** (One) + **să** (One).
*Logic:* "One of One" (1/1 = 100%).
