### Noun class (warm / cold)

Cold

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Number Noun (Quantifier)
- **Meaning:** "Most", "The majority", "Almost all".
- **Nuance:** Implies a large portion but explicitly less than `săsă` (All/Whole).
