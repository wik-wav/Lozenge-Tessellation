### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Degree Prefix
- **Function:** **Approximative** ("About", "Roughly")
- **Meaning:** Indicates the quality is present but inexact or questionable.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /me/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">me</span>
- **Pattern:** `L`
- **Class:** `atonal`

### Etymology

Derived from **[[e (particle)|me]]** (Skepticism/Question).

### Example sentence

#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** to [machine] meshěsonýj [calculating]nů.
- **English:** The machine calculates about as intelligently (as a book).
- **Polish:** x

### Analysis

- **Adverb:** `shěsonýj` (book-like/smart)
- **Verbal mode:** `-n-` (performance/function)
