# panå- ([[14_Future & Complex Tenses]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">panå-</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Tense & Aspect Prefix
- **Function:** **Declarative Switch** / **Immediate Future Switch**
- **Meaning:** "Will now (do)", "Switching to", "Going to (instead)".

### Usage Notes

Indicates that the subject is immediately changing their course of action or plan.
* **Structure:** `panå-[Verb]`.
* **Distinction:** Distinct from the sentence-final particle **[[panå (Particle)|...panå]]** ("Not yet").

### Pronunciation

IPA: /panau̯/

### Example sentence

**To wo panåshěsonů.**
_I will read now (instead of what I was doing)._

### Etymology

**[[pa- (Relational Locative Prefix)|pa-]]** (Future) + **[[nå (NOW Aspectual Marker)]]** (Now).
*Lit:* "Future-Now."

### Synonyms

- **[[opa- (Tense Prefix)|opa-]]** (Immediate Future - "About to", but less emphasis on *switching*).
