# xă- ([[29_Degree & Comparison]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">xă-</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Degree Prefix
- **Function:** **Intensifier** ("Very", "Truly")
- **Meaning:** Increases the intensity of the adjective or adverb to a high degree.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɦaɪ/

### Etymology

Derived from **[[xă (Particle)|xă]]** (Indeed/Emphasis).

### Example sentence

**To John [box] xădănă [pushing]xů.** _John pushes the box **very heavily**._

- _Adverb:_ `dănă` (Elephant-like/Heavy)
    
- _Verbal Mode:_ `-x-` (Interaction/Force).
