### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Polarity Particle Variant
- **Function:** **Pre-Labial Negation**
- **Meaning:** "Not" (Specific context).

### Usage Note

Used exclusively before particles or elements starting with the semi-vowel **/w/**.

- **Standard:** `ná`
- **Contextual:** `nỏ` (before `w`).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /nou̯/

### Derived terms

- **nỏwă** (Not want).
- **nỏwë** (Not need / Don't have to).
