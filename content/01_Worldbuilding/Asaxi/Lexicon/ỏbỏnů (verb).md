# ỏbỏnů ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_ů (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">ỏbỏnů</span>

### Transitivity / Valency

**Transitive**
Takes an object (the thing being protected/cushioned).

### Lexical Aspect

**Activity:** Functioning as a buffer.

### Grammatical Note

Derived from **[[ỏbỏ (noun)|ỏbỏ]]** (Cushion) + **[[-n- (Morphological Infix)|-n-]]** (Performance/Function) + **-ů**.
* *Logic:* "To use a cushion" / "To function as a cushion."

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Housing & Shelter]] / Safety

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ou̯bou̯nuu̯/

### Translations

- English: to cushion, to buffer, to soften (a blow), to pad
- Polish: amortyzować, łagodzić

### Example sentence

Context: Tom just fell out of a window from a high-rise building.
**Wo Tom zèxỏbỏnů.**
_I cushioned Tom (from something)._

### Etymology

"To perform the function of a cushion."
