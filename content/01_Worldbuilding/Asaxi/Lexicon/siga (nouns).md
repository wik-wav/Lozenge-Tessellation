# siga ([[39_Numerals & Mathematics]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">siga</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Cardinal Number
- **Value:** **0**

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɕiɡa/

### Etymology

Borrowed from Polish _zero_ / Japanese _zero_, adapted to Asaxi phonology (`z` → `dz`).

### Example sentence

**To wo dzeŕo apo ma.** _I have zero apples._
