# ja ([[02_Particles in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">ja</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">ja</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Particle type:** [[02_Particles in Asaxi|Connective Particle]] (Coordinator)
- **Function:** **Nominal Conjunction** ("And")
- **Meaning:** Connects nouns or noun phrases within a single argument slot.

### Syntax Rules

- **Placement:** Placed before the **last** item in a list.
- **Comma Usage:** Asaxi uses commas (or pauses) between earlier items.
- **Scope:** When used inside a particle block (like `zá` or `då`), the relational particle applies to the **entire group**.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /Ʝa/

### Antonyms

- **[[jhaná (particle)|jhaná]]** (But Not): Excludes the following item. `A jhaná B` (A but not B).

### Example sentence

**John zá Tom, Jerry ja Barry xoxo.** _John leaves with Tom, Jerry, and Barry._
