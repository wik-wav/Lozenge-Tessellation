### Grammatical function

- **Particle type:** [[02_Particles in Asaxi|High Binding Particle Compound]]
- **Function:** **Imperative Switch**
- **Meaning:** "Now do!", "Switch to!", "Do this instead!".

### Usage Notes

Used to command someone to stop their current action and immediately begin a new one. It often appears in the construction `[Verb 1] tomo nåhè [Verb 2]`.
* **Structure:** `nåhè` + `[Verb]` (or as a standalone command to switch).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /nau̯xə/
