### Alternative Forms

### Etymology
A reduplicated root (**po-po**) — reduplication being a signature of basic, tactile objects — evoking the rounded, knobbed curl and hollow knock of a shell. Patterns with [[ihjo (noun)|ihjo]] (bone): both cold, pale mineral remnants of living things. (The bright reduplicated form is lexical; warmth still follows sense — an inert remnant stays cold.)
