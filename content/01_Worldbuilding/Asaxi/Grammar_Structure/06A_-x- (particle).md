# -x- ([[06_Verbs in Asaxi]])

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Morphological Infix (Bridge)
- **Function:** **Interaction Mode**
- **Meaning:** To do X to (someone), to force X upon, to apply X transitively.

### Pronunciation
