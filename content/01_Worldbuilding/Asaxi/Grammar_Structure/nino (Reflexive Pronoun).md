# nino ([[13_Pronouns in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">nino</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Reflexive Pronoun
- **Function:** **Self-Reference** (2nd Person Singular)
- **Meaning:** "Yourself".

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɲino/

### Example sentence

**To no nino ijo.** _You see yourself._

### Etymology

**[[ni (Relational Particle)|ni]]** (Towards) + **[[no (Pronoun)|no]]** (You).
