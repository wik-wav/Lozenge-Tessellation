### Transitivity / Valency
**Monotransitive**
Takes an object (the person/thing loved).

### Lexical Aspect
- **State** — an ongoing condition, not an event.
- **Aspect markers**: **na-** "do repeatedly" · **tå-** "do once" · **ni-** "begin to" · **chå-** "do fully".

### Grammatical Note
**[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** Compound root verb.

[[Smntc_Field Emotion]]

### Pronunciation
IPA: /daɪsɯki/

### Translations
- English: to love, to adore, to cherish (deeply)
- Polish: kochać, miłować, uwielbiać

### Example sentence
**To wo ni no sùkixů.**
_I love you._ (I love unto you).
