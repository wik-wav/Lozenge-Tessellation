### Etymology
From **fgă** (contraction of *fůgăxă*) + **ŕo** (water). *fgăŕo* is the **general term for any lake** — a large, standing body of water of no particular cultural marking. It is distinct from [[sháŕo (noun)|sháŕo]], which is specifically the culturally-significant **waterhole** where creatures gather.
