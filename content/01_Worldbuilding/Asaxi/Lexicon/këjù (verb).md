### Transitivity / Valency
_monotransitive_

### Lexical Aspect
- **Punctual (Achievement)** — happens in a single moment.
- **Aspect markers**: **na-** "do repeatedly" · **tå-** "do once" · **ni-** "begin to" · **chå-** "do fully" · **-ů** "make it ongoing".

### Grammatical Note
- **[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** Root/compound verb — may take **-ů** to derive a durative reading, and `na-`/`tå-`/`ni-`/`chå-` for other aspects.
