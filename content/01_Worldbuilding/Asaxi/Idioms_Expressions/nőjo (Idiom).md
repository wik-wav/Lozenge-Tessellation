### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Fixed Expression (Greeting)
- **Meaning:** "Hello!", "Hi!" — general greeting, any time of day.

### Usage Note

Literally "(I) see (you) there."
* Reply by echoing: nőjo!
- **Full formal form:** to wo no ijo. ("I see you.")

### Structure
`no-` (MED) + `ijo` (see), with i-coalescence (`o` + `i` → `ő`)

### Pronunciation
IPA: /noɪjo/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">nő · jo</span>
- **Pattern:** `H · L`
- **Class:** `lexical`

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** nőjo, ăjo john! ă no ksá?
- **English:** Hello, John! How are you feeling?
- **Polish:** x

**[[no (medial locative) (particle)|no-]]** (Medial Demonstrative, "there") + **[[ijo (verb)|ijo]]** (to see), coalesced per [[22_Phonotactics & Euphony]].
