# wë ([[02_Particles in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">wë</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">wë</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Mood Particle (Sentence Final)
- **Function:** **Necessitative Mood**
- **Meaning:** "Need", "Require", "Necessary".

### Pronunciation

IPA: /weɪ/

### Example sentence

**To wo haśù wë.** _I need to run._

### Etymology

Sound symbolism for "Weight" or "Pressure" (related to `w`).
