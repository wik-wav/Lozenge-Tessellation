# xi gavină ([[63_Social Formulae (Greetings & Everyday Speech)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">xi gavină</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">xi gavină</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Fixed Expression (Formal Greeting)
- **Meaning:** "Good day!" — formal daytime greeting.

### Usage Note

Literally "**The day/sun (is) good**" — a zero-copula attribution ([[08_Minimal Sentence]], pattern *Apo gapo*).
* Used in formal or polite contexts; the casual equivalent is **[[nőjo (Idiom)|nőjo]]**.
* May also close a daytime encounter ("good day to you").

### Structure
`[Noun] + [Adjective]` (zero copula)

### Pronunciation
IPA: /ɦi gaʋinaɪ/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">xi | ga · vi · nă</span>
- **Pattern:** `H | H · L · L`
- **Class:** `phrase`

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** xi gavină! sè no zèvo kjo?
- **English:** Good day! What is your name?
- **Polish:** x

### Etymology

**[[xi (noun)|xi]]** (sun, day) + **[[gavină (adjective)|gavină]]** (good, well) — the sun as the measure of a good day.

### Synonyms

- **[[nőjo (Idiom)|nőjo]]** (casual)

### Antonyms

- [[måmå natăka (Idiom)|måmå natăka]] (counterpart: farewell)
