### Grammatical function

- **Particle type:** [[15_NPCP and The Agglutinative Block|Relational Particle (NPCP)]]
- **Function:** **Genitive** Case Marker
- **Meaning:** Indicates possession or association ("Of", "'s", "Belonging to").

### Pronunciation

IPA: /sə/

### Example sentence

1. **Standard Possession:** 
	**Sè** John. _Of John / John's._

2. **Nested Structure:** 
	To wo **sè** John gavishěso ijo. 
	_I see John's green-book._

### Derived terms

- **sèŕa** (Predicative Fusion): The result of fusing `sè` + `xiŕa`. Means "To be owned by" or "To be yours".

- **násèŕa** (Negative Predicative): Means "Is NOT yours".
