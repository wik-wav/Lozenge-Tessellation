### Grammatical function

- **Particle type:** [[15_NPCP and The Agglutinative Block|Compound Relational Particle]]
- **Function:** **Terminative-Allative** Case Marker
- **Meaning:** "As far as to", "Up to", "All the way to".

### Logic & Etymology

A fusion of **[[måmå (particle)|måmå]]** (Until/Limit) and **[[ni (particle)|ni]]** (Direction/Allative).

- _Logic:_ Defines the specific endpoint of a path. Unlike simple `ni` (which implies direction), `måmåni` implies reaching the limit and stopping there.

### Syntax

Occupies the **Oblique Argument** slot (Goal).

- _Structure:_ `måmåni [Location]`

### Pronunciation

IPA: /mau̯mau̯ɲi/
