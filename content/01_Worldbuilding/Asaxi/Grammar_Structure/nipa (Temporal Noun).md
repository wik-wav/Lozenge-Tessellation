# nipa ([[46_Temporal Nouns]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">nipa</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">nipa</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Temporal Noun (Locative)
- **Meaning:** In the future, into the future.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɲipa/

### Semantic field

[[Smntc_Field Time]]

### Example sentence

**Nipa, to wa pahaśù.**
_In the future, we will run._

### Etymology

**[[ni (Relational Particle)|ni]]** (Towards) + **[[pa (Noun)|pa]]** (Future).
*Lit:* "Towards-Future." (Standard Directional Time).
