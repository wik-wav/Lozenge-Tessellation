# nőjo ([[63_Social Formulae (Greetings & Everyday Speech)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">nőjo</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">nőjo</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Fixed Expression (Greeting)
- **Meaning:** "Hello!", "Hi!" — general greeting, any time of day.

### Usage Note

Literally "**(I) see (you) there.**"
* Reply by echoing: **Nőjo!**
* Full formal form: **To wo no ijo.** ("I see you.")

### Structure
`no-` (MED) + `ijo` (see), with i-coalescence (`o` + `i` → `ő`)

### Pronunciation
IPA: /noɪjo/

### Example sentence

**Nőjo, ăjo John! Ă no ksá?**
_"Hello, John! How are you feeling?"_

### Etymology

**[[no (Medial Locative Prefix)|no-]]** (Medial Demonstrative, "there") + **[[ijo (verb)|ijo]]** (to see), coalesced per [[22_Phonotactics & Euphony]].

### Synonyms

- **[[xi gavină (Idiom)|xi gavină]]** (formal, daytime)

### Antonyms

- **[[måmå natăka (Idiom)|måmå natăka]]** (farewell)
