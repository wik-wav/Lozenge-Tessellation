# hjá ([[01_Asaxi Nouns (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">hjá</span>

### Noun class (warm / cold)

**Warm**
- Reasoning: Animate young living being.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /çɑ/
*Phonotactics:* `hj` becomes the voiceless palatal fricative /ç/.

### Semantic field

[[Smntc_Field Animals]]

### Translations

- English: child, young one, offspring, baby, kid
- Polish: dziecko, młode (zwierzęcia)

### Usage Note (Morphology)

Functions as a **Suffix** to animal roots to denote their young.
* **Structure:** `[Animal Root] + hjá`.
* **Example:** `mmrå` (Cat) + `hjá` $\rightarrow$ **mmråhjá** (Kitten).

### Example sentence

**To hjá ŕima.**
_The child thinks._

### Etymology

**[[hji (noun)|hji]]** (Slightness/Smallness) + **-á** (Animate Suffix? / Warm Vowel).
*Lit:* "The slight-one."
