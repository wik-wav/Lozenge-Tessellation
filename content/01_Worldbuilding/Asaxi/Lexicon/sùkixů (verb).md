# sùkixů ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">sùkixů</span>

### Transitivity / Valency

**Monotransitive**
Takes an object (the person/thing loved).

### Lexical Aspect

**State:** Emotional condition.

### Grammatical Note

**[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** Compound root verb.

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Emotion]]

### Pronunciation

IPA: /daɪsɯki/

### Translations

- English: to love, to adore, to cherish (deeply)
- Polish: kochać, miłować, uwielbiać

### Usage Note

Stronger and more empathetic than simple the word for "like". Used for romantic love or deep familial bonds.

### Example sentence

**To wo ni no sùkixů.**
_I love you._ (I love unto you).

*Lit:* "love unto"
