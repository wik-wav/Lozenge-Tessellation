# năbwonădăsùkină ([[03_Asaxi Adjectives (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">năbwonădăsùkină</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Superlative Adjective
- **Function:** **Ultimate Endearment**
- **Meaning:** "Most beloved", "Dearest", "Most adored".

### Pronunciation

IPA: /naɪbʷonaɪdaɪsɯkinaɪ/

### Translations

- English: most beloved, dearest, most cherished
- Polish: najukochańszy, najdroższy

### Usage Note

A highly poetic and formal term of endearment.

### Example sentence

**To no năbwonădăsùkină xiŕa.**
_You are the most beloved._

### Etymology

**[[nă- (Degree Prefix)|nă-]]** (Most - Warm) + **[[bwonădăsùki (noun)|bwonădăsùki]]** (Adoration) + **-nă** (Adjective suffix).
*Lit:* "The Most Adored-One."
