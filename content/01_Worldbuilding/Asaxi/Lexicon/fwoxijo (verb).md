- **Aspect markers**: **na-** "do repeatedly" · **tå-** "do once" · **ni-** "begin to" · **chå-** "do fully".

### Grammatical Note
**[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** Compound root verb.

### Semantic Field
[[Smntc_Field Emotion]]

### Pronunciation
IPA: /fʷoɦiꞲo/

### Translations
- **English:** to be lost in thought, to have a hazy outlook, to daydream (vaguely), to stare into the fog.
- **Polish:** zamyślić się, bujać w obłokach, patrzeć w dal (bez celu).

### Usage Note
Implies a state of "dreaming of going places but not knowing how to get there." It is a passive, hazy vision, unlike the active desire of `jå`.
