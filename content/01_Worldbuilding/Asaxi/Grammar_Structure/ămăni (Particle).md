# ămăni ([[02_Particles in Asaxi]])

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Temporal Adverb / Phrase
- **Function:** **Permissive Time**
- **Meaning:** "At your leisure", "Whenever you feel like", "When the time is right for you".

### Usage Note

Used to give the listener total freedom over the schedule. It implies "It doesn't matter to me; do it when it suits you."

### Pronunciation

IPA: /aɪmaɲi/
