Use the translator below to search reviewed, human-authored Asaxi and English
phrases. Authored results carry a verification mark and remain available in
the static site.

The complete rule-based translator and grammatical analysis require the local
Workbench service (or a future deployed equivalent). The static publication
keeps those controls visibly unavailable rather than substituting a reduced,
incompatible grammar engine.
