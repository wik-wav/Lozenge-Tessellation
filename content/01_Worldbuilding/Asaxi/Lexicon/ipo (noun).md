### Example sentence

**Sè xő pỏ gacù ja gasi ipa zèma.**
_His coat had brown and white spots._

### Alternative Forms

### Etymology

From the perception root of **ijo** (see) / **ijpù** (spot, notice) + **-po** (spot, cf. ŕopo "puddle" = water-spot): a visible fleck.
