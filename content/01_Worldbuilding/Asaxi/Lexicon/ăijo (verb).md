# ăijo ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">ăijo</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">ăijo</span>
### Transitivity / Valency
**Intransitive** (Internal) / **Monotransitive** (Cognitive) Usually used without an object to describe an internal shift in state, but can take a clause or fact as an object.

### Lexical Aspect
- **Punctual (Achievement)** — happens in a single moment.
- **Aspect markers**: **na-** "do repeatedly" · **tå-** "do once" · **ni-** "begin to" · **chå-** "do fully" · **-ů** "make it ongoing".

### Grammatical Note
**[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** This belongs to the closed class of primitive verbs.

- **Morphology:** It functions as a compound root.

### Semantic Field
[[Smntc_Field Emotion]]

### Pronunciation
IPA: /aɪijo/ (A distinct glide from the diphthong /aɪ/ into /i/).

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">ă · i · jo</span>
- **Pattern:** `H · H · L`
- **Class:** `mixed`

### Translations
- **English:** to get a grip on the situation, to have a sudden conviction, to realize (deeply), to see clearly (metaphorical), to have an epiphany about one's state.
- **Polish:** zorientować się (w sytuacji), nabrać przekonania, przejrzeć na oczy (przenośnie), uświadomić sobie stan rzeczy.

### Example sentence
Null

### Alternative Forms

### Etymology
**[[ă (particle)|ă]]** (Subjective Subject Marker / "The Internal Self") + **[[ijo (verb)|ijo]]** (To see). _Lit:_ "Self-see" or "Subjective-view."

### Synonyms
- **[[cè (verb)|cè]]** (To know - static knowledge).

### Antonyms

- [[năijo (verb)|năijo]] - to feel lost (in a situation)
- [[ŕăijo (noun)|ŕăijo]] - power
- [[fůŕăijo (noun)|fůŕăijo]] - weakness
- [[ŕăijonă (adjective)|ŕăijonă]] - strong
- [[ŕăijoshá (noun)|ŕăijoshá]] - lion

### Usage Note
Describes a moment of clarity where the speaker assesses their reality and forms a strong, unshakeable belief (positive or negative) about the outcome. It implies looking "inward" (`ă`) to see (`ijo`) the truth.

### Example sentences

#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** wo ăijo. pwo gavină paniŕa.
- **English:** Positive Conviction: I grasp the situation. Tomorrow will turn out well.

- **English:** Gloss: SUBJ John [situation] very-heavily REALIZED: "John got a very heavy (serious) grip on the situation." (Note: Using `xădănă` as an adverb for mental weight).
