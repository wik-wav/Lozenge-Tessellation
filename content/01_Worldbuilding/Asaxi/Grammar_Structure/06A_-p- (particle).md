# -p- ([[06_Verbs in Asaxi]])

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Morphological Infix (Bridge)
- **Function:** **Tactile Mode**
- **Meaning:** To feel like (to the touch), to have the texture of.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /p/

### Example sentence

to tobo apopů. The table feels like an apple (smooth/waxy).

### Etymology

Labial plosive (lips touching).
