# nixő ([[13_Pronouns in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">nixő</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Reflexive Pronoun
- **Function:** **Self-Reference** (3rd Person Singular Male)
- **Meaning:** "Himself".

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɲiɦoɪ/

### Example sentence

**To xő nixő ijo.** _He sees himself._
