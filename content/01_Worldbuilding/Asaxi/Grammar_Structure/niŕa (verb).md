### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Stative Verb
- **Function:** **Allative State**
- **Meaning:** "To become", "To turn into", "To lead to", "To be destined for".

### Logic

**[[ni (particle)|ni]]** (Towards) + **[[xiŕa (particle)|-ŕa]]** (Validity/Fact).
* *Lit:* "To exist-towards."
* *Nuance:* Unlike `ůků` (Transform/Morph), `niŕa` implies a natural progression or destiny. "The boy becomes a man" (`niŕa`).
