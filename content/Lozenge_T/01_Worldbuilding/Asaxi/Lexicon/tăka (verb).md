# tăka ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">tăka</span>

### Transitivity / Valency

**Reciprocal** (We join) or **Transitive** (I join you).

### Lexical Aspect

**Activity:** The process of merging.

### Grammatical Note

**[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** Compound root verb.

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Behaviour]] / Social

### Pronunciation

IPA: /t̪aɪka/

### Translations

- English: to unite, to join forces, to merge, to ally
- Polish: jednoczyć się, łączyć się, sprzymierzać

### Usage Note

Derived from the mathematical concept of **Addition** (`tă`) applied reciprocally.
* *Logic:* "To add to one another."

### Example sentence

**To wa tăka.**
_We unite (join forces)._

### Etymology

**[[tă (Mathematical Operator)|tă]]** (Plus) + **[[-ka (Verbal Suffix)|-ka]]** (Reciprocal).
*Lit:* "Plus-Exchange."

### Antonyms
- **[[mëka (verb)]]** (To separate).
