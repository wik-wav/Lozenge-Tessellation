# wao ([[01_Asaxi Nouns (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">wao</span>

### Noun class (warm / cold)

**Warm**
- Reasoning: A world is viewed as a living system or a radiating sphere of influence.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /wa.o/

### Semantic field

[[Smntc_Field Existence]]

### Translations

- English: world, realm, domain, sphere
- Polish: świat, domena

### Example sentence

**To sè wo wao sèni, tama nèŕa.**
_This is my world, therefore no limits exist._

### Etymology

**[[wa (Pronoun)|wa]]** (We) + **[[o (noun)|o]]** (Sky/Sphere).
*Lit:* "Our-Sphere."

### Derived terms

- **[[waonă (adjective)]]** (Homely/Familiar).
- **[[owao (noun)]]** (Earth).
