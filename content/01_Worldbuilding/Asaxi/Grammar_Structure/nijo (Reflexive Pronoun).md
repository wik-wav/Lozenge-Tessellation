# nijo ([[13_Pronouns in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">nijo</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">nijo</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Reflexive Pronoun
- **Function:** **Self-Reference** (3rd Person Inanimate)
- **Meaning:** "Itself".

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɲiꞲo/

### Example sentence

**To jo nijo ijo.** _It sees itself._

### Etymology

**[[ni (Relational Particle)|ni]]** (Towards) + **[[jo (Pronoun)|jo]]** (It).
