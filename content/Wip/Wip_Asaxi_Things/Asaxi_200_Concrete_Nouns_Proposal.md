# Proposal — Next 200 Concrete Nouns (v3, revised)

**Mode:** reviewed. **Strategy:** gap-fill by frequency. **Loans:** ~19, marked **†**. **Cls** = W(arm)/C(old).

**v3 changes:** all your sentence corrections applied; **`gùma`→`kma`** (global, +'stick' sense); 18 new verbs + 3 adjectives **documented in their own vault notes** (xè, kiŕonů, hkanů, xokŕonů, ånů, xo, ŋèxů, fùcù, ůŕů, ůshů, toponů, banů, mënů, chỏnů, dåmë, xopă; sxëbonýj, miană, zhýnýj); **loan endings** (consonant→è, a→á); **shávo removed** (→ **199 nouns** — `shá` already = person). Open coinage to confirm: **magma = `ŕăŕo`** (used in the volcano definition).

**Earlier (v2) revisions:** `š`→`sh`, `u`→`ù`, `ź`→`zh`; **head-final** morpheme order (`kaehjá`→`hjákae`, etc.); **back-vowel + aa → á** (`nomaka`→`nomáka`); **consonant-harmony** on non-iconic repeated-vowel roots (`koko`→`kogo`); your specific renames; food → **C**; **every example now carries its English gloss**.

> New root introduced: **kùso** "faeces, dung" (← Jp. *kuso*), used in `kùsokùki` (fly). It would get its own entry on write.
