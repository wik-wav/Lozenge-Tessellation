### Grammatical function

- Particle type: Frequency Adverb / Floating Modifier
- Function: Annual Frequency
- Meaning: "Yearly", "Annually", "Every year".

### Pronunciation

IPA: /tʰaɪmaɪ/

### Example sentence

To wa txămă xoxo.
We depart yearly.
