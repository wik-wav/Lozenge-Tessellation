### Noun class (warm / cold)
**Warm**
- Reasoning: Born of fire and still kin to its heat — the glowing-dark residue of the hearth, hence **warm**.

### Pronunciation

### Etymology
Transparent compound: **ŕa** (fire, from [[ŕăa (noun)|ŕăa]]) + **go** (stone) → *fire-stone*. Warm by its fire-association, unlike cold inert ash.
