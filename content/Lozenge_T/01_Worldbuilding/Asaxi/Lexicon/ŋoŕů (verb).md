# ŋoŕů ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_ů (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">ŋoŕů</span>

### Transitivity / Valency

**Transitive** (To weigh upon someone/something).

### Lexical Aspect

**Activity / State:** Exerting pressure or burden.

### Grammatical Note

Derived from **[[ŋo (Unit Noun)|ŋo]]** (Weight) + **[[06A_-ŕ- (Morphological Infix)|-ŕ-]]** (Generative Mode) + **-ů**.
* *Lit:* "To generate weight (upon)."

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Emotion]] / Physics

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ŋoɾuu̯/

### Translations

- English: to add weight, to weigh down, to burden, to weigh upon (metaphorical)
- Polish: dociążyć, ciążyć (komuś), obciążać

### Example sentence

**Kozètètá wo ŋoŕů izånixå.**
_Regrets weigh on me from time to time._

### Antonyms

- **ŋoshů** - to remove weight
