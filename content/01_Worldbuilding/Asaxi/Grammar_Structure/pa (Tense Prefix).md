# pa ([[14_Future & Complex Tenses]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">pa</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Tense Prefix
- **Function:** **Explicit Future**
- **Meaning:** Indicates an action that will definitely occur.

### Etymology

Derived from **[[pa- (Relational Locative Prefix)|pa-]]** (Front/Anterior).

### Example sentence

**To wo pashěsonů.** _I will read._
