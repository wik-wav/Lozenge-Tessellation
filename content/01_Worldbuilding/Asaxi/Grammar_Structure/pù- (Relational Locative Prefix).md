# pù- ([[07_Describing Location in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">pù-</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Degree / Comparative Prefix
- **Function 1:** **Comparative Degree** ("Less").
    - *Usage:* `pù-[Adjective]`. (e.g., `pùdănă` "Smaller").
- **Function 2:** **Compound Comparative Prefix** ("Less than [Noun]").
    - *Usage:* `pù-[Noun]-[AdjectiveRoot]`.
    - *Example:* **pùjýnnjýno** ("Less loud than a hyena" / quieter).
- **Meaning:** Below, under, beneath.

### Syntax

Attaches to the front of the noun.

- **Compound:** `pù` + `o` → **pùwo** (Below-here).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /pɯ/

### Example sentence

**To shěso pùtobo xiŕa.**
_The book is under the table._

**To wo pùjýnnjýnă.**
_I am less chatty than a hyena._

### Derived terms

- **pùnů** - To be below.
- **pùŕa** - To be below (Stative).
