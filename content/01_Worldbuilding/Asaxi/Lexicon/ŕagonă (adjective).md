### Etymology
The **aura/simile** colour of [[ŕago (noun)|ŕago]] (charcoal): ŕago + **-nă**. It is the **warm other-class equivalent** of [[ŋènýj (adjective)|ŋènýj]] — the same hue at the opposite emotional temperature, but built from a genuinely **warm** object so that the suffix obeys **Source Agreement** (warmth follows the source noun, never a flipped suffix).
