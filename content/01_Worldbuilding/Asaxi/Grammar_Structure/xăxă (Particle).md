# xăxă ([[33_Desiderative Subjunctive Moods]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">xăkă</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">xăkă</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Subjunctive Particle (Sentence Final)
- **Function:** **Hypothetical Marker**
- **Meaning:** "Would", "As if", "Supposedly".

### Usage Note

Marks the statement not as reality, but as a constructed or imagined scenario. It carries a nuance of internal visualization ("scheming" or "nodding along").

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɦaɪɦaɪ/

### Example sentence

**Xő jo topù chě, wo ma xăxă.** _If he dropped it, I **would** have (it)... (yes, yes...)_

### Etymology

Reduplication of **[[xă (Particle)|xă]]** (Indeed/Yes). _Lit:_ "Yes... yes..." (Trailing off into imagination).

### Synonyms

x

### Antonyms

- **[[xă (Particle)|xă]]** (Fact/Realis).
