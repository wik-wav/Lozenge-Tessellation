# chěná ([[18_Conjunctions & Clause Linking]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">chěná</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">chěná</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Conditional Compound
- **Function:** **Negative Conditional**
- **Meaning:** "Unless", "Except if".

### Syntax

Placed at the **end** of the exception clause.
- **Structure:** `[Condition] chěná, [Result].`

### Etymology

**chě** (If) + **[[ná (particle)|ná]]** (Not). _Logic:_ "If not \[X\]."

### Pronunciation

IPA: /tʃɚnɑ/

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** john aśù chěná, wo pahaśù.
- **English:** Unless John walks, I will run.
