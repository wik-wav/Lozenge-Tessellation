# Act II Initiation: Arrangement 9402 (Zone 2)
**Location:** Platform 7 Zone (Embi s-07)

## 1. The Dynamic of Approval

* **The Encounter:** Kjè encounters a traveler-busker (seducer nature). Desperate for connection and validation, Kjè perceives this entity as possessing unparalleled beauty and aggressively seeks its approval.
* **The Offer:** Blind to the entity's true busker nature, Kjè accepts an offer to take a ticket, join its journey, and perform together, stepping out of his rigid comfort zone for the promise of connection.

## 2. The Shared Failure & The Contagion Delusion

* **The Station Hazard:** Upon exiting the train, they face a crowd of intimidating figures on the opposite side of the platform. Crucially, the crowd completely ignores the duo. 
* **The Internal Rationalization:** Because Kjè views the traveller as inherently charismatic and flawless, his internal logic refuses to blame the traveller or the environment for this rejection. Instead, he concludes that his own physical meekness and unworthiness are a contagion — that his mere presence physically diminished the traveller’s light.
* **The Separation:** Consumed by this perceived guilt, Kjè loses his way on the isolated station, effectively severing the connection.

## 3. The Ambiguity Pivot

* **Lem's Intervention:** Lem arrives to guide Kjè onto the correct train back. 
* **The Structural Friction:** Lem casually mentions that the traveller looked utterly repulsive. Because the reader is locked in Kjè's tight perspective (where the traveller was beautiful), this creates total narrative ambiguity. The reader cannot tell if Lem is lying to comfort him, if the traveller was a predatory illusion, or if Kjè's mind is completely unravelling.
