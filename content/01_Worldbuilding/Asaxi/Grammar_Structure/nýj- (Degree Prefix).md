# nýj- ([[29_Degree & Comparison]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">nýj-</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Superlative Prefix
- **Function:** **Maximum Degree** ("Most")
- **Constraint:** Used only when the Adjective's source noun is **Cold**.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /nɪj/

### Etymology

Duplication of the cold adjectival suffix **-nýj**.

### Example sentence

**To [computer] nýjshěsonýj [processing]nů.** _The computer processes the **most intelligently**._
- _Adverb:_ `shěsonýj` (Book-like/Smart - Cold Source `shěso`)
- _Verbal Mode:_ `-n-` (Performance).
