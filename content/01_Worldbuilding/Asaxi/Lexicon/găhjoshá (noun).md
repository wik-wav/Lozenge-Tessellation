# găhjoshá ([[01_Asaxi Nouns (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">găhjoshá</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">găhjoshá</span>
### Noun class (warm / cold)
**Warm**
- Reasoning: Animate person (even if emaciated).

### Pronunciation
IPA: /ɡaɪçoʃɑ/

### Semantic Field
[[Smntc_Field The Body]] / Health

### Translations
- English: emaciated person, walking skeleton, bag of bones, starving creature
- Polish: chudzielec, kościotrup (żywy), mizerota

### Example sentence
**Sè găhjosháma tëbiwa hjihjină tamỏbwoxatëbibåba ma.**
`of` `bony-people` `arms` `thin` `biceps` `have`
_Skinny people's arms have small biceps._

### Alternative Forms
Null

### Etymology
**[[găhjo (noun)|găhjo]]** (Emaciation/Bone-type) + **[[shá (noun)|shá]]** (Person).
*Lit:* "Bone-type-person."

### Synonyms
- **[Thin person]** (Derived from `hjihji`).

### Antonyms
- **[[gabwoshá (noun)|gabwoshá]]** (Fat person).
- **[[gabåbåshá (noun)|gabåbåshá]]** (Muscular person).

### Derived terms
- **găhjoshánă / găhjoshánýj** (adjectival forms) *(potential)*

### Usage Note
Refers to a living being who is so thin their bones are visible (`găhjo`). Distinct from **[[ihjokam (noun)|ihjokam]]** (Actual skeleton structure).
