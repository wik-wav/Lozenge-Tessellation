### Grammatical function

This word marks a noun as being "over yonder".

#### As an adverb

As an adverb, `ko` refers to the thing or place being pointed at physically. (e.g, `ko.` "This (place or thing over yonder) I'm pointing at").

#### As a Tense Prefix

Function: Remote / Mythic Tense
Meaning: "Long ago" (with Past) or "Someday/Far future" (with Future).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ko/

### Alternative Forms

kő - for when the word after this prefix starts with a vowel
