# xèjå ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">xèjå</span>

### Transitivity / Valency

**Monotransitive**
Takes an object (the thing being attempted).

### Lexical Aspect

**Activity:** Process of attempting.

### Grammatical Note

**[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** This belongs to the closed class of primitive verbs.

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Behaviour]]

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɦəjau̯/

### Translations

- English: to try, to attempt, to exert effort
- Polish: próbować, starać się

### Example sentence

**To wo \[task\] xèjå.**
_I attempt the task._

### Etymology

**xè** (Sound of exhale during exertion) + **[[jå (verb)|jå]]** (To want).
*Lit:* To want with exertion.

### Derived terms

- **xè-** (Conative Prefix: "Try to...").
