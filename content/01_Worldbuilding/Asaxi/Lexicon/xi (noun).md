# xi ([[01_Asaxi Nouns (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">xi</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">xi</span>
### Noun class (warm / cold)
**Warm**
- Reasoning: The sun is the source of heat.

### Pronunciation
IPA: /ɦi/

### Semantic Field
[[Smntc_Field Weather & Climate]] / [[Smntc_Field Time]]

### Translations
- English: sun, day (unit)
- Polish: słońce, dzień

### Example sentence

#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** to xi dănă xiŕa.
- **English:** The sun is big.
- **Polish:** x

#### Example 2

- **Asaxi:** onă xi gavină zèbů.
- **English:** The day was good
- **Polish:** x

#### Example 3

- **Asaxi:** vwo, xi dănă pjoŕů.
- **English:** Today, the sun shone very brightly.
- **Polish:** x

### Alternative Forms
- **mă** (in frequency compounds like `ximă`).

### Etymology
Hi (Japanese 日 - Sun/Day).

### Synonyms
- x

### Antonyms
- **[[mao (night-time) (noun)|mao]]** (Night/Moon).

### Derived terms
- [[gaxi (noun)|gaxi]] - yellow
- [[xină (adjective)|xină]] - sunny, golden, radiant
- [[xiro (noun)|xiro]] - gold
- [[xiŕă (noun)|xiŕă]] - sunlight, sunshine
### Usage Notes
- Write the word with a capital letter if referring to the sun, eg. `xi`
- When used in the sense of "day", use `xi`.
