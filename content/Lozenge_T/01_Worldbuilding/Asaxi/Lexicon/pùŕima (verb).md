# pùŕima ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">pùŕima</span>

### Transitivity / Valency

**Monotransitive** (Suspecting X) or **Intransitive**.

### Lexical Aspect

**State / Activity:** A continuous state of low-confidence thinking.

### Grammatical Note

**[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** Compound root verb.

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Perception]] / Cognition

### Pronunciation

IPA: /pɯɾima/

### Translations

- English: to suspect, to doubt, to be unsure, to reckon (weakly), to suppose
- Polish: podejrzewać, przypuszczać (słabo), nie być pewnym

### Usage Note

Used with the particle **tte** to form the epistemic expression **tte pùŕima** ("I'm not at all sure that...").

### Example sentence

**To wo tte pùŕima.**
_I suspect so (weakly)._ / _I'm not sure._

### Etymology

**[[pù- (Relational Locative Prefix)|pù-]]** (Below/Under) + **[[ŕima (verb)|ŕima]]** (Think).
*Lit:* "Under-think." (To think with low confidence/intensity).

### Antonyms

- **[[xăcè (verb)|xăcè]]** (To be sure).
