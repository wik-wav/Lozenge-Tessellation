# dzè ([[18_Conjunctions & Clause Linking]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">dzè</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">dzè</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Logical Clause Connector
- **Function:** **Adversative** (Contrast)
- **Meaning:** "But", "However".

### Syntax

Placed at the **end** of the first clause.

- **Structure:** `[Clause A] dzè, [Clause B]`

### Pronunciation

IPA: /d̻͡z̪ə/

### Example sentence

**To wo aśù dzè, haśùná.** _I walk, but I do not run._
