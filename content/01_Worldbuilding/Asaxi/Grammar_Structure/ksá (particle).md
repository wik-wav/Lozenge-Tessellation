# ksá ([[25_Interrogative Pronouns (Wh-Words)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">ksá</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">ksá</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Interrogative Adverb (Modal)
- **Meaning:** How? / In what manner?

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ksɑ/

### Etymology

Derived from the question prefix **k-** + **[[zá (particle)|zá]]** (With/Comitative). _Phonotactic Note:_ The voiced `/z/` is devoiced to `/s/` due to the preceding voiceless `/k/` (`k`+`zá` → **ksá**).

### Example sentence

**John ksá shěsonů?** _How does John read?_ (e.g., By what method/tool).
