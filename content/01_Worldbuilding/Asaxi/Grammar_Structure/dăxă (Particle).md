# dăxă ([[33_Subjunctive & Desiderative Moods]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">dăxă</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Desiderative Particle (Sentence Final)
- **Function:** **Optative Marker** (Positive Wish)
- **Meaning:** "Hopefully", "I wish", "Desirably".

### Pronunciation

IPA: /daɪɦaɪ/

### Example sentence

**To wo gajýnnshá niŕa dăxă.** _I wish I were a were-hyena._

### Etymology

**[[dăshá (noun)|dă]]** (Elephant) + **[[xa- (Relational Locative Prefix)|xa/xă]]** (Above/High). _Lit:_ "High Elephant." (Cultural association of the Elephant with height, greatness, and desire).

### Synonyms

x

### Antonyms

- **pùxă** (I hope not).
