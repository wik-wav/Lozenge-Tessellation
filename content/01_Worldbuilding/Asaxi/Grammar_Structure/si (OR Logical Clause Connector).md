# si ([[18_Conjunctions & Clause Linking]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">si</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Logical Clause Connector
- **Function:** **Disjunctive** (Alternative)
- **Meaning:** "Or".

### Syntax

Placed at the **end** of the first clause.

- **Structure:** `[Clause A] si, [Clause B]`

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɕi/

### Example sentence

**John pashěsonů si, paxoxo?** _Will John read, or will he leave?_
