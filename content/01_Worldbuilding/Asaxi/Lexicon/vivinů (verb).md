# vivinů ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_ů (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">vivinů</span>

### Transitivity / Valency

**Intransitive** Does not take an object.

### Lexical Aspect

**Activity:** The ongoing process of being alive.

### Grammatical Note

Derived from the noun **[[vivi (noun) - as in family of plants|vivi]]** (Grass/Life) + **[[06A_-n- (Morphological Infix)|-n-]]** (Performance Infix) + **[[ů (verb)|-ů]]** (Universal Verbalizer).

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Existence]] / [[Smntc_Field The Body]]

### Pronunciation

IPA: /vivinuu̯/

### Translations

- English: to live, to be alive, to thrive
- Polish: żyć

### Usage Note

**Contrast with `vanů`:**

- **vivinů:** Use for biological state. "I live (I am not dead)."
- **vanů:** Use for housing. "I live in (inhabit) a block."

### Example sentences

**To wo vivinů.** _I live._ / _I am alive._

**To kjèpo vivinů.** _The tree lives._

**To wo ŕo vanů, dzè vivinů.** _I live in a block, but I am alive._
