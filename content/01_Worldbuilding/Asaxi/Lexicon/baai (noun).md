### Noun class (warm / cold)

- Reasoning: the pain-bearing flank of a living body; *ai* is felt, internal.

### Cultural Note — The Pain-Flank
The right is named **"the pain-flank"**: the side that aches, the side where old injuries live and burdens are carried. Its counterpart **[[bafwă (noun)|bafwă]]** (left) is "the painless flank." In Asaxi thought the body is asymmetrical in suffering — one side bears, the other is spared — and the lexicon encodes it. *(Cultural doctrine to be expanded — burdens are carried on the baai; offerings made from the bafwă, etc.)*
