# kő ([[01_Asaxi Nouns (List)|01_Asaxi Nouns (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">kő</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Noun class:** Warm
    - _Reasoning:_ Something with understanding must be animate; implies a living consciousness or heart.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /koɪ/

### Semantic field

[[Smntc_Field Emotion]]
[[Smntc_Field Perception]]

### Translations

- **English:** understanding, empathy, comprehension, affection, feelings (romantic/platonic)
- **Polish:** zrozumienie, empatia, uczucie

### Usage Note

Distinct from **[[cőcő (noun)|cőcő]]** (Understanding/Grasp).
* **cőcő:** "I get you." (Cognitive/Logic).
* **kő:** "I feel you." (Emotional/Vibration).

### Example sentence

**To kő dănă xiŕa.**
_The empathy is huge._

### Alternative Forms

- koi

### Etymology

Japanese 恋.

### Synonyms

- **cè** (To know - intellectual comprehension).
- **gavi** (Goodness/Green - positive state).

### Antonyms

- **nákő** (Misunderstanding / Alienation).

### Derived terms

- **kőnů** (verb) - To understand / To empathize.
- **nákőchů** (verb) - To feel misunderstood.
