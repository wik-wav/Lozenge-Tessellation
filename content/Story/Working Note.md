Arrangement 1
characters:
Kjè, Lem, Jý, Bill, tall and muscular animals of the waterhole

events: Kjè refuses Jý's offer to enter the pool, walking into a baobab tree while lost in thought. After recovering from the impact, Kjè discovers his companion and the bathers have vanished, and he wanders alone while perceiving the world around him as growing larger. 
Exhausted by nightfall, he rests on a rock, records himself singing, and is approached by a smoking shoebill stork named Bill. The two converse and listen to the recording until the audio distorts due to the device's low battery
