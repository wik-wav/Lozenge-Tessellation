# mëjonů ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_ů (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">mëjonů</span>

### Transitivity / Valency

**Intransitive** / **Transitive** (To eye something).

### Lexical Aspect

**Activity:** Continuous usage of the eyes.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /meɪꞲonuu̯/

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Behaviour]]

### Grammatical Note

Derived from **[[mëjo (noun)|mëjo]]** (Eye) + **-n-** + **-ů**.

### Meaning

- "To look", "To use one's eyes", "To gaze".
- Distinct from **ijo** (Spot/See - Instant) and **năjo** (Observe - Iterative). `mëjonů` implies the physical act of looking.

### Example Sentence

**To ko zèmëjonů.**
_She looked._
