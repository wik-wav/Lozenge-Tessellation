### Cultural note
The **sháŕo** is the savanna-style **waterhole** — the communal watering hole where creatures (`shá`) of every kind gather to drink. It is a culturally central place in Asaxi life: a site of meeting, truce, and gathering, and a recurring image in idiom and story (cf. [[sháŕo ma (Idiom)|sháŕo ma]]). This cultural specificity sets it apart from [[fgăŕo (noun)|fgăŕo]], which is merely a generic lake.
