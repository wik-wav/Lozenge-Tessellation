# pănů ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_ů (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">pănů</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">pănů</span>
### Transitivity / Valency
**Intransitive** (Motion) or **Transitive** (Spatial State)

### Lexical Aspect
- **Durative (Activity)** — unfolds over time.
- **Aspect markers**: **na-** "do repeatedly" · **tå-** "do once" · **ni-** "begin to" · **chå-** "do fully".

### Semantic Field
[[Smntc_Field Motion]]

### Pronunciation
IPA: /paɪnuu̯/

### Translations
- English: to go outside, to exit, to leave (a container)
- Polish: wychodzić, wyjść na zewnątrz

### Example sentence
**To wo kamm pănů.**
_I am outside the building._ (State) / _I exit the building._ (Action)

### Alternative Forms
Null

### Etymology
**pă-** (Outside) + **ů**. Forward tongue movement in the mouth on pă.

### Synonyms
- **[[xoxo (verb)|xoxo]]** (To depart/leave a location).

### Antonyms
- **[[vanů (Spatial Verb)|vanů]]** (To enter / To be inside).

### Root Noun
Null

### Derived terms
- **pănůshá** (agent noun) *(potential)*

### Grammatical Note
Derived from the Relational Prefix **[[pă- (Relational Locative Prefix)|pă-]]** (Outside) + **-n-** + **[[ů (verb)|ů]]**.
