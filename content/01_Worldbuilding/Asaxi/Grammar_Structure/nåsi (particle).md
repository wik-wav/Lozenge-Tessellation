### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Frequency Particle / Adverb
- **Function:** **Negative Frequency**
- **Meaning:** "Never", "at no time".

### Logic & Etymology

**[[nå (particle)]]** (Now) + **[[siŕo (noun)|si]]** (Root of _siŕo_ - The Great Unknown/Void).

- _Lit:_ "Time-void" / "Now-unknown."
- _Logic:_ The current moment dissolves into nothingness; it has no place in time.

### Syntax

Placed at the **end** of the sentence.

- _Negation Concord:_ It does **not** strictly require the negative verb suffix (`ná`), as `nåsi` carries inherent negative force, but using `ná` adds emphasis.

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** to wo shěsonů nåsi. i never read.
- **English:** To wo shěsonůná nåsi. I (definitely) never read.
