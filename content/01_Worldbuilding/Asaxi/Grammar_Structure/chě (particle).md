### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Conditional Particle
- **Function:** **Hypothetical**
- **Meaning:** "If".

### Syntax

Placed at the **end** of the conditional clause.

- **Structure:** `[Condition] chě, [Result].`

### Pronunciation

IPA: /tʃɚ/

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** topù chě, onă o-jýnnshá chỏnů.
- **English:** If I drop (it), this hyena will eat (it).
