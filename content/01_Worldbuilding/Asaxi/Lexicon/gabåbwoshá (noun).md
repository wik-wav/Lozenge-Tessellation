### Usage Note
Describes a creature possessing both high muscle mass (`båbå`) and significant body fat (`bwo`). It implies functional, bulky strength ("Bear Mode"), distinct from the lean/defined strength of `gabåbåshá` (Bodybuilder) or the pure fatness of `gabwoshá`.

**Animal Representatives:**

1.  **[Bear]** (The archetype of muscle+fat).
2.  **[Hippo]** (Deceptively fat but immensely strong).
