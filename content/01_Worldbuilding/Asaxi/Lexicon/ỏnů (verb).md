# ỏnů ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_ů (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">ỏnů</span>

### Transitivity / Valency

**Transitive** (Spatial State)
Takes the location/container as the object.

### Lexical Aspect

**State:** The condition of being central.

### Grammatical Note

Derived from **[[ỏ- (Relational Locative Prefix)|ỏ-]]** (Middle) + **-n-** + **[[ů (verb)|ů]]**.

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field The Body]]

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ou̯nuu̯/

### Translations

- English: to be in the middle of, to center, to bisect
- Polish: być w środku, pośrodku

### Example sentence

**To shěso tobo ỏnů.**
_The book is in the middle of the table._

**Toonýj shěso no-gă ỏnů.** 
_The book is around there._

### Etymology

**ỏ-** (Middle) + **ů** (Act/Be).

### Synonyms

- **[[vanů (Spatial Verb)]]** (To be inside - broader scope).

### Antonyms

- **[[pănů (verb)|pănů]]** (To be outside / on the edge).
