# eśnå ([[01_Asaxi Nouns (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">eśnå</span>

### Noun class (warm / cold)

**Warm**

### Pronunciation

IPA: /eɕnau/

### Semantic field

[[Smntc_Field Emotion]] / [[Smntc_Field Behaviour]]

### Translations

- English: grin, smirk
- Polish: uśmiech (szeroki/złośliwy), grymas

### Usage Note

Implies a wider, perhaps more mischievous, self-assured or forced expression than `exå`.

### Example sentence

**To xő eśnånů.**
_He is grinning._

### Etymology

**Mimetic:** The physical action of saying the phoneme **/ɕ/** (alveolo-palatal fricative) pulls the corners of the mouth back into a grin.

### Synonyms

- **exå** (Smile - softer).

### Antonyms

x

### Derived terms

- **eśnånů** (To grin).
