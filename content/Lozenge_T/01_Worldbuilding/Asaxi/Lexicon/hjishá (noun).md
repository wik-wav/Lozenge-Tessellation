### Translations

- English: damselfly

### Usage Note (Metaphor)

The damselfly represents the archetype of the **hjină** personality: delicate, quiet, keeping to itself, and easily startled by intrusion.

### Example sentence

**To hjishá nanåsháŕo xiŕa.**
_The damselfly is often at the pool._

### Etymology

**[[hji (noun)|hji]]** (Slightness/Introversion) + **[[shá (noun)|shá]]** (Creature).
*Lit:* "The Slight/Introverted Creature."
