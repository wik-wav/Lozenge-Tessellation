# săni ([[39_Numerals & Mathematics]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">săni</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">săni</span>
### Noun class (warm / cold)

Cold

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Number Noun (Quantifier)
- **Meaning:** "Most", "The majority", "Almost all".
- **Nuance:** Implies a large portion but explicitly less than `săsă` (All/Whole).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /saɪɲi/

### Semantic field

[[Smntc_Field Numerals]]

### Example sentence

Săni sháma jýnů ken.
Most creatures can chat.

### Alternative Forms
x

### Etymology

**[[să (Number)|să]]** (One/Whole) + **[[ni (particle)|ni]]** (Towards).
Logic: "Towards the whole" (Approaching 100%).

### Synonyms

- **[[wănani (particle)|wănani]]** (Many - less specific).

### Antonyms

- **[[hjigo (particle)|hjigo]]** (Few).

### Derived terms

- **să**-family: [[sănani (particle)|sănani]] (some), [[săsă (particle)|săsă]] (all of)
