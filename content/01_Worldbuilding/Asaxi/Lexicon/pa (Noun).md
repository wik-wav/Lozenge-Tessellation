### Noun class (warm / cold)

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Temporal Noun
- **Meaning:** The Future, the time to come.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /pa/

### Semantic field

[[Smntc_Field Time]]

### Translations

### Example sentence

**Nipa, wa pahaśù.**
