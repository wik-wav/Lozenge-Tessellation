### Grammatical function

#### As Medial Locative Prefix

This word marks a noun as being "there".

#### As an adverb

As an adverb, `no` refers to the thing or place being pointed at physically. (e.g, `No.` "(that) There (place or thing) I'm pointing at").

### Pronunciation

IPA: /no/

### Alternative Forms

nő - for when the word after this prefix starts with a vowel
