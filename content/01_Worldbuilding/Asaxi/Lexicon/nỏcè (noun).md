### Etymology
A blend of English **"Note"** and the Asaxi components **[[nỏ (particle)|nỏ]]** (Not) + **[[cè (verb)|cè]]** (Know).
*Lit:* "Not-Know." (Writing something down because you might not know/remember it later).
