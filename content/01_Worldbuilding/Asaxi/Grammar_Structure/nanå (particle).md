### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Frequency Particle / Adverb
- **Function:** **Frequentative Aspect**
- **Meaning:** "Often", "Frequently", "Many times".

### Logic & Etymology

**[[na- (tense) (particle)|na]]** (Iterative Prefix) + **[[nå (particle)]]** (Now)
- _Lit:_ "Re-now" / "On-now."
- _Logic:_ Represents multiple instances of "now" piled on top of each other.

### Syntax

Placed at the **end** of the sentence or clause.
- **Structure:** `[Action] nanå.`

### Example sentence

**To wo shěsonů nanå.** _I read often._
