### Grammatical function

- **Part of Speech:** **Temporal Conjunction** / Adverbial
- **Function:** Connects two events where one defines the specific temporal limit of the other.
- **Meaning:** "By the time that...", "Until the specific moment when...", "Pending the deadline that..."

### Pronunciation

IPA: /mau̯ɲiɦau̯kam/ (Based on `å` = /au/)

### Usage Note

Used to introduce a clause that sets a **definite deadline** or a specific termination point for the main action. It is often paired with the **Future Perfect (`pazè-`)** in the following clause.

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** ko pazèshěsonů måniåkam, xő pazèxoxo.
- **English:** By the time (moment) she will have read, he will have departed.
- **Polish:** x

### Alternative Forms
- **måni åkam** (Analytic / Spoken slowly for emphasis).

### Etymology

A fusion of the compound particle **måni** (Until - from `måmåni`) + **-x-** (Epenthetic bridge) + **[[åkamm (noun)|åkamm (noun) - moment]]** (Specific moment/Time-structure). Literal: Until-the-moment.
