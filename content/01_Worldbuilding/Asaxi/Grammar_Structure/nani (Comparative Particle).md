# nani ([[29_Degree & Comparison]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">nani</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">nani</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Comparative Coordinator
- **Function:** **Superiority** ("More X than...")
- **Meaning:** Indicates that the first noun possesses a higher degree of the quality than the second.

### Syntax

Must be paired with the complement **[[izo (Relational Particle)|izo]]** (From/Than).

- **Structure:** `Nani [Noun A] izo [Noun B]...`

### Pronunciation

IPA: /naɲi/

### Etymology

**[[na- (Relational Locative Prefix)|na]]** (Top) + **[[ni (Relational Particle)|ni]]** (Towards). _Lit:_ "Towards-Top."

### Example sentence

**Nani John izo Tom apa dănă chỏnů.** _John munches on apples more heavily than Tom._
