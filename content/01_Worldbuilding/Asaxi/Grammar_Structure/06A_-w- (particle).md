# -w- ([[06_Verbs in Asaxi]])

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Morphological Infix (Bridge)
- **Function:** **Semblance Mode**
- **Meaning:** To act like, to mimic, to behave in the manner of.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /w/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">w</span>
- **Pattern:** `none`
- **Class:** `atonal`

### Example sentence

to john apowů. John acts like an apple. (He is being passive/crisp/etc.).

### Etymology

Linked to the warm pronouns (`wo`, `wa`) implying identity or self.
