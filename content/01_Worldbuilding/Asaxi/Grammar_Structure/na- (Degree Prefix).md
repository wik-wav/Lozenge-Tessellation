# na- ([[29_Degree & Comparison]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">na-</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">na-</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Comparative Prefix
- **Function:** **Superiority** ("More")
- **Meaning:** Indicates a higher degree of the quality compared to a standard.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /na/

### Etymology

Derived from **[[na- (Relational Locative Prefix)|na-]]** (On/Top/Stacking).

### Example sentence

**To John izo Tom nashánă [judging]nů.** _John judges **more wisely** (humanely) than Tom._

- _Adverb:_ `shánă` (Person-like/Wise)
- _Verbal Mode:_ `-n-` (Performance).
