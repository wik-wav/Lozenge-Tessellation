# -zh- ([[06_Verbs in Asaxi]])

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Morphological Infix (Bridge)
- **Function:** **Gustatory Mode**
- **Meaning:** To taste like.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ʑ/

### Example sentence

toonă topo apozhů. This rain tastes like apples.

### Etymology
