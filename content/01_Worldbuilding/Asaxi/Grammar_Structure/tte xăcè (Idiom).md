# tte xăcè ([[45_Idioms & Fixed Expressions]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">tte xăcè</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">tte xăcè</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Epistemic expression (Quotative + cognition verb).
- **Meaning:** Flags the preceding clause as **certain** — "I am sure that..."

### Usage Note

The positive counterpart of `tte pùŕima` / `tte ŕima`; asserts full confidence in the clause.

### Structure

`[Clause] tte xăcè`

### Pronunciation

IPA: /tːə ɦaɪt͡s̪ə/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">t · te | xă · cè</span>
- **Pattern:** `L · L | H · H`
- **Class:** `phrase`

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** to owao pxỏnýj xiŕa tte xăcè.
- **English:** I am sure that the Earth is round.
- **Polish:** x

### Etymology

**[[tte (particle)|tte]]** (quotative) + **[[xăcè (verb)|xăcè]]** (to be sure, to know for a fact).
