# ono- ([[07_Describing Location in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">ono-</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">ono-</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Attainable Locative Prefix
- **Function:** **Attainable Locative**
- **Meaning:** "Somewhere (reachable)", "A specific place (not here but accessible)", "Within range".

### Logic & Etymology

**[[o (proximal locative) (particle)|o]]** (Here) + **[[no (medial locative) (particle)|no]]** (There).
* *Lit:* "Here-There."
* *Logic:* A location that is defined by the path between "here" and "there." It is not unknown (`gă`), nor is it far away (`ko`). It is a "somewhere" that can be reached.

### Syntax

Attaches to the front of the noun.
* **Structure:** `ono-[Noun]`

### Pronunciation
IPA: /ono/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">o · no</span>
- **Pattern:** `L · L`
- **Class:** `atonal`

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** toonă ono-kem.ma găxănă xiŕa.
- **English:** The attainable possibilities are infinite.
- **Polish:** x
