### Etymology
Derived from the noun **[[fno (noun)|fno (noun)]] (eye floater, vitreous opacity)** + the verbal suffix **-nů**. The logic follows that just as eye floaters never stop moving when one tries to focus on them, this verb describes the state of moving.

### Definition
To move or drift; general word for movement. Unlike **[[aśù (verb)|aśù (verb) - walk on foot, ambulate]]**, or [[haśù (verb)|haśù (verb) - to run]], **fnonů** can be used to refer to any kind of movement.
