# zá ([[15_NPCP and The Agglutinative Block|Relational Particle]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">zá</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Particle type:** [[15_NPCP and The Agglutinative Block|Relational Particle (NPCP)]]
- **Function:** **Comitative** Case Marker
- **Meaning:** Indicates accompaniment or association ("With", "Accompanied by", "Together with").

### Pronunciation

IPA: /zɑ/ (Based on phoneme `á` = /ɑ/)

### Example sentence

1. **Standard Comitative:** 
	John **zá** kjèpowa jýnů. 
	_John is talking with the trees._

### Derived terms

- **záŕa** (Predicative Fusion): The result of fusing `zá` + `xiŕa`. Means "To be with" or "To accompany".

- **názáŕa** (Negative Predicative): Means "To not be with" or "Unaccompanied by" (Suggested based on Negative Stacking rules).
