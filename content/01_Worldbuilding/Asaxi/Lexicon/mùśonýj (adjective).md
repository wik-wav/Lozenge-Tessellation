### Etymology
The **aura/simile** colour of [[mùśo (noun)|mùśo]] (mould): mùśo + **-nýj**. It is the **cold other-class equivalent** of [[vènă (adjective)|vènă]] — the same hue at the opposite emotional temperature, but built from a genuinely **cold** object so that the suffix obeys **Source Agreement** (warmth follows the source noun, never a flipped suffix).
