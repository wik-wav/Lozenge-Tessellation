### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Reflexive Pronoun
- **Function:** **Self-Reference** (3rd Person Plural Male)
- **Meaning:** "Themselves" (male group).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɲiɦa/

### Example sentence

**To xa nixa ijo.** _They (men) see themselves._
