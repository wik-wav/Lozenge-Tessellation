# nivå ([[18_Conjunctions & Clause Linking]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">nivå</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">nivå</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Temporal Connector
- **Function:** **Durative**
- **Meaning:** "While", "During".

### Syntax

Placed at the **end** of the durative clause.

- **Structure:** `[Event A] nivå, [Event B].`

### Etymology

**[[ni (Relational Particle)|ni]]** (To/Into) + **[[vå (WHEN Temporal Connector)|vå]]** (When). _Lit:_ "Into the when."

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɲivau̯/

### Example sentence

**Aśù nivå, apo chỏnů.** _While walking, I eat an apple._
