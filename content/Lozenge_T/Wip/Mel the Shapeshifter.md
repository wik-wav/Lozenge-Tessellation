#### Knowns:

- Physical traits: Albino. Weight and height vary. Seems to prefer large shapes.  
    Always albino. Has a fur pattern on their neck that resembles a lunar eclipse. Life expectancy unknown.
- Shapeshifter.  
    forms encountered so far: albino lioness, numbat.
- Affect: Keeps things simple.
- Loves fractals, especially in nature. Thinks of herself as one of them, or perhaps a function in a computer simulation.
- She changes slowly, over time, into unexpected forms. Optimistic about her future, she is happy not knowing what she’ll be next.
- Seeks out Irregular Patterns.
- Believes she came from Melotte 22 (Pleiades);  
    has the number 6383 written on her tail cuff,  
    which she always wears.
- Her shapeshifting is perfectly out-of-sync with that of another creature named [Lem](Lem the shapeshifter). If Lem were a sinusoid, Mel would be the cosine. They are equal and opposite.
- Has vivid dreams, quite indiscernable from reality, which she usually forgets. Prominent themes are from her early adulthood, the time before dawn, places she visited often, set phrases, obedience, dry and still weather, animals, quiet. Very often they are like shots from a documentary; Has trouble telling apart dream from reality.
- Loves to get lost looking at dark alleys or corridors.

#### Meta:

I designed Mel primarily to be a **vessel for creative expression**. Her very nature is fluid; as a gender non-conforming shapeshifter, she isn't bound by rigid definitions of form or identity. This was intentional, as I wanted to provide a flexible foundation that encourages creators to explore a wide array of stories and themes, allowing for interpretations that might even stretch beyond the character's established lore. While some themes like, sexual content, gore, and violence would be inconsistent  
with the lore, I don’t mind it if such stories come to be (within reason, please refer to Terms of Use found in this document for specific boundaries which are to be respected)
