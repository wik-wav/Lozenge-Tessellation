# vozè ([[46_Temporal Nouns]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">vozè</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Temporal Noun (Locative)
- **Meaning:** In the immediate past, just a moment ago.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /vozə/

### Semantic field

[[Smntc_Field Time]]

### Example sentence

**Vozè, to wo zèxogă.**
_I arrived a moment ago._

### Etymology

**[[va- (Relational Locative Prefix)|va]]** (In) + **[[ozè (Tense Prefix)|ozè]]** (Immediate Past).
*Fusion:* `va` + `o` $\rightarrow$ **vo**.
