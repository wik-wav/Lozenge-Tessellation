# ăni ([[15_NPCP and The Agglutinative Block|Relational Particle]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">ăni</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Particle type:** Relational Particle (NPCP)
- **Function:** **Topical / Referential Case Marker**
- **Meaning:** "About", "Concerning", "Regarding", "On the subject of".

### Usage Notes

Marks the noun as the topic of thought, speech, or content.
* **Structure:** `ăni [Noun]`.
* **Predicative:** `ăni` + `ŕa` $\rightarrow$ **ăniŕa** (To be about / To concern).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /aɪɲi/

### Example sentence

**To wo ăni no ŕima.**
_I am thinking about you._

**To shěso jýnnshá ăniŕa.
**
_The book is about hyenas._

### Etymology

**[[ă (Relational Particle)|ă]]** (Subjective/Internal) + **[[ni (Relational Particle)|ni]]** (Towards).
*Lit:* "Towards the internal subject." (Mental direction).

### Derived terms

- **ăniŕa** (Stative: To be about).
