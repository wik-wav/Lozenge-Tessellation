# xů ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">xů</span>

### Transitivity / Valency

**Transitive**
Takes a direct object (the thing being handled or done).

### Lexical Aspect

**Activity:** General processing or manipulation.

### Grammatical Note

**[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** This functions as a primitive root verb.

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Behaviour]] / Action

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɦuu̯/

### Translations

- English: to do, to handle, to execute, to deal with
- Polish: robić, czynić, zajmować się

### Usage Note

**1. Generic Action:** Used when the specific verb is unknown or irrelevant ("What are you doing?" / "I am doing it").
**2. Contrast with `ů`:**
* **ů:** Intransitive/Copular ("To be/act as"). `John doctor ů.`
* **xů:** Transitive/Active ("To handle/manipulate"). `John [task] xů.`

### Example sentence

**To no kjo xů kè?**
_What are you doing?_

**To wo jo xů.**
_I am doing (handling) it._

### Etymology

**[[xă (Particle)|xă]]** (Force/Energy) + **[[ů (verb)|ů]]** (Act).
*Lit:* "Force-Act."

### Derived terms

- **[[06A_-x- (Morphological Infix)|-x-]]** (Interaction Mode Infix: `apoxů` "To apple-do").
