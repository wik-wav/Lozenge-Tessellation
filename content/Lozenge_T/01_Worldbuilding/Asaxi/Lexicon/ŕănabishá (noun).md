### Etymology

**[[ŕăno (noun)|ŕăna]]** (Stripes - Plural) + **[[bi (noun)|bi]]** (Line) + **[[shá (noun)|shá]]** (Creature).
*Lit:* "Stripes-Line-Creature" (referring to the side stripe common on gazelles or the blur of motion).
