# -sh- ([[06_Verbs in Asaxi]])

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Morphological Infix (Bridge)
- **Function:** **Privative Mode**
- **Meaning:** To remove, to strip, to eliminate, to clear away.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ʃ/

### Example sentence

**To wo tobo shěsoshů.** _I clear the books from the table._

### Etymology

Sound symbolism (shushing/wiping away).
