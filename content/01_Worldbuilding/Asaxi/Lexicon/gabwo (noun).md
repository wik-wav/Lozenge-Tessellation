### Usage Note (Noun vs Adjective)
While translated as "Fat," `gabwo` is grammatically a **Compound Noun** ("Fat-constitution").
* **Usage:** `tom gabwoshá xiŕa.` ("Tom is a fat-person").
* **Contrast:** **[[bwonă (adjective)]]** implies "Curvy/Beautiful."
