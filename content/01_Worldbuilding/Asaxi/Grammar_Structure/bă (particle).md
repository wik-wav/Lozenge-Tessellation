# bă ([[15_NPCP and The Agglutinative Block|Relational Particle]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">bă</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">bă</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Particle type:** [[15_NPCP and The Agglutinative Block|Relational Particle (NPCP)]]
- **Function:** **Instrumental** / **Perlative** / **Causal** / **Temporal** Case Marker
- **Meaning:** Indicates the tool, method, path, cause, or duration associated with an action ("Using", "With", "Via", "Through", "Because of", "During").

### Usage Notes

1. **The Perlative Construction (Motion Through/Along):** Combines with locative prefixes to describe movement through space.
    - _Along:_ `bă` + `[Noun]` (Using the object as a path).
    - _Through:_ `bă` + `va-` (Inside) + [Noun] (Via the interior).
2. **Causality (Because of):** Marks the **agent** or **force** that compels an event to happen. This is the active counterpart to the predicative form `băŕa` ("to be caused by").
    - _Logic:_ "Action happens _by means of_ `[Force]`."
3. **Temporal Duration (During/Throughout):** Marks the passage _through_ a specific period of time.
    - _Logic:_ "Action happens _via_ the `[Time Period]`."
    - _Contrast:_ Unlike **måmå** (Until), which stops at the limit, **bă** implies enduring the entire duration.


### Pronunciation

IPA: /baɪ/

### Example sentences

**1. Perlative Path (Along/Via)** John **bă shosa** aśù. _John walks **along** the road._

**2. Causal Case (Because of)** John **bă topo** shěsonůná. _John does not read **because of** the rain._

**3. Temporal Duration (Throughout)** John **bă mao** shěsonů. _John reads **through** the night._

### Derived terms

- **băŕa** (Predicative Fusion): The result of fusing `bă` + `xiŕa`. Means "To be caused by" or "To be made by".
- **nábăŕa** (Negative Predicative): Means "Is not made by".
