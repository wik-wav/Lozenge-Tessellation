### Transitivity / Valency

**Transitive** (Forgetting X) or **Intransitive** (Being forgetful).

### Lexical Aspect

**Achievement:** The moment of loss.

### Grammatical Note

**[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** Compound root verb.

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Perception]] / Cognition

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɦuu̯ɦuu̯/

### Translations

- English: to forget, to lose (a memory), to slip one's mind
- Polish: zapomnieć

### Example sentence

**To wo [name] xůxů.**
_I forget the name._

### Etymology

Reduplication related to **[[xoxo (verb)|xoxo]]** (To depart).
*Logic:* "It departs-departs." (The thought leaves the mind).
