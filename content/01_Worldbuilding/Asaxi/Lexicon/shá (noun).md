# shá ([[01_Asaxi Nouns (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">shá</span>

### Noun class (warm / cold)

warm

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ʃɑ/

### Semantic field

[[Smntc_Field The Body]]

### Translations

- English: creature of land, person, someone
- Polish: istota lądowa, kreatura, osoba, ktoś, coś żywego

### Example sentence

John shá xiŕa.
John is a [[People and Creatures in the Lozenge Tessellation|Person]].

### Cultural note

There is no distinction between person and animal (creature) in Asaxi. Animals are persons. You can specify that a person is a "human being" by saying, for example:

> John _Homo Sapiens_ xiŕa.

Alternatively, you can use "[[yomåshá (noun)]]", if you must specify that John is a human.
