# xona ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">xona</span>

### Transitivity / Valency

**Ambitransitive**
* **Intransitive:** To move upward, to ascend, to rise.
* **Transitive:** To raise, to lift, to move X upward.

### Lexical Aspect

**Activity:** Continuous upward motion.

### Grammatical Note

**[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** Compound root verb.

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Motion]]

### Pronunciation
IPA: /ɦona/

### Translations

- English: to ascend, to rise, to raise, to lift, to elevate
- Polish: wznosić się, podnosić, unosić

### Example sentences

**Intransitive:**
**To jhecè xona.**
_The jet ascends._

**Transitive:**
**To wo të xona.**
_I raise (my) hand._

### Etymology

**[[xoxo (verb)|xo]]** (Move) + **[[na- (Relational Locative Prefix)|na]]** (Up/Top).
*Lit:* "Move-Up."
