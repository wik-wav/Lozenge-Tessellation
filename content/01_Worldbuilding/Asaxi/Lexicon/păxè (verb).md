# păxè ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]])

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">păxè</span>
### Transitivity / Valency
_monotransitive verb_ / _intransitive verb_
To spread (wings/arms) or to be spread out.

### Lexical Aspect
- **Durative (Activity)** — unfolds over time.
- **Aspect markers**: **na-** "do repeatedly" · **tå-** "do once" · **ni-** "begin to" · **chå-** "do fully".

### Grammatical Note
- **[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** Compound root verb.

### Semantic Field
[[Smntc_Field Motion]]

### Pronunciation
IPA: /paɪɦə/

### Translations
- English: to spread out, to unfurl, to extend (wings/arms), to open up
- Polish: rozpościerać, rozkładać (skrzydła)

### Example sentence
To txenshá txewa zèpăxè.
The angel spread (its) wings.

### Alternative Forms
x

### Etymology
**[[pă- (Relational Locative Prefix)|pă]]** (Outside) + **[[xè (noun)|xè]]** (Effort/Exertion).
Logic: "Outside-Effort" (Exerting energy to move limbs outward).

### Synonyms
- **[[em.mo (verb)|em.mo]]** (To open).

### Antonyms
- **[[chěcho' (verb)|chěcho']]** (To close).

### Derived terms
- **păxèshá** (agent noun) *(potential)*
