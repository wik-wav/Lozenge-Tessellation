## Affixes

In the Asaxi language, the definite articles are affected by ("warmth"). See [[00_Noun Classification (Gender) in Asaxi]] for detail.
Definite articles are generally omitted. They're only used for empathizing definiteness.

- The forms of the definite article:
	- For warm:
	  [[onă (Determiner)]]
	- For cold:
	  [[onýj (Determiner)]]
- The form of the Indefinite article: 
	- [[anő (Determiner)]] for warm and cold nouns
