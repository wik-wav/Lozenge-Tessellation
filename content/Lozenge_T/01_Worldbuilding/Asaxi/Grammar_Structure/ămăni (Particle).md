# ămăni ([[02_Particles in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">ămăni</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Temporal Adverb / Phrase
- **Function:** **Permissive Time**
- **Meaning:** "At your leisure", "Whenever you feel like", "When the time is right for you".

### Usage Note

Used to give the listener total freedom over the schedule. It implies "It doesn't matter to me; do it when it suits you."

### Pronunciation

IPA: /aɪmaɲi/

### Example sentence

**No ămăni shěsonů kă.**
_Please read whenever you feel like it._])]

### Etymology

**[[ămă (Particle)|ămă]]** (Right Time) + **[[ni (Relational Particle)|ni]]** (Towards).
*Lit:* "Towards the right time." (Move towards the moment that feels right to you).

### Synonyms

- **[[gămă (Particle)|gămă]]** (Whenever / At any time - more neutral/random).
