### Grammatical function
A bound root naming the glassy, vitreous element of sand. Used to derive worked-silica terms.

### Pronunciation
IPA: /sɯ/

### Alternative Forms
Related to [[hùso (noun)|hùso]] (sand).

### Antonyms
Null
