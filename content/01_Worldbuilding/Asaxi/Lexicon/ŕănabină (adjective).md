### Etymology
Derived from **[[ŕănabishá (noun)|ŕănabi(shá)]]** (Gazelle) + **-nă**.
*Reduction:* The `shá` (creature) suffix is dropped, isolating the "Stripe-Line" (`ŕănabi`) quality as the essence of speed.
