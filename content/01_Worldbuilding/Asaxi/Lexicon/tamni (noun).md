### Grammatical function
- **Type:** Noun / Adverbial
- **Meaning:** - (n.) Companionship, the state of being a pair.
    - (adv.) Together as a pair; "robienie we dwójkę."

### Usage Note
- **Social Context:** Specifically denotes "doing together" with a close, trusted partner or friend. It implies a high degree of intimacy or mutual reliance.
- **Adverbial Position:** When used as an adverb, it is placed directly before the verb and is in the adjectival form.
- **Cultural Context:** In Asaxi culture, `tamni` represents a bond that transcends simple cooperation; it is the "towardness" of two becoming a single unit of action.
