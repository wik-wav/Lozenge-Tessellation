<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">ponă</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Determiner (Specific Indefinite)
- **Agreement:** **Warm** Nouns
- **Meaning:** "A certain", "A specific (but unnamed)", "One".

### Example sentence

**Ă ponă ŕimshá zèxiŕa.**
_There was a certain lemur._

### Etymology

**po** (Certainty - likely from Polish *pewien*) + **-nă** (Warm Suffix).
