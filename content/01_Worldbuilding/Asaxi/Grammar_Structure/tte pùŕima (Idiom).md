# tte pùŕima ([[45_Idioms & Fixed Expressions]])

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Epistemic expression (Quotative + cognition verb).
- **Meaning:** Flags the preceding clause as a **doubtful** belief — "I suspect that..., I am unsure."

### Usage Note

Appended after a clause to lower its certainty: per the neutrality rule ([[16_Adjectives_Constitution vs Simile]]), a `ga-`compound fact you only suspect must be qualified with `tte pùŕima`.

### Structure

`[Clause] tte pùŕima`

### Example sentence

**To John gafůbåbå shá tte pùŕima.**
_I suspect that John is a muscleless person._
