### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Superlative Prefix
- **Function:** **Maximum Degree** ("Most")
- **Constraint:** Used only when the Adjective's source noun is **Warm**.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /naɪ/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">nă</span>
- **Pattern:** `L`
- **Class:** `atonal`

### Etymology

Duplication of the warm adjectival suffix **-nă**.

### Example sentence

#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** to wo nădănă [eating]chů.
- **English:** I feel like I am eating the most heavily (gluttonously).
- **Polish:** x

### Analysis

- **Adverb:** `dănă` (elephant-like/heavy; warm source `dă`)
- **Verbal mode:** `-ch-` (subjective feeling)
