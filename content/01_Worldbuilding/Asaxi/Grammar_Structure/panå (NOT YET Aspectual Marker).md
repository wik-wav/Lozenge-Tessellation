# panå ([[18_Conjunctions & Clause Linking]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">panå</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">panå</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Temporal Particle / Aspectual Marker
- **Function:** **Prospective State**
- **Meaning:** "Not yet".

### Syntax

Placed at the **end** of the sentence.

- **Structure:** `[Action] panå.`

### Etymology

**[[pa- (Relational Locative Prefix)|pa]]** (Front/Future) + **[[nå (NOW Aspectual Marker)|nå]]** (Now). _Lit:_ "In front of now." (The action is still in the future).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /panau̯/

### Example sentence

**To wo xoxo panå.** _I have not departed yet._

### Antonyms

- **[[hùnå (ALREADY Aspectual Marker)|hùnå]]** (Already).
- **[[nå (NOW Aspectual Marker)|nå]]** (Now / Currently).
