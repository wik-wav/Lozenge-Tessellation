# xogă ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">xogă</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">xogă</span>
### Transitivity / Valency
**Intransitive** or **Transitive** (Direct object = Destination/State).

### Lexical Aspect
- **Punctual (Achievement)** — happens in a single moment.
- **Aspect markers**: **na-** "do repeatedly" · **tå-** "do once" · **ni-** "begin to" · **chå-** "do fully" · **-ů** "make it ongoing".

### Grammatical Note
**[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** Compound root verb.

### Semantic Field
[[Smntc_Field Motion]] / [[Smntc_Field Emotion]] (Metaphorical)

### Pronunciation
IPA: /ɦoɡaɪ/

### Translations
- English: to arrive, to reach, to attain, to come to (a realization)
- Polish: przybyć, dotrzeć, osiągnąć

### Example sentence

#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** to wo vashěsokam xogă.
- **English:** Physical: I arrive at the library.
- **Polish:** x

#### Example 2

- **Asaxi:** to wo gavină xogăni.
- **English:** Abstract/emotional: I reach a good state / I arrive at wellness.
- **Polish:** x

### Alternative Forms
- **xogăni** (Fused Allative form: `xogă` + `ni`). Used to emphasize the *connection* made upon arrival.

### Etymology
**[[xoxo (verb)|xoxo]]** (Depart/Move) + **[[gă (noun)|gă]]** (Place/Target).
*Lit:* "Move-Place" or "Depart-to-Place."

### Synonyms
- x

### Antonyms
- **[[xoxo (verb)|xoxo]]** (To depart / leave).

### Derived terms
- [[maxogă (verb)|maxogă]] - to bring
- [[xogăni (verb)|xogăni]] - to arrive at

### Usage Note
Can be used for physical travel ("reach the city") or abstract states ("reach a conclusion / emotional state").
