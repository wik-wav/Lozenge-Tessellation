# cő ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">cő</span>

### Transitivity / Valency

**Transitive** (Understanding X) or **Intransitive** (I understand).

### Lexical Aspect

**State / Achievement:** The state of comprehension or the moment of grasping.

### Grammatical Note

**[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** This functions as a primitive root verb.

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Perception]] / Cognition

### Pronunciation

IPA: /t͡soɪ/

### Translations

- **English:** to understand, to comprehend, to get it, to grasp (mentally)
- **Polish:** rozumieć, pojąć

### Usage Note

Distinct from **[[cè (verb)|cè]]** (To know).
* **cè:** Possession of facts/data.
* **cő:** Grasping the logic or meaning.

### Example sentence

**To wo ő cő.**
_I understand (it) like this._ (My understanding follows this manner).

**To wo no cő.**
_I understand you._

### Etymology

Derived from **[[cè (verb)|cè]]** (To know).
*Logic:* Vowel shift to indicate depth or internal processing.

### Synonyms

- **[Grasp/Realize]** (**[[ăijo (verb)|ăijo]]** is close, but emphasizes the *moment* of epiphany).
