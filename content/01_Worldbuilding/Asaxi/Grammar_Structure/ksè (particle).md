# ksè ([[25_Interrogative Pronouns (Wh-Words)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">ksè</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">ksè</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Interrogative Adverb (Causal)
- **Meaning:** Why?

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ksə/

### Etymology

Derived from the question prefix **k-** + **[[sèwo (particle)|sè]]** (Root of _sèwo_ - Because).

### Example sentence

**Ko ksè xoxo?** _Why does she depart?_
