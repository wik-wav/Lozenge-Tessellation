# cèná ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">cèná</span>

### Transitivity / Valency

**Monotransitive**
Takes an object (the fact/person unknown).

### Lexical Aspect

**State:** Lack of knowledge.

### Grammatical Note

Strictly speaking, this is the negative form of **[[cè (verb)|cè]]**, but it is lexicalized as a distinct concept ("To be ignorant of") rather than just a grammatical negation.

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Perception]] / Cognition

### Pronunciation

IPA: /t͡sənɑ/

### Translations

- English: to not know, to be ignorant of, to be unaware
- Polish: nie wiedzieć, nie znać

### Example sentence

**To wo no cèná.**
_I don't know you._

### Etymology

**[[cè (verb)|cè]]** (Know) + **[[ná (Particle)|-ná]]** (Not).

### Antonyms

- **[[cè (verb)|cè]]** (To know).
