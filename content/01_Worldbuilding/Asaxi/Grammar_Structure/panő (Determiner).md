# panő ([[05_Determiners in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">panő</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Determiner (Specific Indefinite)
- **Agreement:** **Neutral / Indefinite**
- **Meaning:** "A certain one", "Some specific thing".

### Example sentence

**To wo panő shěso jå.**
_I want a certain book._

### Etymology

**po** (Certainty) + **[[anő (Determiner)|anő]]** (Indefinite).
*Reduction:* `po` + `anő` -> **panő**.
