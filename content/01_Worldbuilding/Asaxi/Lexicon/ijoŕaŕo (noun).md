### Translations
- English: sanctuary, meditation hall, sanctuary, dream-place

### Usage Note

Activities in an ijoŕaŕo must be low-energy: reading, writing, poetry, sleep, and meditation.

### Example sentence

**Wo vajoŕaŕo shěsonů.**
_I read in the vajoŕaŕo (dream-place)._

**[[ijoŕo (noun)|ijoŕa]]** (Dreams - Plural of `ijoŕo`) + **[[ŕo (Suffix)|ŕo]]** (Place).
*Lit:* "Place of Dreams" or "Place of Visions."
