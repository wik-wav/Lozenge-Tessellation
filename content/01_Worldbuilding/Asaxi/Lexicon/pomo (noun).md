### Noun class (warm / cold)
**Warm**
- Reasoning: A living bird — animate, hence **warm**; soft and grey-feathered.

### Pronunciation
IPA: /pomo/

### Semantic Field
[[Smntc_Field Animals]]

### Translations
- English: dove, pigeon
- Polish: gołąb

### Example sentence
**To pomo kjè nanů.**
_The dove is on the tree._

### Alternative Forms
Null

### Etymology
An onomatopoeic root echoing a dove's soft coo (*po-mo*); bright warm vowels match its animacy. The full creature may also take the **-shá** suffix (*pomoshá*).
