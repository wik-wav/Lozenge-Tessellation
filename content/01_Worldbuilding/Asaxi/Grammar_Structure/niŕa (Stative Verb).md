# niŕa ([[21_Existential Logic & The Validity System|Stative Verb]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">niŕa</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">niŕa</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Stative Verb
- **Function:** **Allative State**
- **Meaning:** "To become", "To turn into", "To lead to", "To be destined for".

### Logic

**[[ni (Relational Particle)|ni]]** (Towards) + **[[xiŕa (Particle)|-ŕa]]** (Validity/Fact).
* *Lit:* "To exist-towards."
* *Nuance:* Unlike `ůků` (Transform/Morph), `niŕa` implies a natural progression or destiny. "The boy becomes a man" (`niŕa`).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɲiɾa/

### Example sentence

**To wa jýnma paniŕa.**
_We will become hyenas._

### Antonyms

- **nániŕa** (To not become / To avoid becoming).

### Derived terms

- **nániŕa** - to not become (negated form; see [[ná (Particle)]])
