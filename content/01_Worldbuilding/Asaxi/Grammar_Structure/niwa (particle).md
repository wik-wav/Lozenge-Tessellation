### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Reflexive Pronoun
- **Function:** **Self-Reference** (1st Person Plural)
- **Meaning:** "Ourselves".

### Pronunciation

### Example sentence

**To wa niwa ijo.** _We see ourselves._
