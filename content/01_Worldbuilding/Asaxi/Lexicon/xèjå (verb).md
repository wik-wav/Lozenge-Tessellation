### Transitivity / Valency
**Monotransitive**
Takes an object (the thing being attempted).

### Lexical Aspect
- **Durative (Activity)** — unfolds over time.
- **Aspect markers**: **na-** "do repeatedly" · **tå-** "do once" · **ni-** "begin to" · **chå-** "do fully".

### Grammatical Note
**[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** This belongs to the closed class of primitive verbs.

[[Smntc_Field Behaviour]]

### Pronunciation
IPA: /ɦəjau̯/

### Translations
- English: to try, to attempt, to exert effort
- Polish: próbować, starać się

### Example sentence
**To wo \[task\] xèjå.**
_I attempt the task._

### Etymology
**[[xè (noun)|xè]]** (Sound of exhale during exertion) + **[[jå (verb)|jå]]** (To want).
*Lit:* To want with exertion.
