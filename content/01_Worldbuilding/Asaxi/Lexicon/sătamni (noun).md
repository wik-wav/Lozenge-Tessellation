### Grammatical function
- **Type:** Noun / Adverbial / Adjective
- **Meaning:** - (n.) Unison, unanimity, the state of a whole group acting as one.
    - (adv./adj.) Together as a whole, in unison, unanimously.

### Usage Note
- **Social Context:** Extends the intimacy of `tamni` (a pair) to the entire group. It describes a state where individual boundaries blur into a single collective action or thought.
- **Adverbial Form:** The form **sătamnină** is used when describing the manner of an action (how everyone did something).
- **Cultural Context:** In Asaxi philosophy, `sătamni` is the ideal state of social harmony, where the "total extent" (_săsă_) of the people moves with the singular direction (_ni_) of a trusted pair.
