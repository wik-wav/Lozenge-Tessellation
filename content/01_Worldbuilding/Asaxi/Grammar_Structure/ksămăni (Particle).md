# ksămăni ([[02_Particles in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">ksămăni</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">ksămăni</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Particle type:** Free-choice manner particle.
- **Function:** Marks the manner of an action as **unrestricted** — "in whatever way, however one likes."
- **Meaning:** however; any way one likes; in whatever manner.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ksaɪmaɪni/

### Etymology

The indefinite **ks-** stem (cf. the interrogatives [[ksá (Interrogative Pronoun)|ksá]] / [[ksè (Interrogative Pronoun)|ksè]]) + **măni** (manner): "by whatever manner."

### Example sentence

**Ksămăni xů.**
_Do it however you like._
