### Pronunciation

IPA: /t̪eɪbi/

### Semantic field

[[Smntc_Field The Body]]

### Translations

- English: arm, upper limb
- Polish: ramię, ręka (cała)

### Example sentence

**To tëbi dănă xiŕa.**
_(His) arm is huge._

### Etymology

**[[të (noun)|të]]** (Hand) + **[[bi (noun)|bi]]** (Line).
*Lit:* "The hand-line." (The extension leading to the hand).
