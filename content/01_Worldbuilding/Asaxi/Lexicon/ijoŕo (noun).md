### Usage Note (Metaphor)
In Asaxi, dreams are viewed as **fluid**. You do not "see" or "have" a dream; you **drink** (`ŕoŕonů`) it.
* *Logic:* Sleep is a pool (waterhole) you drink from, and the visions are the water you consume.
