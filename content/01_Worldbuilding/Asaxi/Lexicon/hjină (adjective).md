# hjină ([[03_Asaxi Adjectives (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">hjină</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Qualitative Adjective (`-nă`)
- **Source Agreement:** **Warm**

### Pronunciation

IPA: /çjinaɪ/

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Emotion]] / Personality

### Translations

- English: shy, introverted, private, boundary-respecting
- Polish: nieśmiały, zamknięty w sobie

### Example sentence

**To John hjină shá xiŕa sèni, ỏbwonů nỏwă.**
_John is a private person, so he does not want to cuddle._

### Etymology

**[[hji (noun)|hji]]** (Introversion) + **-nă**.
