### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Temporal Particle / Aspectual Marker
- **Function:** **Prospective State**
- **Meaning:** "Not yet".

### Syntax

Placed at the **end** of the sentence.

- **Structure:** `[Action] panå.`

### Etymology

**[[pa- (particle)|pa]]** (Front/Future) + **[[nå (particle)|nå]]** (Now). _Lit:_ "In front of now." (The action is still in the future).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /panau̯/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">pa · nå</span>
- **Pattern:** `L · L`
- **Class:** `atonal`

#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** to wo xoxo panå.
