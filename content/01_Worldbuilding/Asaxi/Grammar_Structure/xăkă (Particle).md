# xăkă ([[02_Particles in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">xăkă</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Particle type:** [[02_Particles in Asaxi|High Binding Particle Compound]]
- **Function:** **Instructional Mood** (High Polite)
- **Meaning:** "You are requested to...", "Please (Instruction)", "Attention:".

### Usage Notes

Used for formal instructions, recipes, or authoritative directions where politeness is required but compliance is expected. It is stronger than a simple request (`kă`) but softer than a direct order (`hè`).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɦaɪkaɪ/

### Example sentence

**Kamm pănůxăkă.** _Please exit the building._ (Standard safety announcement).

### Etymology

A fusion of **[[xă (Particle)|xă]]** (Indeed/Emphasis) + **[[kă (Particle)|kă]]** (Please). _Lit:_ "Emphatic Request."
