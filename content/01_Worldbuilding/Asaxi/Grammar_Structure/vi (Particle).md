# vi ([[02_Particles in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">vi</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">vi</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Interjection / Discourse Particle
- **Function:** **Affirmation / Agreement**
- **Meaning:** "Good!", "OK!", "Will do", "It's alright", "Fine".

### Usage Note

Used as a standalone response to agree to a request or confirm a state.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /vi/

### Example sentence

**John:** "Apo chỏnůkă." (Please eat the apple.)
**Me:** "Vi!" (OK! / Will do!)

### Etymology

Shortening of **[[gavi (noun)|gavi]]** (Goodness/Green).
*Logic:* "Good!"

### Derived terms

- **vină** (Okay / Good).
