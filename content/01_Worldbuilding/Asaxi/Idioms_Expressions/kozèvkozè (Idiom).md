# kozèvkozè ([[46_Temporal Nouns]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">kozèvkozè</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Temporal Adverb / Idiom
- **Meaning:** "Once upon a time", "Long, long ago", "In the deep past".

### Usage Note

The standard opening for fairy tales and legends.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /kozəfkozə/
*Phonotactics:* `v` devoices to `/f/` before `k`.

### Etymology

**[[kozè (Noun)|kozè]]** (Distant Past) + **[[va- (Relational Locative Prefix)|v(a)-]]** (In) + **kozè**.
*Lit:* "In the distant past of the distant past."

### Synonyms

- **[[kozè (Temporal Noun)|vkozè]]** (In the distant past - standard).
