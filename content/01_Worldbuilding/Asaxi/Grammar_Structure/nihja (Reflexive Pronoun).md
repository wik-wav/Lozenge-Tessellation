# nihja ([[13_Pronouns in Asaxi]])

- - -

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Reflexive Pronoun
- **Function:** **Self-Reference** (3rd Person Plural Inanimate)
- **Meaning:** "Themselves" (things).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɲiça/

### Example sentence

**To hja nihja ijo.** _They (things) see themselves._

### Etymology

**[[ni (Relational Particle)|ni]]** (Towards) + **[[hja (Pronoun)|hja]]** (They-things).
