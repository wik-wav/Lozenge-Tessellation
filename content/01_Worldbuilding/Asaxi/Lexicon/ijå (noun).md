### Noun class (warm / cold)

**Cold**
- Reasoning: Abstract concept or visual phenomenon.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /iꞲau̯/

### Semantic field

[[Smntc_Field Perception]] / Appearance

### Translations
- English: a sight, a view, a vision, a spectacle, a scene
- Polish: widok, wizja, spektakl

### Usage Note

Refers to the specific instance of something being seen ("That was quite a sight") or the visual field itself.

### Example sentence

**To onă o-wao gavină ijå xiŕa.**
_This world is a good sight (beautiful view)._

*Lit:* "A seeing-instance" or "A unit of sight."
