- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">ăjawa</span>

### Noun class (warm / cold)

**Warm**
- Reasoning: High-energy social event/sound.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /aɪjawa/

### Semantic field

[[Smntc_Field Behaviour]]
[[Smntc_Field Conflict]]

### Translations

- English: war cry, battle cry, rallying cry, roar (of a crowd)
- Polish: okrzyk bojowy

### Usage Note

Implies a collective or group shout (even if done by one person, it invokes the group).

### Example sentence

**To wo ăjawa minů.**
_I hear the war cry._

### Etymology

**[[ăja (verb)|ăja]]** (Shout) + **[[wa (Pronoun)|wa]]** (We/Us).
*Lit:* "The Shout of Us" or "The Collective Shout."
