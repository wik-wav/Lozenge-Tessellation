# pù- ([[29_Degree & Comparison]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">pù-</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">pù-</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Comparative Prefix
- **Function:** **Inferiority** ("Less")
- **Meaning:** Indicates a lower degree of the quality compared to a standard.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /pɯ/

### Etymology

Derived from **[[pù- (Relational Locative Prefix)|pù-]]** (Below).

### Example sentence

**To [speaker] pùjýnă [shouting]sů.** _The speaker shouts **less loudly** (hyena-like)._

- _Adverb:_ `jýnă` (Hyena-like/Loud)
- _Verbal Mode:_ `-s-` (Auditory/Sounding).
