# -k- ([[06_Verbs in Asaxi]])

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Morphological Infix (Bridge)
- **Function:** **Transformative Mode**
- **Meaning:** To turn into, to transform (something/someone) into.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /k/

### Example sentence

to [Wizard] john gajýnnků. The wizard turns John into a were-hyena.
