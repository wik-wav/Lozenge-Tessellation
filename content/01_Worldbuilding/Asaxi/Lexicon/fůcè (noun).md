<span class="asaxi-script">fůcè</span>

### Noun class (warm / cold)

**Cold**
- Reasoning: Abstract concept of ignorance or missing data.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /fuu̯t͡sə/

### Semantic field

[[Smntc_Field Perception]] / Cognition
[[Smntc_Field Virtues & Failings]]

### Translations

- English: the unknown, ignorance, lack of knowledge
- Polish: nieznane, niewiedza

### Usage Note

Distinct from **[[siŕo (noun)|siŕo]]** (The Great Void / The Unknowable).
* **siŕo:** That which *cannot* be known (Cosmic).
* **fůcè:** That which is *not yet* known (Data gap).

### Example sentence

**To wo fůcè ůchů.**
_I feel ignorance (I feel I do not know)._

### Etymology

**[[fů (root word)|fů]]** (Lack) + **[[cè (verb)|cè]]** (Know).
*Lit:* "Know-lessness."
