### Example sentence

### Alternative Forms

### Etymology
**Mimetic:** The physical action of saying the phoneme **/ɕ/** (alveolo-palatal fricative) pulls the corners of the mouth back into a grin.

### Synonyms
- **exå** (Smile - softer).

### Antonyms

### Derived terms

### Usage Note
Implies a wider, perhaps more mischievous, self-assured or forced expression than `exå`.
