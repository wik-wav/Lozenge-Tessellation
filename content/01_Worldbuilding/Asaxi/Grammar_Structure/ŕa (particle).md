# ŕa ([[18_Conjunctions & Clause Linking]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">ŕa</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Logical Clause Connector
- **Function:** **Coordinative** (Clause Linking)
- **Meaning:** "And" (joining full sentences/predicates).
- **Note:** Use **[[ja (particle)|ja]]** for joining nouns. Use **ŕa** for joining actions.

### Syntax

Placed at the **end** of the first clause.

- **Structure:** `[Clause A] ŕa, [Clause B]`

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɾa/

### Example sentence

**John shěsonů ŕa, Mary aśù.** _John reads and Mary walks._
