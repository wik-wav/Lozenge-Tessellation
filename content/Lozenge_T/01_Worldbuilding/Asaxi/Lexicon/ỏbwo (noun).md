### Noun class (warm / cold)

**Warm**

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ou̯bʷo/

### Semantic field

[[Smntc_Field The Body]]

### Translations

- English: belly, paunch, stomach (fat), gut
- Polish: brzuch (tłusty), kałdun

### Usage Note

Refers specifically to a soft or large belly, distinct from the anatomical organ "stomach" (internal).

### Example sentence

**To wo gadăỏbwo ma.**
_I have a giant belly._

### Etymology

**[[ỏ- (Relational Locative Prefix)|ỏ]]** (Middle) + **[[bwo (noun)|bwo]]** (Fat).
*Lit:* "Middle-fat."
