### Transitivity / Valency
**Transitive**
Takes an object (the thing being protected/cushioned).

### Lexical Aspect
- **Durative (Activity)** — unfolds over time.
- **Aspect markers**: **na-** "do repeatedly" · **tå-** "do once" · **ni-** "begin to" · **chå-** "do fully".

### Semantic Field
[[Smntc_Field Housing & Shelter]] / Safety

### Pronunciation
IPA: /ou̯bou̯nuu̯/

### Translations
- English: to cushion, to buffer, to soften (a blow), to pad
- Polish: amortyzować, łagodzić

### Example sentence
Context: Tom just fell out of a window from a high-rise building.
**Wo Tom zèxỏbỏnů.**
_I cushioned Tom (from something)._
