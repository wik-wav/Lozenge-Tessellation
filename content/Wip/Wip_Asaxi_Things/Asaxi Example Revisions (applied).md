# Example-Sentence Revisions — APPLIED

These 88 bland/ambiguous examples have been rewritten in their entries, incorporating all of your corrections: the **mutable vs immutable copula** rule (`-nů`/`ů`/`bů` for changeable states, `-ŕa`/`xiŕa` only for intrinsic facts with a specific subject), the case particles (`bă` instrumental, `zá` comitative, `då` dative, `sè` genitive, `ja` list-and), `ga-` for "made of", and the verbs `ůků`/`ůchů`/`tănů`/`xona`/`táka`/`wëè`. `kamshá` was left as human-written.
