### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Temporal Connector
- **Function:** **Durative**
- **Meaning:** "While", "During".

### Syntax

Placed at the **end** of the durative clause.

- **Structure:** `[Event A] nivå, [Event B].`

### Etymology

**[[ni (particle)|ni]]** (To/Into) + **[[vå (particle)|vå]]** (When). _Lit:_ "Into the when."

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɲivau̯/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">ni · vå</span>
- **Pattern:** `L · L`
- **Class:** `atonal`
