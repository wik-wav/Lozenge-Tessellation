# na- ([[29_Degree & Comparison]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">na-</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">na-</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Comparative Prefix
- **Function:** **Superiority** ("More")
- **Meaning:** Indicates a higher degree of the quality compared to a standard.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /na/

### Etymology

Derived from **[[na- (relational locative) (particle)|na-]]** (On/Top/Stacking).

### Example sentence

#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** to john izo tom nashánă [judging]nů.
- **English:** John judges more wisely (humanely) than Tom.
- **Polish:** x

### Analysis

- **Adverb:** `shánă` (person-like/wise)
- **Verbal mode:** `-n-` (performance)
