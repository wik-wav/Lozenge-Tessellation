# tte ([[02_Particles in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">tte</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Low Binding Particle (Clause Final)
- **Function:** **Quotative / Explanatory**
- **Meaning:** "That", "So", "It is said", "Speaking of".

### Pronunciation

IPA: /tːe/

### Example sentences

**Quotation:** **Xő xoxo tte.** _He says he is leaving._ (Lit: He leaves [quote]).

**Explanation:** **John zèshěsonů tte, gavină.** _John read, so he is good._

### Etymology

Borrowed from Japanese **って** (_tte_).
