# xèronů ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_ů (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">xèronů</span>

### Transitivity / Valency

**Intransitive**

### Lexical Aspect

**Activity:** Targeted training.

### Grammatical Note

Derived from **[[xèro (noun)|xèro]]** (Weights) + **-n-** + **-ů**.

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field The Body]] / Fitness

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɦəɹonuu̯/

### Translations

- English: to weight-train, to bulk, to lift weights (for mass)
- Polish: trenować siłowo, robić masę

### Usage Note

Synonymous with **bulking** or targeted bodybuilding, whereas `ronů` is the general act of lifting.

### Example sentence

**Wo gabwokamshá ma sèni, xèronů.**
_I have a fat body, so I weight-train._

### Etymology

"To use weights."
