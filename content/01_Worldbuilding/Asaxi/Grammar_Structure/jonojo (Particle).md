# jonojo ([[02_Particles in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">jonojo</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Adverbial Particle / Aspectual Modifier
- **Function:** **Numeric/Sequential Distributive**
- **Meaning:** "One by one", "Item by item", "In sequence."
- **Nuance:** Implies an orderly processing of distinct units.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ꞲonoꞲo/

### Etymology

**[[jo (Pronoun)|jo]]** (It) + **[[no (Proximal Locative Prefix)|no]]** (There) + **jo** (It).
*Lit:* "It... there... it." (Visualizing pointing at items in a line: "This one there, then this one").

### Example sentence

**To wo shěso shěsonů jonojo.**
_I read the books one by one._
