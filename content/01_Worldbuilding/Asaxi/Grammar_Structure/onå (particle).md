### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Temporal Particle / Adverb
- **Function:** **Eternal Aspect**
- **Meaning:** "Forever", "Eternally", "Always".

### Logic & Etymology

**[[o (noun)|o]]** (Sky) + **[[nå (particle)]]** (Now).
- _Lit:_ "Sky-now."
- _Logic:_ The sky is the ultimate constant. To exist in "Sky-time" is to exist forever.
- _Distinction:_ Distinct from the proximal prefix `o-` (Here). The phonetic weight of the noun `o` implies the celestial body, not the pointer.

### Syntax

Can function as a **Sentence-Final Particle** (Adverbial) or a **Floating Modifier** (Poetic).

### Example sentence

**To wo no jåijo onå.** _I want to see you forever._ (Lit: I want-see you sky-now).
