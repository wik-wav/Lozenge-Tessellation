### Etymology
Reciprocal frame **X-ni-X** (cf. *gőnigő*, *okonoko*, *jonojo*) on **[[dao (verb)|dao]]** (give) — „give-each-other”. The repetition is grammatical reduplication and is **not** reduced by haplology.
