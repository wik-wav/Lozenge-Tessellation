# vanå ([[18_Conjunctions & Clause Linking]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">vanå</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Temporal Particle / Aspectual Marker
- **Function:** **Persistive Aspect** ("Still")
- **Meaning:** Indicates an action that was happening before and is _still_ happening now.

### Logic & Etymology

**[[va- (Relational Locative Prefix)|va]]** (Inside) + **[[nå (NOW Aspectual Marker)]]** (Now).

- _Lit:_ "Inside-now."
- _Logic:_ The action is contained within the current moment; it has not exited or finished.
- _Symmetry:_
    - **panå** (Front-now): Not yet.
    - **hùnå** (Behind-now): Already.
    - **vanå** (Inside-now): Still.


### Syntax

Placed at the **end** of the sentence or clause.

- **Structure:** `[Action] vanå.`

### Example sentence

**To wo shěsonů vanå.** _I am still reading._

**Topo toponů vanå.** _It is still raining._
