# bi ([[29_Degree & Comparison]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">bi</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">bi</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Noun / Comparative Marker
- **Function 1:** **Noun** ("Line", "Straight", "Level").
- **Function 2:** **Comparative Coordinator** ("Equal to").
- **Function 3:** **Compound Comparative Prefix** ("As [Noun] as").
- **Meaning:** Indicates that the first noun is equal in degree to the second noun.

### Syntax

Must be paired with the complement **[[zá (particle)|zá]]** (With).

- **Structure:** `Bi [Noun A] zá [Noun B]...`

### Pronunciation

IPA: /bi/

### Etymology

Derived from the noun **[[bi (noun)|bi]]** (Line/Level). _Logic:_ "On the level of A, with B."

### Example sentence

**Bi John zá Tom apa dănă chỏnů.** _John and Tom munch on apples as much as one another._ (Lit: Equal John with Tom...).
