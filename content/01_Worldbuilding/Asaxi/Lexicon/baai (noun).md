# baai ([[01_Asaxi Nouns (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">baai</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">baai</span>

### Noun class (warm / cold)
**Warm**
- Reasoning: the pain-bearing flank of a living body; *ai* is felt, internal.

### Pronunciation
IPA: /ba.ai/ — three morae; the doubled *a* is held two full beats ([[22_Phonotactics & Euphony]]).

### Semantic Field
[[Smntc_Field Shapes & Dimensions]] / [[Smntc_Field The Body]]

### Translations
- English: the right side, the right
- Polish: prawa strona

### Example sentence
**Ni baai aśùhè, zå pjovină.**
_"Walk to the right, then straight."_

### Alternative Forms
Null

### Etymology
**ba-** (flank, beside; [[ba- (Relational Locative Prefix)]]) + **[[ai (noun)|ai]]** (pain, sorrow) — "the pain-flank."

### Synonyms
- [[ai (noun)|ai]] (related: pain)

### Antonyms
- [[bafwai (noun)|bafwai]] (the left, the painless flank)

### Derived terms
- **baaină** (right-hand, rightward) *(potential)*

### Usage Note


The right is associated with pain. Its counterpart **[[bafwai (noun)|bafwai]]** (left) is "the painless flank."
