### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Frequency Particle / Adverb
- **Function:** **Emphatic Negative Frequency**
- **Meaning:** "Absolutely never", "Certainly never", "Under no circumstances".

### Logic & Etymology

A high-binding fusion of three particles:

1. **[[ná (particle)|ná]]** (Not)
2. **[[xă (particle)|hă]]** (Indeed/Emphasis)
3. **[[nåsi (particle)|nåsi]]** (Never / Time-Void)])]

- _Logic:_ "Not-Indeed-Never" (Double negative logic usually intensifies in Asaxi: "It is a fact that I never...").

### Syntax

Placed at the **end** of the sentence.

- _Structure:_ `[Action] náxănåsi.`

### Pronunciation

IPA: /nɑɦaɪnau̯ɕi/
