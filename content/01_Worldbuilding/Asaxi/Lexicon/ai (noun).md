### Etymology
Inspired by the Dreamer's memory of **哀** (*ai*, sorrow, pathos). **Note:** spelled *a+i* — two morae /a.i/, phonologically distinct from the diphthong *ă* /aɪ/ (one mora). Contrasts with **[[tètá (noun)|tètá]]**, the bodily pain.
