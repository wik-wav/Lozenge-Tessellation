# nihè ([[02_Particles in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">nihè</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">nihè</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Particle type:** [[02_Particles in Asaxi|High Binding Particle Compound]] (Imperative)
- **Function:** **Inceptive Command**
- **Meaning:** "Start!", "Begin!", "Commence!", "Go!".

### Usage Notes

Used as a standalone command to initiate an action or event. It can also function as a prefix to a verb to command the *start* of that specific action (though `nishěsonůè` is also valid, `nihè` emphasizes the *moment* of beginning).
* **Standalone:** nihè! ("Start!").
* **Prefix:** nihèshěsonů! ("Start reading!" - Emphatic).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɲixə/
*Phonotactics:* The `h` is retained because `ni` ends in a pure vowel.

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">ni · hè</span>
- **Pattern:** `L · L`
- **Class:** `atonal`
