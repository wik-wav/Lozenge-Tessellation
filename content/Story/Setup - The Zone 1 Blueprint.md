# Act I Setup: The Zone 1 Low-Stakes Blueprint

## 1. The Psychological Paradox

* **The Desire:** A latent need for connection.
* **The Defense (The Curse):** A perceived cosmetic flaw that makes the Projection feel unworthy.

## 2. Chapter 1: The Success of Avoidance

This chapter establishes the deep NREM baseline. The physical stakes are deliberately low. The tension is purely psychological and atmospheric.

1. **The Prospect of Horror:** The Projection is preparing for or engaging in the early stages of the activity. The activity requires mutual trust and is terrifying to the Projection strictly because of the vulnerability involved.
2. **The Minor Incongruity:** An opportunity for genuine connection, communication, or vulnerability presents itself during the routine.
3. **The Evasion (Focus):** Paralyzed by their internal conflict, the Projection uses "Focus." They intentionally deaden the experience, look away, or perform a rote action to bypass the moment of vulnerability.
4. **The Hollow Victory:** The avoidance works flawlessly. The environment remains perfectly calm. There is no immediate physical danger, no REM shift, and no catastrophic failure. The activity concludes safely.
5. **The Lingering Void:** The chapter ends not with a cliff-hanger of physical danger, but with a quiet, unsettling emptiness. The Projection is physically safe, but emotionally isolated.

## 3. Chapter 2: The Delayed Cost

The physical and environmental consequences of Chapter 1's emotional suppression begin to manifest here, ramping up the tension.

1. **The Time Skip/Roadblock:** The environment presents a physical anomaly (e.g., sudden moss overgrowth, a structural shift) indicating that time behaved abnormally while the Projection was Focused in Chapter 1.
2. **The Curse Advances:** The Projection notices their cosmetic "curse" has visibly worsened. This is the delayed physical manifestation of the self-resentment generated by their evasion in the previous chapter.
