### Noun class (warm / cold)

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Event Noun
- **Meaning:** "The start", "The beginning", "The commencement", "The go-ahead".

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɲihəko/

### Example sentence

**To nihèko dănă xiŕa.**
_The beginning is huge (important)._
