# fůmă ([[02_Particles in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">fůmă</span>

### Pronunciation

**IPA:** /fuu̯maɪ/

### Lexical Classification

**Category:** Particle
**Sub-category:** Quantifier (Existential)

### Definition

A quantifier used to specify a subset containing zero members of a category. Semantic opposite of **mă** (every). While **mă** denotes a total inclusion, **fůmă** denotes absolute absence within a specific frame of reference.

### Etymology

Derived from the privative prefix **fů-** (denoting lack or the opposite) and the quantifier **mă** (every).

### Examples

- **To wo apa fůmă chỏnů**
- *I ate none of the apples*
