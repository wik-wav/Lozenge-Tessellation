# jå- ([[23_Modality & Verbal Compounding]])

---

<span class="asaxi-script">jå-</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">jå-</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Modal Prefix (Auxiliary Root)
- **Meaning:** To want to; expresses desire or intent to perform the action.

### Usage Note

- **Position:** Attaches to the front of the main verb root, following Tense and Negation prefixes.
- **Morphophonology (Bridge Rule Exception):** The vowel **å** is a diphthong (IPA /au̯/). Because it ends in a glide/semi-vowel quality, it does **not** trigger the insertion of the hiatus-breaking bridge **-x-** when attached to a vowel-initial root.
    - _Example:_ `jå` + `ijo` becomes **jåijo** (not _jåxijo_).

### Structure

`[Tense] - [Negation] - jå - [Root]`

### Pronunciation

IPA: /jau̯/

### Example sentence

**To wo zènájåijo.** _I did not want to see._
