# nikpa ([[46_Temporal Nouns]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">nikpa</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">nikpa</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Temporal Noun (Locative)
- **Meaning:** In the distant future, far ahead in time.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɲikpa/

### Semantic Field
[[Smntc_Field Time]]

### Example sentence

**Nikpa, to owao siŕo paxiŕa.**
_In the distant future, Earth will be the void._

### Etymology

**[[ni (particle)|ni]]** (Towards) + **[[kopa (noun)|kopa]]** (Distant Future).
*Reduction:* `ni` + `kopa` $\rightarrow$ **nikpa** (Vowel elision for flow).
