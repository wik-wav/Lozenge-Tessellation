# pùni ([[29_Degree & Comparison]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">pùni</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">pùni</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Comparative Coordinator
- **Function:** **Inferiority** ("Less X than...")
- **Meaning:** Indicates that the first noun possesses a lower degree of the quality than the second.

### Syntax

Must be paired with the complement **[[izo (particle)|izo]]** (From/Than).

- **Structure:** `pùni [Noun A] izo [Noun B]...`

### Pronunciation

IPA: /pɯɲi/

### Etymology

**[[pù- (relational locative) (particle)|pù]]** (Below) + **[[ni (particle)|ni]]** (Towards). _Lit:_ "Towards-Below."

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** pùni john izo tom apa dănă chỏnů.
- **English:** John munches on apples less heavily than Tom.
