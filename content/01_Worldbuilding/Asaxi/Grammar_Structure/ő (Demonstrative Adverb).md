# ő ([[02_Particles in Asaxi]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">ő</span>

### Grammatical function
- **Type:** Demonstrative Adverb
- **Function:** **Proximal Manner**
- **Meaning:** "Like this", "In this way", "Thus".

### Usage Notes
Used to demonstrate a method or style. It typically appears immediately before the verb (or imperative complex).
* **Structure:** `Ő [Verb]`.

### Pronunciation
IPA: /oɪ/

### Example sentence
**Teacher:** **Ő hèshěsonů!**
_Read like this!_ (Emphatic command).]), shěsonů (verb)]

**Student:** **To wo ő xů.**
_I do (it) like this._

### Etymology
Likely derived from **[[o (Proximal Locative Prefix)|o]]** (Here) + a manner suffix or vowel shift.
*Logic:* "The Here-Way."
