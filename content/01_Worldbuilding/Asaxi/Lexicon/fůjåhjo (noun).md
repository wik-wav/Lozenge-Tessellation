### Translations

- Polish: zgubny cel, anty-przeznaczenie

### Usage Note (The Dementing Fů)

Here, the prefix **fů-** acts not just as "Lack," but as a corruption or dementia of the concept.
* **jåhjo:** Guiding Star (True Destination).
* **fůjåhjo:** An un-guiding star. A goal that causes the subject to "forget" their true path or nature.

### Example sentence

**To onă o-gă fůjåhjo xiŕa.**
_This place is a false destination._

### Etymology

**[[fů (root word)|fů]]** (Corruption/Lack) + **[[jåhjo (noun)|jåhjo]]** (Guiding Star).
