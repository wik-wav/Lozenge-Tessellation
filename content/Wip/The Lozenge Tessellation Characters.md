This is an account of the Characters of [[The Lozenge Tessellation]]. 
# [[Lem the Shapeshifter]]

Lem is a vibrant and energetic Projection whose primary Nature is that of a Shapeshifter. They present as male with a gender non-conforming affect, often behaving flamboyantly or engaging in playful pretense. Physically, Lem's form is fluid, reflecting their Shapeshifter nature; while they seem to prefer smaller, agile mammal forms like a phascogale or marten (ranging between roughly 140cm and 170cm tall depending on the form), they have been observed shifting into larger shapes like a red kangaroo when agitated or needing strength. These shifts are often triggered by strong emotion or stress, and can sometimes be involuntary or incomplete, reflecting their internal state. Despite the transformations, certain features persist: a tail always ending in a brush tip, a light-colored heart-shaped patch of fur centered on their upper chest, bright safety-orange hair often styled in a chaotic half-buzzcut/ponytail, and a bittersweet-colored bow-tie marked with the number 3836, hinting at a mysterious connection to a minor planet and a self-concept of being partly stellar. Their fur colors are typically jasmine yellow and ivory, though patterns change with form. Lem can only shift into organic, primarily mammalian forms (real-world or mythical, but never human, plants, fungi, or mechanical constructs) and requires a template – an observed or imagined form – to shift into. This ability is also constrained by age; the range of forms available changes over time, a limitation Lem finds both troubling and meaningful.

Lem possesses a restless, high-energy personality, marked by high openness to experience, extraversion, and novelty-seeking. They are intensely curious, easily bored by stillness or monotony ("Standing still is how you get mossy"), and often act impulsively, driven by a desire to explore or understand the strange patterns of the dreamscape. They love shapes and fractals, seeing themselves as part of such patterns. This fascination drives them to seek out repeating patterns and investigate anomalies, sometimes leading them into chaotic situations. Lem communicates quickly and dynamically, often speaking fast with a rich vocabulary (in English), using interjections, and expressing excitement or frustration openly. They can be assertive, argumentative, and prone to challenging statements or perceived injustices, sometimes using surreal humor or sarcasm.

Despite their outward confidence and flamboyance, Lem exhibits significant neuroticism and anxiety. They are scared of the dark and possess a profound sensitivity to existential dread, reacting with visceral fear to perceptions of emptiness or 'nowhere'. When overwhelmed by fear or distress, their bravado can crumble, revealing a deep vulnerability and a powerful need for connection and reassurance; they might impulsively seek physical closeness (like hugging or hand-holding) as an anchor. Lem is fiercely protective of companions, reacting with immediate, instinctual defense when perceiving a threat towards them. They can also show surprising situational gentleness, moderating their usual energy to offer comfort.

Lem's perception of reality can be affected by tiredness, leading to stylized or hallucinatory experiences mirroring their vivid, nonsensical dreams (which often feature themes of childhood video games, animals, specific times like pre-midnight, and weather). The origin of Lem and the precise mechanics of their shapeshifting remain a mystery, even to Lemself, though they are intensely curious about it. However, the perceived danger of investigating their own Nature prevents deeper examination. Lem's primary motivation seems to be a quest for release or escape, perhaps pursued through their occasional role as a singer, though their skill is not perceived as anything particularly outstanding.

# [[Mel the Shapeshifter]]

Mel is a rather reserved Projection whose primary nature is that of a Shapeshifter. She present as a confident female character, preferring large forms. Her pattern of shifting is tied to that of Lem's, while being directly opposite of  it; when Lem is meagre, she turns huge and vice versa.

# Zephyr

Zephyr is a gentle and introspective Projection whose primary Nature appears to be that of a Librarian, often combined with the role of a Musician. Usually referred to as Zeph. They present as male with a soft, androgynous affect. Physically, Zephyr takes the form of a slender, anthropomorphic fennec fox, standing around 150cm tall, with pale cream and sandy fur, large characteristic ears, and gentle amber eyes. Their long, pale lavender hair is distinct from their fur, adorned with a jewelry chain with a lapis lazuli bead centered on the forehead. They typically wear layered, flowing clothes in soft fabrics, favoring asymmetrical tops (often shimmering sky blue) over mesh shirts and wide-leg charcoal trousers, with soft leather ankle wraps instead of shoes. Zephyr is almost always seen carrying or holding a carved wooden flute, which serves as both a musical instrument and a tool for emotional expression and interaction with the dreamscape's energies.

Zephyr's personality is marked by high openness, agreeableness, and introversion. They are deeply empathetic, highly sensitive (especially to sound and emotional atmospheres), and possess a strong intuition which they trust implicitly. They communicate softly and slowly, often using poetic language, metaphors, and gentle questions to explore their own feelings and understand the world around them. Their primary motivations seem to be emotional expression and creating harmony. They value connection, beauty, and meaning, often perceiving the world through feelings and resonance rather than strict logic. Zephyr embodies a quiet optimism, often acting as a subtle beacon of hope or possibility in moments of despair or confusion. Their presence can be calming, and through music or gentle observation, they sometimes highlight overlooked potentials or paths towards resolution.

Despite their quiet demeanor and inner strength, Zephyr possesses clear shortcomings. Their high sensitivity makes them prone to being overwhelmed by harsh sensory input or intense negative emotions, sometimes leading them to withdraw or become lost in melancholic introspection. While capable of empathetic courage, their natural aversion to conflict and low assertiveness can make them hesitant to act decisively in confrontational situations, relying instead on indirect methods or hoping for harmony. Their reliance on intuition and metaphorical communication, while insightful, can sometimes lead to misunderstandings or appear evasive to more direct personalities. Furthermore, their deep empathy means they internalize criticism and the suffering of others profoundly, potentially leading to periods of self-doubt or feeling burdened, even if they maintain a calm exterior. Zephyr is highly attuned to the emotional states of others, offering comfort through quiet presence, gentle words, or soothing music. They value interpersonal connection deeply and respond warmly, if shyly, to gestures of closeness and support, particularly after stressful events. Their Librarian Nature manifests in their tendency to find memories, emotions, and even time as things that can be held, echoed, or organized, like the 'feeling of home' or the 'echoes' from Arrangements.
