### Noun class (warm / cold)

**Warm**
- Reasoning: paired with [[baai (noun)|baai]]; a flank of a living body.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /bafʷaɪ/

**Formation note:** *fů* + *ai* contracts by **glide formation** — the *ů* weakens to *w* before the vowel, and *a+i* coalesces into the diphthong *ă*: `fů-ai` → **fwă**. (See [[22_Phonotactics & Euphony]].)

### Etymology

**ba-** (flank, beside) + **fů-** (privative) + **[[ai (noun)|ai]]** (pain), contracted `fů-ai` → *fwai* — "the flank without pain."

### Synonyms

- [[fůjå (noun)|fůjå]] (related: a privative state — wantlessness)

### Antonyms

- [[baai (noun)|baai]] (the right, the pain-flank)

### Derived terms

- **bafwaină** (left-hand, leftward) *(potential)*

### Usage Note

The left is **"the painless flank"** — the side that was spared, the clean side. Where **[[baai (noun)|baai]]** (right) bears the body's pain and burdens, the *bafwai* is whole. *(Cultural doctrine to be expanded alongside baai.)*
