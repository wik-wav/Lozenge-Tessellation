# -x- ([[06_Verbs in Asaxi]])

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Morphological Infix (Bridge)
- **Function:** **Interaction Mode**
- **Meaning:** To do X to (someone), to force X upon, to apply X transitively.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɦ/

### Example sentence

**To John wo apoxů.** _John apples me._ (Context: He throws an apple at me or gives me one).

Linked to **[[xă (Particle)|xă]]** (Force/Emphasis).
