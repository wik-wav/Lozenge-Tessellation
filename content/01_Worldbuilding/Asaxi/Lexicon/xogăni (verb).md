# xogăni ([[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">xogăni</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">xogăni</span>
### Transitivity / Valency
**Transitive**
Takes a direct object (the state or place reached) without needing an external preposition.

### Lexical Aspect
- **Punctual (Achievement)** — happens in a single moment.
- **Aspect markers**: **na-** "do repeatedly" · **tå-** "do once" · **ni-** "begin to" · **chå-** "do fully" · **-ů** "make it ongoing".

### Grammatical Note
**[[02_Asaxi Verbs_Root (List)]]:** Compound root verb.
* **Structure:** `[Subject] [Destination] xogăni`.

### Semantic Field
[[Smntc_Field Motion]] / [[Smntc_Field Emotion]] / Abstract

### Pronunciation
IPA: /ɦoɡaɪɲi/

### Translations
- English: to arrive at, to reach (a state), to attain, to come into
- Polish: dotrzeć do, osiągnąć (stan), dojść do

### Example sentence
**To wo gavină xogăni.**
_I reach a good state / I arrive at wellness._

### Alternative Forms
Null

### Etymology
**[[xogă (verb)|xogă]]** (Arrive) + **[[ni (particle)|ni]]** (Towards/Into).
*Lit:* "Arrive-into."

### Synonyms
- **[[niŕa (verb)|niŕa]]** (To become / lead to - Stative/Passive).
- **[[xogă (verb)]]** (To arrive - General).

### Antonyms
Null

### Derived terms
- **xogănishá** (agent noun) *(potential)*

### Usage Note
While `xogă` is often physical ("Arrive at the city"), **xogăni** is preferred for abstract or emotional states ("Reach happiness", "Attain enlightenment").
