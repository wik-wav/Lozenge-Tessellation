### Noun class (warm / cold)

### Pronunciation

IPA: /ɲo/

### Semantic field

[[Smntc_Field Existence]]

### Translations

- English: thing, object, physical entity
- Polish: rzecz, przedmiot, obiekt

### Plural Form

**ńa** (Things).

### Example sentence

Frë ńo nèŕa.
Freedom isn't a thing.

### Alternative forms

As a suffix **-ńo**: Transforming a concept into a physical object.

### Etymology

**no-** (Locative prefix implying physical presence/vicinity) + **[[jo (pronoun)|jo]]** (It/Inanimate).
Logic: "The physical it."
