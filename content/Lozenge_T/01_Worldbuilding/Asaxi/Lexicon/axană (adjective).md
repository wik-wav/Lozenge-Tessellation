# axană ([[03_Asaxi Adjectives (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">axană</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Qualitative Adjective (`-nă`)
- **Source Agreement:** **Warm** (Root `axa` is Warm).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /aɦanaɪ/

### Semantic Field

[[Smntc_Field Emotion]] / Personality

### Translations

- English: approachable, friendly, cheerful, jolly, good-natured
- Polish: przystępny, pogodny, radosny

### Usage Note

Does **not** mean "Funny" (causing laughter). It means "Full of laughter" or "Prone to laughter." Describes someone easy to talk to.

### Example sentence

**To Dokùshá John axană shá xiŕa.**
_Doctor John is an approachable person._

### Etymology

**[[axa (noun)|axa]]** (Laughter) + **-nă**.

### Antonyms

- **hjină** (Introverted/Reserved).
- **exenýj** (Weepy/Tragic).
