### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Tense Modifier Prefix
- **Function:** **Immediate Proximity**
- **Meaning:** "Just now" (with Past) or "About to" (with Future).

### Syntax

Stacks on top of Tense prefixes.

- **Immediate Past:** `ozè-[Verb]`
- **Immediate Future:** `opa-[Verb]`

### Example sentence

**To wo ozètopù.** _I just dropped (it)._
