### Etymology
The **aura/simile** colour of [[vèno (noun)|vèno]] (wood): vèno + **-nă**. It is the **warm other-class equivalent** of [[cùnýj (adjective)|cùnýj]] — the same hue at the opposite emotional temperature, but built from a genuinely **warm** object so that the suffix obeys **Source Agreement** (warmth follows the source noun, never a flipped suffix). See [[16_Adjectives_Constitution vs Simile]].
