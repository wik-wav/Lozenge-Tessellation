### Etymology
The **aura/simile** adjective of [[ihjo (noun)|ihjo]] (bone): ihjo + **-nýj**. "Bone-like" -> bony, gaunt, skeletal. (This corrects the earlier mis-suffixed *ihjonă*: bone is **cold**, so Source Agreement requires the cold **-nýj**, not the warm -nă.)
