### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Temporal Connector
- **Function:** **Simultaneous Point**
- **Meaning:** "When".

### Syntax

Placed at the **end** of the time-setting clause.

- **Structure:** `[Event A] vå, [Event B].`

### Etymology

Derived from **[[va- (particle)|va]]** (Inside). _Logic:_ "Inside the time (of)..."

### Pronunciation

IPA: /vau̯/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">vå</span>
- **Pattern:** `L`
- **Class:** `atonal`
