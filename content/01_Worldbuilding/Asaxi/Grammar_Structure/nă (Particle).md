# nă ([[02_Particles in Asaxi]])

### Grammatical function

- **Particle type:** [[02_Particles in Asaxi|High Binding Particle]] (Prefix/Suffix)
- **Function:** **Cessation Command** / **Interruption**
- **Meaning:** "Stop!", "Cease!", "Don't (continue)!".

### Usage Notes

Used strictly to interrupt an ongoing action.
* **Suffix:** `Exenă!` (Stop crying).
* **Prefix:** `Năjýnů!` (Stop talking).

### Pronunciation

IPA: /naɪ/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">nă</span>
- **Pattern:** `L`
- **Class:** `atonal`

### Example sentence


#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** tom, exenă!
- **English:** Tom, stop crying!
- **Polish:** x

#### Example 2

- **Asaxi:** john, năshěsonů.
- **English:** John, stop reading.
- **Polish:** x

### Etymology

Shortened form of **[[ná (particle)|ná]]** (Not) or **[[nèŕa (particle)|nè]]** (No).
*Phonotactics:* The shift to the `ă` diphthong adds emphasis/sharpness.

### Synonyms

- **[[tomo' (verb)|tomohè]]** (Stop! - Full verb command).

### Antonyms

- **[[nihè (particle)|nihè]]** (Start! / Begin!).
