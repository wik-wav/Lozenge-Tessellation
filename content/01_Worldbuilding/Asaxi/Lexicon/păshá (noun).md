### Translations
- English: another (person), the other, someone else, a stranger, an outsider
- Polish: inny (człowiek), bliźni, obcy, ktoś inny

### Usage Note
Refers to an entity that is not the Self (`niwo`) or the immediate group (`wa`). It spatially locates the person "outside" the self-concept.

### Example sentence

#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** to păshá xogă.

- **Polish:** x

#### Example 2

- **Asaxi:** to wo ni păsháma gavină ů.
