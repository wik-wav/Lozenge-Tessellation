### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Superlative Prefix
- **Function:** **Minimum Degree** ("Least")
- **Constraint:** Used only when the Adjective's source noun is **Cold**.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /nɪjnə/

### Pitch Accent

- **Morae:** <span class="asaxi-text">ný · jnè</span>
- **Pattern:** `L · L`
- **Class:** `atonal`

### Etymology

**nýj-** (Cold Superlative) + **nè** (Fused Negative `ná`+`xi`). _Note:_ Uses `nè` to distinguish it phonetically from `nă-ná`.

### Example sentence

#### Example 1

- **Asaxi:** to [knife] nýjnègonýj [cutting]xů.
- **English:** The knife cuts the least hard (rock-like).

### Analysis

- **Adverb:** `gonýj` (rock-like/hard; cold source `go`)
- **Verbal mode:** `-x-` (interaction/force)
