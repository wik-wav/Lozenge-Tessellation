# nă- ([[29_Degree & Comparison]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">nă-</span>

<span class="asaxi-script-alpha">nă-</span>
### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Superlative Prefix
- **Function:** **Maximum Degree** ("Most")
- **Constraint:** Used only when the Adjective's source noun is **Warm**.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /naɪ/

### Etymology

Duplication of the warm adjectival suffix **-nă**.

### Example sentence

**To wo nădănă [eating]chů.** _I feel like I am eating the **most heavily** (gluttonously)._

- _Adverb:_ `dănă` (Elephant-like/Heavy - Warm Source `dă`)
- _Verbal Mode:_ `-ch-` (Subjective Feeling).
