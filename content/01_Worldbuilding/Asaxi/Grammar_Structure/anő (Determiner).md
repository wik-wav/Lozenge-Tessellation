# anő ([[05_Determiners in Asaxi]])

- - -

### Grammatical function

[[09_Asaxi Determiners (List)|Determiner]]. An [[02_Definiteness in Asaxi|indefinite]] noun marker for [[00_Noun Classification (Gender) in Asaxi|all nouns]].
- Pronoun
  That (indefinite)
- As an Indefinite Determiner
  When used to introduce a noun, anő is neutral regarding the Warm/Cold distinction.
    - Cold Noun: anő shèso (A book)
    - Warm Noun: anő shá (A person)
- Filler word: eg.
  Asa: [[xă (Particle)|xă]] anő...
  En: Yeah that...
