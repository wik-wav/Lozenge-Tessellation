# tá ([[03_Asaxi Root Words (List)]])

- - -

<span class="asaxi-script">tá</span>

### Grammatical function

- **Type:** Root Word / Component
- **Meaning:** "of or relating to friction", "clash", "opposition", "strike", "conflict".

### Usage Notes

Rarely used alone. Primarily found in compounds describing pain or conflict.

### Pronunciation

IPA: /t̪ɑ/

### Etymology

Sound symbolism for a hard impact. Root of **[[tètá (noun)|tètá]]** (Pain - "Repeated clash").

### Derived terms

- **[[cőtájýn (noun)]]** (Argument / Understanding-Clash).
